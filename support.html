Support

Email support@drivedrill.app and a human will reply.

I paid, but Premium is not unlocked

Open Settings › Restore Purchases in the app. Purchases are tied to the Apple Account that bought them, so make sure you are signed in to the same one. If it still does not unlock, email us with the date of purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › DriveDrill › Cancel. You can also tap Manage Subscription inside the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends, or it renews. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Can I buy once instead of subscribing?

Yes. The Pass Pack is a one-time $49.99 purchase that never renews, and it unlocks every state in the app, not just the one you are studying.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every question for every state is built into the app. Practice and mock exams work with no signal. Only the purchase screen needs a connection, because prices come from the App Store.

I think a question is wrong

Please tell us — email support@drivedrill.app with the state and the question text. Traffic law changes every legislative session, and we would rather fix it than have you study something stale. We keep a record of the source behind every state-specific fact in the app, so a correction is quick.

Is this the real test?

No. These are original questions written in the style of public state driver handbooks. The exam format is built to match your state's real test — its question count, its pass score, and any separately scored sections — but the questions themselves are ours. Your state's current official handbook is always the authoritative source.

Which states are included?

All 50 states and the District of Columbia, each with its own question bank and its own exam format.
