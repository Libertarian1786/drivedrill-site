Support

Email support@drivedrill.app and a human will reply.

I paid, but my purchase is not unlocked

Open Settings › Restore Purchases in the app. Purchases are tied to the Apple Account that bought them, so make sure you are signed in to the same one. If it still does not unlock, email us with the date of purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. On your iPhone or iPad: Settings › your name › Subscriptions, then tap the CDL Prep subscription and tap Cancel. You can also open Settings › Manage or cancel subscription inside the app: it opens a screen where you can cancel or ask Apple for a refund (Apple decides refunds). Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends, or it renews. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Can I buy once instead of subscribing?

Yes. CDL Full Access is $35.99, paid once, with no subscription. It never renews, and it unlocks everything in CDL Prep.

Pass Guarantee claim

Did not pass your knowledge test? Email support@drivedrill.app with these three things:

  • a photo of the failed result (your state's notice or score sheet);
  • a screenshot of your practice exam history in CDL Prep (Settings › Practice exam history);
  • the receipt email for each charge you claim.

You may cover your license number, date of birth and address on the result before you send it. We answer within 5 business days. What is covered and the conditions: Pass Guarantee.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every question, picture and flashcard is built into the app, and practice and practice exams work with no signal. Only the purchase screen needs a connection, because prices come from the App Store.

How do I switch the app to Spanish?

Open Settings › Language and pick Español. Every question, explanation and flashcard is in both languages. Some states give the CDL knowledge test only in English, so check with your state.

I think a question is wrong

Please tell us: email support@drivedrill.app with the test and the question text. We would rather fix it than have you study something wrong.

Is this the real test?

No. These are original practice questions written from the national CDL manual most states use. Each practice exam is scored at 80%, the federal pass mark; the number of questions on the real test varies by state. Your state's current CDL manual is always the authoritative source.

Which states are included?

All 50 states and the District of Columbia. Pick yours at setup.

I cannot find the app on my Home Screen

The name under its icon is CDL Prep.
