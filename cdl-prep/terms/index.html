What CDL Prep is

CDL Prep is an independent study aid for the commercial driver's license (CDL) knowledge tests. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any state agency or the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA). Practice questions are written by us from the national CDL manual most states use. They are not official test questions, and no question in this app will necessarily appear on your test.

No warranty

Using CDL Prep does not guarantee you will pass any test. Rules change; your state's current CDL manual is always the authoritative source, and you should study it alongside this app. We make no warranty that the content is complete, current or error-free for your situation.

Not legal or driving advice

Nothing in the app is legal advice or driving instruction. Always follow posted signs, signals, an officer's directions, and the law in the jurisdiction you are driving in.
