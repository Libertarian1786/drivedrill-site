CDL Prep is an iPhone and iPad app for your commercial driver's license knowledge tests. It has 1,689 practice questions for all 8 CDL knowledge tests and the pre-trip inspection, with a clear explanation on every question, in English or Spanish.

Every CDL test

Each test has its own practice exam, scored at 80%, the federal pass mark. Pick Class A, B or C, and the app starts you on the tests for that class.

  • General Knowledge638 questions. The test everyone takes.
  • Air Brakes161 questions.
  • Combination Vehicles141 questions.
  • Hazardous Materials (Hazmat)241 questions.
  • Tanker65 questions.
  • Doubles/Triples82 questions.
  • Passenger102 questions.
  • School Bus114 questions.
  • Pre-Trip Inspection145 quiz questions and a 125-step checklist for both versions of the test.

Learn it, then test it

  • Short lessons, test by test.
  • 188 animated road scenes. Watch the situation, then answer.
  • 24 teaching diagrams: the air brake gauge, the air system, the fifth wheel, the wheel brake, the walk-around route and 17 hazmat placards.
  • 220 flashcards, road signs included.
  • Redo the questions you miss and bookmark the hard ones.
  • Listen: questions, answers and explanations read aloud.

Stay on track

  • Set your test date and get a study plan with a "ready by" date.
  • A daily goal and a streak.
  • A results page after every practice exam.

En español
