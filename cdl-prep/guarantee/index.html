The CDL Prep Pass Guarantee

Pass your knowledge test or get your money back. Study with CDL Prep and pass at least 5 full practice exams. If you still don't pass your knowledge test, we refund what you paid for CDL Prep in the 45 days before it.

Covers every plan, including one-time purchases. Claim by email within 30 days of your result. Conditions apply, read them below.

Last updated: 21 November 2026

What it covers

Every paid CDL Prep purchase, for everyone, whether bought in the App Store or through CDL Prep's own online payment link where the app offers one: any subscription charge, whatever the weekly plan, or the one-time CDL Full Access. A free trial costs nothing, so there is nothing to refund for it.

What you get back

We refund the CDL Prep charges from the 45 days before your test. For a subscription, that is every charge made in those 45 days; for a one-time purchase, it is that purchase.

The conditions

All three must be true:

  1. You passed at least 5 full practice exams in CDL Prep (reached the exam's pass score) for the CDL knowledge test you took, and the last one was within 30 days before your test.
  2. You took your state's CDL knowledge test within 45 days after each purchase or renewal charge you claim.
  3. You claim by email within 30 days of the failed result.

How to claim

Email support@drivedrill.app with:

  • a photo of the failed result (your state's notice or score sheet);
  • a screenshot of your practice exam history in CDL Prep (Settings › Practice exam history);
  • the receipt email for each charge you claim (from Apple, or the payment email for an online purchase).

You may cover your license number, date of birth and address on the result before you send it.

One claim per person, and it is not transferable. We answer within 5 business days.

How the refund works

Online purchases are refunded by us, to the card that paid, once your claim meets the conditions. If a parent paid, either of you can claim.

App Store purchases are refunded by Apple, so there are two steps:

  1. When your claim meets the conditions, we confirm it by email and ask you to request the refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple usually answers within 48 hours.
  2. When Apple asks us about your request, we tell Apple your claim qualifies under this guarantee. Apple makes the final decision on App Store refunds.

Not covered

  • CDL skills tests (the pre-trip inspection, basic control and road tests).
  • Tests outside the United States.
  • Purchases made anywhere other than the App Store or CDL Prep's own online payment link.
  • Charges already refunded.

Changes to these terms

We may change these terms for future purchases. The terms in force on your purchase date are the ones that apply.
