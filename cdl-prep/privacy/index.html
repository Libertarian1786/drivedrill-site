Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Short version: apart from an email you choose to send, CDL Prep does not collect your name, email or any other personal information. There is no account and no sign-up, and we do not track you. The only things the app sends from your device are a record of your in-app purchases, a few study-plan details, study counts and device details, under an anonymous ID, so we can check purchases and see how the app and its plans are doing.

What the app stores, and where

Everything the app stores about you stays on your device:

  • The state, CDL class and tests you chose, and the app language.
  • Which questions you have answered, whether you got them right, and which you bookmarked.
  • Your practice exam history, lesson progress, daily goal and streak.
  • If you set them: your test date, and the time for practice reminders. Reminders are scheduled by your device itself; nothing is sent to us or to any server.
  • If you type it during setup: your first name. It is used only on your screens and in a progress report you choose to send.
  • If you set up a weekly progress report for a parent: their phone number or email address. It stays on your device and is used only by your own Messages or Mail app.

This lives in your device's local storage, and it is deleted when you delete the app. We cannot see it, apart from the few study-plan details and study counts the app also stores with RevenueCat (see Purchases).

Sharing

Some screens let you share a short message (for example that you passed a test section, with a link to CDL Prep in the App Store) or send a progress report picture by Messages or Mail. The progress report can show the first name you typed. These are made on your device and open the share sheet, Messages or Mail; you choose where they go, if anywhere. Nothing is sent to us.

iCloud sync (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Sync progress with iCloud in Settings, your progress is copied to your own private iCloud storage so it appears on your other devices. That data goes to Apple under your Apple Account and is governed by Apple's privacy policy. We have no access to it. You can turn the setting off at any time.

Purchases

Purchases made in the app are processed by Apple. We never see your name, payment details or Apple Account.

We use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) to check purchases and to count trials, subscriptions, renewals, cancellations and refunds. When you buy, restore, or a subscription renews, the app and Apple send RevenueCat a record of your purchases: which plan, when, the price and currency, and whether it is a free trial, active, cancelled or refunded. This record is kept under a random ID that RevenueCat creates for this installation of the app. It is not linked to your name, email, Apple Account or any other app, and we do not use it to track you or to advertise to you. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

With that record the app also sends RevenueCat the device's identifier for vendor (an ID Apple gives each app on a device, not shared with other companies' apps), the device model and iOS version, and, if you allow practice reminders, the device's push token. RevenueCat also sees the IP address of each request. These are used only to count installs and purchases and to keep the record working; they are not linked to you and are never used for tracking or advertising.

When you finish setup, the app also stores with RevenueCat, under the same random installation ID, a few study-plan details: how many days until your test (not the date itself), whether a test date is set, your answers to the readiness and daily-study-time questions, your age range, whether it is your first time taking the test, your answers to any other setup questions the app shows (for example who is paying for the app, and how you heard about it), your state, the app version and language, whether the app came from the App Store or a test build, and how many seconds each setup and offer screen was open. If you later change or clear your test date, the first two are updated. The first name you can type during setup stays on your device and is never sent.

On that same anonymous record the app also keeps a few counts, dates and yes/no answers, so we can see how the app and its plans are doing: what happened on the plans screen (seen, closed, the app left while it was open, a purchase started, cancelled, failed or completed), whether you opened the Pass Guarantee link, how many practice questions and practice exams you have done, your best practice exam score, how many practice exams you passed and the day of the last one, your knowledge-check score and weakest topic, how many flashcards you have seen, which parts of the app you use and how often, whether practice reminders are on, whether the app asked you for a rating, whether you sent a progress report, your answer if you tell the app whether you passed the real test, and a support code made from the anonymous ID. Never the questions or answers themselves, never your test date, and never anything typed. These are never linked to you and never used for advertising.

Managing your subscription: Settings › Manage or cancel subscription opens a screen provided by RevenueCat where you can cancel, restore purchases or ask Apple for a refund. If you answer its optional question about why you are cancelling, your answer is stored with RevenueCat on the same anonymous record. Refund requests go to Apple, which decides them.

Paying online (where offered): if the app offers a payment link, for example for a parent to pay, the checkout page is run by RevenueCat and the card payment is processed by Stripe. The person paying gives their email address and card details there. We see that email address and the purchase in RevenueCat, and use them only to send receipts and to handle cancellations and refunds. We never see the card number. The link carries the random ID of the learner's installation, so the purchase unlocks that copy of the app. Stripe's privacy policy is at stripe.com/privacy.

To show newer pictures and animations during setup, the app may download them from our website. That request carries no ID and no cookies, but, like any web request, our web host sees your IP address. We do not use it.

If you installed CDL Prep after tapping one of our ads in App Store search results, Apple provides an attribution token that tells us which search ad campaign and keyword led to the install. It contains no personal information, it is sent once per installation, and it does not require, and the app does not show, an App Tracking Transparency prompt.

What we do not do

  • Apart from an email you choose to send, we do not collect names, email addresses or phone numbers.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs. The only third-party SDK in the app is RevenueCat, which counts purchases under an anonymous ID (see Purchases). It gives us no way to identify you, and it is not used for advertising.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites, and the app shows no App Tracking Transparency prompt because there is nothing to track.
  • We do not sell or share data. Purchase records stay with RevenueCat, which processes them for us.

Children

CDL Prep is made for people preparing for a commercial driver's license. Apart from an email you choose to send, we collect no personal information from anyone, children included. What the app sends from the device is the anonymous record described under Purchases: purchases, a few study-plan details, study counts and device details, never linked to a person.

Your rights

Apart from an email you choose to send, we hold no personal data that identifies you. The anonymous record is tied only to this installation, and nothing in it identifies you, so we cannot find it from an email. Deleting the app ends it: a new installation starts a new, empty record. To remove everything else the app stores, delete the app, or use Reset CDL progress in Settings. Questions: support@drivedrill.app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@drivedrill.app
