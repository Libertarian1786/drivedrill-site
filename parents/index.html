Questions parents ask

Does it work on my teen's phone if I pay on mine?

Yes. With the text link or on the web, you can pay on your own phone or computer: the purchase unlocks on your teen's iPhone (for a web purchase, once the unlock link is opened there). With Ask to Buy, you approve the request and the purchase goes to your teen's Apple Account.

Can I cancel?

One-time purchases have nothing to cancel. To cancel a weekly plan, cancel a web one from the link in its emails, or an App Store one in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

What if my teen doesn't pass?

DriveDrill comes with the Pass Guarantee: if your teen doesn't pass the knowledge test, you get your money back. Conditions apply: see the Pass Guarantee. If you paid, either of you can claim.

Does my teen need an account?

No. There is no account to create and no sign-up in the app.
