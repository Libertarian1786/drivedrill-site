Questions parents ask

Does it work on my teen's phone if I pay on mine?

Yes. With Ask to Buy, you approve the request on your own iPhone and the purchase goes to your teen's Apple Account.

Can I cancel?

One-time purchases have nothing to cancel. To cancel a weekly plan, go to Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on the iPhone that bought it.

What if my teen doesn't pass?

DriveDrill comes with the Pass Guarantee: if your teen doesn't pass the knowledge test, you get your money back. Conditions apply: see the Pass Guarantee.

Does my teen need an account?

No. There is no account to create and no sign-up in the app.
