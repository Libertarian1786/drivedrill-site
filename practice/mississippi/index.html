Q1. In Mississippi, the maximum speed limit in a school zone is:

Mississippi's manual sets the maximum speed for any vehicle in a school zone at 15 mph.

Q2. At a railroad crossing, the gate goes up, but the red lights are still flashing. What should you do?

Do not cross the tracks until the red lights stop flashing, even if the gate rises. Another train may be coming.

Q3. In Mississippi, how early may you renew your driver's license?

A Mississippi license may be renewed up to six months before its expiration date.

Q4. If you double your speed, about how much longer is your braking distance?

Braking distance grows much faster than speed. Doubling your speed makes the braking distance, and the force of a crash, about four times greater.

Q5. Implied consent means that by driving in the state you have agreed to:

Refusing the test carries its own suspension, usually longer than the one for failing it, and the refusal can be used against you.

Q6. Your car is stuck in snow. What is the best way to try to free it?

Rock the car slowly forward and back without spinning the tires. Spinning the wheels only digs the car in deeper.

Q7. In Mississippi, which of these brings a mandatory one-year revocation of your license?

Mandatory one-year revocations in Mississippi include vehicular manslaughter, a felony involving a vehicle, failing to give aid after a crash, and three reckless driving convictions within 12 months.

Q8. A curb painted with diagonal yellow lines next to a wide striped area means:

Striped buffer areas separate traffic from hazards, medians or opposing lanes. Driving or parking in them is prohibited.

Q9. You may not pass another vehicle within a certain distance of the crest of a hill because:

A blind crest hides an oncoming vehicle until it is far too late to abort the pass.

Q10. Black ice is most likely to form:

Bridges and shaded stretches lose heat fastest. If the road looks wet but spray has stopped coming off other cars, suspect ice.

Q11. If you move to Mississippi, how long do you have to get a Mississippi driver's license?

New residents must get a Mississippi driver's license within 60 days and must surrender all out-of-state licenses.

Q12. In Mississippi, what is the minimum speed limit for a passenger vehicle on an interstate highway?

Mississippi's speed limit table sets the interstate minimum at 40 mph.

Q13. In Mississippi, the minimum age to apply for a learner permit is:

Applicants under 18 also need parental consent, and the permit must be held for a set period before the road test.

Q14. When leaving a freeway you should:

Keep your speed until you are on the deceleration ramp, then slow. Braking in the travel lane surprises the traffic behind you.

Q15. A three-point turn should be used only:

The maneuver blocks the whole road for several seconds, so it needs a low-traffic street and a clear view both ways.

Q16. Your lane has a painted arrow that shows both a curved arrow and a straight arrow. What may you do from this lane?

A lane marked with both a curved and a straight arrow is a shared lane: you may turn in the direction the curved arrow points or continue straight.

Q17. In Mississippi, a crash must be reported to law enforcement if it involves injury, death, or apparent property damage exceeding:

Mississippi requires drivers to report a crash to law enforcement if it involves injury, death, or apparent property damage exceeding $500.00.

Q18. In Mississippi, if you refuse to take a breath or chemical test, your license is administratively suspended for:

Mississippi law administratively suspends the license of a driver who refuses a breath or chemical test for ninety days.

Q19. In Mississippi, you may not park within how many feet of the approach to a traffic signal or stop sign?

Mississippi bans parking within 30 feet of the approach to any traffic control device, such as a signal or stop sign.

Q20. Parking is prohibited:

Never park in a crosswalk, on a sidewalk, in an intersection, or where you would block a curb ramp.

Q21. On a divided highway or a one-way street, a solid yellow line runs along the left edge of your lanes. What does it mark?

On divided highways and one-way roads, a solid yellow line marks the left edge of the road, and a solid white line marks the right edge. Keep to the right of the yellow line.

Q22. A motorcycle ahead of you has its turn signal blinking. What should you keep in mind?

Motorcycle turn signals usually do not cancel themselves, and riders sometimes forget to turn them off. Make sure the rider is really turning before you pull out.

Q23. In Mississippi, your foot brake must be strong enough to stop the vehicle from 20 mph within:

The foot brake must stop the vehicle within 30 feet at 20 mph. The parking brake must stop it within a longer distance at the same speed.

Q24. The road is most slippery:

Oil and rubber build up on dry pavement and float to the surface when rain starts. Slow down and double your following distance.

Q25. You notice one headlight is out at night. You should:

From a distance one light reads as a motorcycle, and oncoming drivers misjudge how much room you take. Fix it before driving at night again.
