Q1. The pedestrian signal shows a flashing orange hand with a countdown while you wait to turn. It means:

The countdown is for people already crossing. Wait until the crosswalk is clear before completing your turn.

Q2. A red warning light appears on the dashboard while you are driving. You should:

Red generally means stop now: oil pressure, temperature, brakes or charging. Amber usually means service soon.

Q3. Driving into low sun glare, the best response is to:

Glare hides brake lights and pedestrians. Slow down, clean the inside of your windshield, and leave more room.

Q4. In Mississippi, the minimum liability insurance coverage required for injury to a single person in a single-vehicle accident is:

Mississippi's minimum insurance amounts include $25,000 for a single person in a single vehicle accident.

Q5. In Mississippi, a driver under 21 is over the limit with:

The under-21 threshold is far below the 0.08% adult limit, so even a small amount of alcohol can reach it.

Q6. Driving while your license is suspended usually results in:

It is one of the few traffic offenses that regularly carries jail, and it extends the original suspension.

Q7. In Mississippi, a crash must be reported to law enforcement if it involves injury, death, or apparent property damage exceeding:

Mississippi requires drivers to report a crash to law enforcement if it involves injury, death, or apparent property damage exceeding $500.00.

Q8. Before opening your door into traffic after parking, you should:

Dooring a cyclist is a common and serious crash. Check the mirror and over your shoulder, and open with the far hand so your body turns toward traffic.

Q9. In Mississippi, when a gate is down, a signal is flashing or a train is approaching, you must stop:

You must stop even if you do not see a train, and stay stopped until it is safe to cross.

Q10. A space cushion around your vehicle is useful because:

Space is time. The more of it you keep on all four sides, the more options you have when something goes wrong.
