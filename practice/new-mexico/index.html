Q1. In New Mexico, an applicant under 18 must log how much supervised driving before the road test?

A parent or guardian certifies the hours. Night driving is where new drivers crash most, which is why a share of the total has to be after dark.

Q2. In New Mexico, a sign reading HOV 3 on a reserved lane means:

New Mexico's HOV lanes are reserved for vehicles with more than one person; a sign such as HOV 3 means there must be at least three people in the vehicle.

Q3. Driving after using marijuana is:

Legal to possess does not mean legal to drive on. Impaired driving laws cover cannabis the same way they cover alcohol.

Q4. The basic speed law means:

The posted limit is the maximum for perfect conditions. In rain, fog, traffic or at night, the legal safe speed is lower.

Q5. In New Mexico, when passing a bicyclist, a motorist should try to keep a clearance of at least:

New Mexico's manual tells motorists to keep a safe distance when passing a bicyclist, trying for at least five feet.

Q6. In New Mexico, a driver under 21 is over the limit with:

The under-21 threshold is far below the 0.08% adult limit, so even a small amount of alcohol can reach it.

Q7. Excessive speeding, well above the limit, is treated more seriously because:

Kinetic energy rises with the square of speed, so a small increase in speed is a large increase in damage and stopping distance.

Q8. A U-turn should never be made:

You need to be visible for the whole maneuver. Blind curves and crests hide you from traffic that arrives at speed.

Q9. A bicycle on a public road is:

Riders must obey signals and signs, and drivers must yield to them the same way they would to another vehicle.

Q10. A flashing yellow traffic light means:

Flashing yellow means proceed with care. You do not have to stop, but you must slow down and watch for cross traffic and pedestrians.

Q11. A wide white line across your lane at an intersection is:

Stop at the line, not past it. If your view is blocked once stopped, edge forward slowly only after stopping at the line.

Q12. When leaving a freeway you should:

Keep your speed until you are on the deceleration ramp, then slow. Braking in the travel lane surprises the traffic behind you.

Q13. A left turn on red from a one-way street onto another one-way street is:

Most states permit this turn after a complete stop and yielding, but it is banned in some cities, so obey any posted sign.

Q14. A crash with the vehicle ahead is unavoidable. The safest choice is usually to:

Reduce speed as much as possible and choose a glancing impact with something soft over a head-on with something solid.

Q15. In New Mexico, a person supervising an instructional permit holder's practice driving must be:

New Mexico requires that a permit holder be supervised by another driver who is at least 21 years of age and licensed to drive that type of vehicle.

Q16. The first ability alcohol affects in a driver is:

Judgment goes first, which is why an impaired driver often believes they are fine. Reaction time, vision and coordination follow.

Q17. In New Mexico, the minimum age to apply for a learner permit is:

Applicants under 18 also need parental consent, and the permit must be held for a set period before the road test.

Q18. Black ice is most likely to form:

Bridges and shaded stretches lose heat fastest. If the road looks wet but spray has stopped coming off other cars, suspect ice.

Q19. The best response to feeling drowsy while driving is to:

Drowsy driving impairs you much like alcohol, and micro-sleeps happen without warning. Only sleep fixes it.

Q20. In New Mexico, if a driver accumulates twelve or more points within a twelve-month period, their license will be:

New Mexico suspends a driver's license for one year if the driver has twelve or more points within a twelve-month period.

Q21. New Mexico's no-parking rules keep a stopped vehicle how far back from a railroad crossing?

New Mexico prohibits parking within 50 feet of a railroad crossing.

Q22. Where a state uses a point system, it is used to:

Points accumulate with each conviction and are removed over time. Reaching the threshold brings a suspension. A few states, such as Minnesota, act on specific violations instead of points.

Q23. In New Mexico, when merging with other traffic, a driver needs a gap of about:

New Mexico's manual states that merging with other traffic requires a gap of about four seconds.

Q24. In New Mexico, drivers may not park within how many feet of a fire hydrant?

New Mexico prohibits parking within 15 feet of a fire hydrant.

Q25. Your brakes fail while driving. You should:

Pump to build pressure, downshift for engine braking, apply the parking brake gradually, and look for an open escape path or an uphill slope.
