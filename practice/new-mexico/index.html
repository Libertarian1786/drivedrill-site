Q1. A steady red arrow means:

A red arrow prohibits that movement entirely. Wait for a green or flashing yellow arrow, even if no traffic is coming.

Q2. Hydroplaning happens when:

Hydroplaning starts with speed, water depth and worn tread. Ease off the accelerator, keep the wheel straight, and do not brake hard.

Q3. After a school bus turns off its red lights, you should still:

Children are unpredictable right after they get off a bus. Move off slowly and keep scanning both sides.

Q4. Before backing out of a parking space you should:

Cameras miss low objects and fast-moving children. Look behind the vehicle before you get in, then back slowly while checking all around.

Q5. In New Mexico, drivers may not park within how many feet of a fire hydrant?

New Mexico prohibits parking within 15 feet of a fire hydrant.

Q6. In New Mexico, a person supervising an instructional permit holder's practice driving must be:

New Mexico requires that a permit holder be supervised by another driver who is at least 21 years of age and licensed to drive that type of vehicle.

Q7. You arrive at an intersection where the traffic signal is completely dark because of a power failure. You should:

A dark signal is treated as an all-way stop. Stop, then take turns in the order vehicles arrived.

Q8. At night, pedestrians are hardest to see:

Most pedestrian deaths happen after dark. Lower your speed so your stopping distance fits inside your headlight range.

Q9. In New Mexico, an applicant under 18 must log how much supervised driving before the road test?

A parent or guardian certifies the hours. Night driving is where new drivers crash most, which is why a share of the total has to be after dark.

Q10. New Mexico's no-parking rules keep a stopped vehicle how far back from a railroad crossing?

New Mexico prohibits parking within 50 feet of a railroad crossing.
