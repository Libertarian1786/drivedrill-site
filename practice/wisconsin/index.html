Q1. In Wisconsin, the vision screening requires a field of vision from the center of each eye of at least:

Wisconsin requires a 70-degree field of vision from the center of each eye to pass the vision screening.

Q2. A pedestrian steps into an unmarked crosswalk at an intersection. You must:

Every intersection has a crosswalk whether or not it is painted. Yield to people crossing there.

Q3. On a long downgrade you should:

Continuous braking overheats the brakes and they fade. Let the engine hold the vehicle back and brake in short firm applications.

Q4. A standard drink of beer, wine and distilled spirits:

A 12 oz beer, a 5 oz glass of wine and 1.5 oz of spirits each contain roughly the same alcohol, which is why counting drinks does not make you safe to drive.

Q5. In Wisconsin, instruction permit and probationary license holders may use a cell phone while driving only to:

Wisconsin limits instruction permit and probationary license holders to using a cell phone while driving only to report an emergency.

Q6. In Wisconsin, a newly licensed driver under 18 generally may not drive between:

Documented exceptions usually cover work, school activities, emergencies and driving with a licensed adult. Carry the paperwork if you rely on one.

Q7. A white rectangular sign with black letters or numbers is:

White rectangles are regulatory: speed limits, turn restrictions, lane use, parking rules. Ignoring one is a traffic violation.

Q8. Before driving on a frosty morning you should:

Driving with a peephole is a citation in most states, and snow left on the roof slides onto your windshield or the car behind you.

Q9. In Wisconsin, a learner permit must be held for at least how long before the road test?

The holding period exists so new drivers practise across seasons and conditions rather than cramming a licence in a weekend.

Q10. Another driver is passing you. You should:

Speeding up while being passed strands the other driver in the oncoming lane. Hold your line and let them complete the pass.
