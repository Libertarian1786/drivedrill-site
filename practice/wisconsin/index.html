Q1. In Wisconsin, an applicant under 18 must log how much supervised driving before the road test?

A parent or guardian certifies the hours. Night driving is where new drivers crash most, which is why a share of the total has to be after dark.

Q2. In Wisconsin, during the restricted period, a young driver may carry:

Crash risk for a teen driver climbs sharply with each additional teen passenger, which is what this rule is built around.

Q3. A flashing yellow traffic light means:

Flashing yellow means proceed with care. You do not have to stop, but you must slow down and watch for cross traffic and pedestrians.

Q4. You are in the left lane of a highway and a line of traffic builds up behind you. You should:

The left lane is for passing. Once traffic stacks up behind you, moving right is both safer and, in many states, required.

Q5. In Wisconsin, the minimum age to apply for a learner permit is:

Applicants under 18 also need parental consent, and the permit must be held for a set period before the road test.

Q6. Before backing out of a parking space you should:

Cameras miss low objects and fast-moving children. Look behind the vehicle before you get in, then back slowly while checking all around.

Q7. In Wisconsin, besides the 50-question knowledge test, applicants must also pass a separate Highway Signs Test. How many questions does it have, and how many must you answer correctly?

Wisconsin requires a separate Highway Signs Test of 15 questions, and you must answer 12 of them (80%) correctly to pass.

Q8. In Wisconsin, you become unable to drive if you accumulate how many demerit points within any 12-month period?

Wisconsin suspends or revokes your driving privilege once you reach 12 or more demerit points within any 12-month period.

Q9. In Wisconsin, a driver under 21 is over the limit with:

This is a true zero tolerance state: any measurable alcohol is enough for a suspension, and mouthwash or cough syrup can reach it.

Q10. In Wisconsin, a new resident who holds a regular (non-commercial) out-of-state license should apply for a Wisconsin license within:

New Wisconsin residents transferring a regular out-of-state driver license should apply for a Wisconsin license within 60 days of moving.

Q11. A U-turn should never be made:

You need to be visible for the whole maneuver. Blind curves and crests hide you from traffic that arrives at speed.

Q12. Grooves cut into the shoulder that make a loud noise when you drive over them are there to:

Rumble strips are a drowsiness and drift warning. If you hit one, ease back into the lane smoothly and consider stopping to rest.

Q13. In Wisconsin, the move-over law requires you to change lanes, or slow down, when passing:

Move to a lane that is not next to the stopped vehicle when you safely can. When you cannot, slow down. People working at the roadside are struck every year by drivers who did neither.

Q14. The recommended hand position on the steering wheel is:

States teach 9 and 3, 8 and 4, or lower still. Each gives good control and keeps your arms out of the airbag's path; 10 and 2 puts them where it deploys.

Q15. An open container of alcohol in the passenger area of the vehicle is:

Open container laws apply to the whole passenger compartment. Keep sealed alcohol in the trunk or a locked area.

Q16. In Wisconsin, using a hand-held cell phone while driving is:

There is no statewide hands-free requirement on ordinary roads, but hand-held use in a marked work zone is prohibited and texting is banned for all drivers.

Q17. A red warning light appears on the dashboard while you are driving. You should:

Red generally means stop now: oil pressure, temperature, brakes or charging. Amber usually means service soon.

Q18. You come up behind slow-moving farm equipment displaying an orange triangle. You should:

The orange triangle marks a vehicle traveling under 25 mph. Closing speed is higher than it looks, so slow early and pass only with a long clear view.

Q19. In Wisconsin, the vision screening requires a field of vision from the center of each eye of at least:

Wisconsin requires a 70-degree field of vision from the center of each eye to pass the vision screening.

Q20. Unless a sign prohibits it, a right turn on red is allowed:

Right on red requires a full stop first, then yielding to everyone already entitled to go. A NO TURN ON RED sign removes the option entirely.

Q21. Driving into low sun glare, the best response is to:

Glare hides brake lights and pedestrians. Slow down, clean the inside of your windshield, and leave more room.

Q22. The basic speed law means:

The posted limit is the maximum for perfect conditions. In rain, fog, traffic or at night, the legal safe speed is lower.

Q23. In heavy fog you should drive with:

High beams reflect off fog and blind you. Use low beams or fog lights, slow down, and pull well off the road if you cannot see.

Q24. Blood alcohol concentration depends on:

Two people drinking the same amount can end up at very different levels, so nobody can judge their own BAC reliably.

Q25. When towing a trailer you should:

Extra weight lengthens every distance. Many states also set lower speed limits for vehicles that are towing.
