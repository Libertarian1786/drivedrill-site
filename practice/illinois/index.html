Q1. In Illinois, a crash involving a school bus must be reported when it causes:

A report must be filed if a crash involves a school bus and results in any property damage or personal injury.

Q2. Under Illinois' zero tolerance law, a driver under 21 loses their driving privileges for:

Illinois is a true zero tolerance state. A first offense suspends driving privileges for three months, and refusing the test brings six.

Q3. How should you wear the shoulder part of a lap and shoulder belt?

Wear the lap belt low across your hips and the shoulder belt across your chest. Never put the shoulder belt under your arm or behind your back; in a crash it cannot hold you there and can cause injuries.

Q4. On an Illinois divided highway, who may use the openings in the median to turn around?

Median openings on divided highways are only for authorized vehicles, such as police and emergency vehicles.

Q5. On a road with no sidewalk, where should people walk?

Where there is no sidewalk, pedestrians should walk on the left side or shoulder, facing traffic, so they can see what is coming. Drivers should expect people walking there, especially at night.

Q6. In Illinois, which of these leads to a license suspension?

A second conviction for failing to obey a railroad-crossing signal is a reason for suspension.

Q7. Illinois's minimum liability insurance is:

Illinois requires at least $25,000 for injury or death of one person, $50,000 for two or more, and $20,000 for damage to another person's property.

Q8. You sell your car in Illinois. What happens to the license plates?

In Illinois, license plates remain with the owner, not the vehicle.

Q9. In Illinois, a driver 21 or older is legally impaired at a blood alcohol level of:

You can still be convicted below 0.08% if other evidence shows impairment.

Q10. When parallel parking on an Illinois two-way street, your right wheels must be within how far of the curb?

Park so the right-hand wheels are parallel to and within 12 inches of the curb.

Q11. On an Illinois street with a two-way left-turn lane in the center, where must you make a left turn from?

When a two-way left-turn lane is provided, a left turn may not be made from any other lane.

Q12. Driving while your license is suspended usually results in:

It is one of the few traffic offenses that regularly carries jail, and it extends the original suspension.

Q13. You are waiting to turn left, and an oncoming car has its right turn signal on. When should you start your turn in front of it?

A driver may have left a signal on by mistake or may change their mind. Do not assume the car will turn; wait until it actually starts its turn before you turn left.

Q14. Your vehicle has four-wheel drive. How should you drive on an icy road?

Four-wheel drive does not make ice safe, because the vehicle can still skid and slide, so drive slowly and carefully. It can give a false sense of security.

Q15. Before backing out of a parking space you should:

Mirrors and cameras miss low objects and small children. Check behind the vehicle before you get in, then turn and look through the rear window as you back slowly. Use the mirrors and camera only as extra help.

Q16. What is a reversible lane?

Reversible lanes carry traffic one way at some times and the other way at other times, usually to handle rush-hour traffic. Check the signs and lane-use signals before you enter one.

Q17. A bicyclist is riding toward an intersection and has not signaled. What should you assume?

At intersections, assume a bicyclist is going straight unless they signal otherwise. Do not turn across their path.

Q18. A pedestrian hybrid beacon over a mid-block crosswalk shows two steady red lights. What must you do?

A pedestrian hybrid beacon is dark until someone pushes the crossing button. Steady red means stop and stay stopped while pedestrians cross; only when it changes to flashing red may you go once your path is clear.

Q19. You come up behind slow-moving farm equipment displaying an orange triangle. You should:

The orange triangle marks a vehicle traveling under 25 mph. Closing speed is higher than it looks, so slow early and pass only with a long clear view.

Q20. Good drivers look:

Looking far ahead gives you time to spot brake lights, debris and merging traffic while you still have choices.

Q21. Compared with daylight, how does darkness change when you can see an object on the road?

At night you must be closer to an object than in daylight before you can see it. Slow down so you still have time to react when you do see it.

Q22. You are driving inside a roundabout that has two lanes. What should you avoid doing?

Choose your lane before you enter a multi-lane roundabout and stay in it until you exit. Do not change lanes inside the roundabout.

Q23. You hit a parked car and cannot find its owner. What must you do?

If you damage an unattended vehicle, you must find the owner or leave a written note where it will be seen, with your name and how to reach you. Some states also require a police report.

Q24. In Illinois, a driver refuses chemical testing after a crash that caused serious injury or death. Driving privileges are:

Refusing testing after a crash that caused serious injury or death brings a revocation of at least one year.

Q25. The only thing that reduces blood alcohol concentration is:

The liver processes alcohol at a fixed rate. Nothing speeds it up, so the only plan that works is not driving.
