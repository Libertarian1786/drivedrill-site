Q1. When passing on an Illinois two-lane road, you must be back in your own lane before an oncoming vehicle is within:

If you cannot finish the pass with that margin, do not start it.

Q2. Overdriving your headlights means:

High beams let you see about 350 feet ahead, and low beams much less. At highway speed you can outrun that, which is why night speeds should be lower.

Q3. Illinois requires you to notify the Secretary of State of an address change within:

You can do it at a DMV office, online at ilsos.gov, or by mail.

Q4. The minimum age for an Illinois instruction permit is:

Applicants aged 15 to 17 must be enrolled in an approved driver education course and hold the permit for at least nine months.

Q5. In Illinois you may not turn around on a curve or hill unless you can see in all directions for at least:

Towns may also ban U-turns outright at some locations.

Q6. If an Illinois chemical test shows a BAC of .08% or more, your driving privileges are suspended for:

This statutory summary suspension is administrative - it starts without a DUI conviction.

Q7. An Illinois driver aged 15 to 17 must hold an instruction permit for at least:

The permit itself is valid for 24 months.

Q8. Driver licensing in Illinois is handled by:

The Illinois Secretary of State issues driver's licenses and permits. The 2026 Rules of the Road calls its offices DMV facilities, and many services can also be done online at ilsos.gov.

Q9. A standard drink of beer, wine and distilled spirits:

A 12 oz beer, a 5 oz glass of wine and 1.5 oz of spirits each contain roughly the same alcohol, which is why counting drinks does not make you safe to drive.

Q10. Driving without the insurance your state requires can result in:

Financial responsibility is a condition of driving. The penalties apply whether or not you have a crash.

Q11. The Illinois school zone speed limit, on school days between 6:30 a.m. and 4 p.m. when children are present and signs are posted, is:

Outside those hours, or with no children present, the ordinary posted limit applies.

Q12. A strong crosswind is pushing your vehicle. You should:

Wind gusts are strongest in open country and beside large vehicles. Slow down, hold the wheel firmly, and expect a push when a truck passes.

Q13. In the first 12 months of an Illinois initial license, a driver under 18 may carry:

The restriction lasts 12 months or until the driver turns 18, whichever comes first. If the driver commits a moving violation, a seat belt violation or a violation of the graduated license law itself (such as carrying too many passengers) in that first year and is convicted of it, the restriction stays in place until the driver goes 6 months in a row without another such violation and conviction.

Q14. A space cushion around your vehicle is useful because:

Space is time. The more of it you keep on all four sides, the more options you have when something goes wrong.

Q15. A flagger in a work zone holds up a sign reading SLOW. You should:

Flaggers have the same authority as signs and signals in a work zone. Follow their direction and watch for workers on foot.

Q16. Blood alcohol concentration depends on:

Two people drinking the same amount can end up at very different levels, so nobody can judge their own BAC reliably.

Q17. Liability insurance pays for:

Liability is the coverage states require. Collision and comprehensive, which cover your own vehicle, are optional.

Q18. After passing another vehicle, you should return to your lane when:

Seeing both headlights of the vehicle you passed in your mirror means you have enough room to move back over without cutting the driver off.

Q19. Brown signs on a highway point to:

Brown is the color for recreational and cultural interest areas: parks, campgrounds, ski areas, museums and historic sites, for example. Blue, by contrast, marks motorist services.

Q20. Scott's Law, Illinois' move over law, requires you to:

Illinois penalties for violating Scott's Law are severe, including large fines and license suspension when a violation causes injury.

Q21. Your vehicle registration must be:

Expired registration is an easy stop for an officer and, in many states, grounds for towing.

Q22. A steady red arrow means:

A red arrow prohibits that movement entirely. Wait for a green or flashing yellow arrow, even if no traffic is coming.

Q23. The only thing that reduces blood alcohol concentration is:

The liver processes alcohol at a fixed rate. Nothing speeds it up, so the only plan that works is not driving.

Q24. Your hood flies open and blocks your view. You should:

Use the gap under the hood or the side window, signal, slow gradually and stop off the road.

Q25. A driver is following you too closely. The safest response is to:

Increase your own space cushion and give them a chance to go by. Brake-checking turns their mistake into your crash.
