Q1. You miss your exit while driving in a roundabout. What should you do?

If you miss your exit, stay in the roundabout and go around again until you return to it. Never stop, back up or cut across the island.

Q2. The road is most slippery:

Oil and rubber build up on dry pavement and float to the surface when rain starts. Slow down and double your following distance.

Q3. In Delaware's problem driver program, what happens when a driver reaches 14 calculated points?

At 14 points the license is suspended for 4 months, and the driver must also finish a behavior modification driving course to be reinstated.

Q4. In Delaware, a vehicle with green revolving or flashing lights is most likely:

Delaware lets snow removal equipment use green revolving or flashing lights. Give these vehicles plenty of room.

Q5. When leaving a freeway you should:

Keep your speed until you are on the deceleration ramp, then slow. Braking in the travel lane surprises the traffic behind you.

Q6. In Delaware, a driver under 21 who is convicted of DUI may lose the license until:

Delaware can revoke a minor's license until the minor is old enough to drink legally.

Q7. How much regular beer counts as one standard drink?

One standard drink is a 12-ounce beer, which has about as much alcohol as a shot of liquor or a glass of wine. A larger can or glass of beer counts as more than one drink.

Q8. In Delaware, the minimum age to apply for a learner permit is:

Applicants under 18 also need a parent's or sponsor's signature.

Q9. In Delaware, bicycle riders and passengers must wear a helmet if they are under the age of:

Delaware requires a properly fitted and fastened bicycle helmet for every rider and passenger under 18.

Q10. The diagonal stripes on a road-work barricade slope down toward the right. Which way should you go?

Barricade stripes slope down toward the side traffic should pass on. Stripes that slope down to the right mean bear to the right.

Q11. How should you help a crash victim who may be going into shock?

To help someone in shock, have them lie flat, raise their legs slightly if they are not hurt there, and keep them warm. Do not give them anything to drink.

Q12. You come to a flooded road, and you know a different way to get where you are going. What should you do?

Turn around and take another route when a road ahead is flooded. You cannot tell how deep the water is or whether the road is still there under it, and a vehicle can be swept away.

Q13. When it rains hard enough that your windshield wipers are on continuously, you should also:

Wipers on, lights on: it helps other drivers see you as much as it helps your own vision, and the law often requires it.

Q14. You have just driven through deep water, and your brakes feel weak. How can you dry them?

After driving through water, test your brakes. If they are wet, drive slowly in a low gear and press the brake pedal lightly until they work normally again.

Q15. In Delaware, besides a BAC of .08 or more, what is conclusive evidence that a driver is under the influence?

In Delaware, a BAC of .08 or more or the presence of any illicit or recreational drug proves a driver was under the influence. A driver can also be charged with a lower BAC.

Q16. Driving at night, you see red reflectors on the lane lines shining back at you in your headlights. What does this mean?

Road reflectors are made to shine red only toward drivers going the wrong way. If they shine red in your headlights, get out of the way and turn around as soon as it is safe.

Q17. A very heavy downpour makes it too hard to see the road even with your wipers on. What should you do?

Get off the road to a safe spot and wait until the downpour eases, because you cannot drive safely when you cannot see. Pressing on blind puts you and other drivers at risk.

Q18. In Delaware, what does a curb painted yellow mean?

Do not stop or park where the curb is painted yellow or a yellow line is painted at the edge of the road.

Q19. In which situation should you leave extra space behind a motorcycle?

Metal surfaces such as bridge gratings and railroad tracks, and gravel, give a motorcycle poor traction. Follow farther back so you have room if the rider has to slow or swerve.

Q20. In Delaware, bicyclists have the right to use:

Bicyclists may ride on every Delaware road except limited access highways such as interstates, so watch for them on other roads.

Q21. In Delaware, a person under 21 caught with alcohol for the first time is fined:

A first underage possession or drinking offense is a civil offense with a $100 fine. Later offenses cost $200 to $500.

Q22. You are waiting to turn left, and an oncoming car has its right turn signal on. When should you start your turn in front of it?

A driver may have left a signal on by mistake or may change their mind. Do not assume the car will turn; wait until it actually starts its turn before you turn left.

Q23. At a railroad crossing, the gate goes up, but the red lights are still flashing. What should you do?

Do not cross the tracks until the red lights stop flashing, even if the gate rises. Another train may be coming.

Q24. Seat belts work best because they:

Belts keep you behind the wheel and away from the windshield, and they position you correctly for the airbag.

Q25. In Delaware, what does finishing an approved defensive driving course do?

The credit is used only against future violations for DMV action. It does not remove or reduce points you already have.
