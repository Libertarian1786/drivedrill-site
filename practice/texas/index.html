Q1. A green arrow pointing left means:

A green arrow is a protected turn. Still check the crosswalk for people before you move.

Q2. When you park facing downhill on a road with no curb, turn your wheels:

With no curb to catch the tire, point the wheels so a rolling vehicle goes off the road rather than into traffic.

Q3. In Texas you must have your headlights on:

The half-hour margins cover dusk and dawn, when drivers most often forget. Poor visibility in daytime triggers the same requirement.

Q4. A standard drink of beer, wine and distilled spirits:

A 12 oz beer, a 5 oz glass of wine and 1.5 oz of spirits each contain roughly the same alcohol, which is why counting drinks does not make you safe to drive.

Q5. After a crash in Texas you must notify law enforcement immediately if:

Stop, help the injured, exchange information, and stay until you have met those duties. Leaving the scene is a criminal offense.

Q6. You should dim your high beams when:

High beams reflect off mirrors and blind the driver ahead as well as the one coming toward you. Dim for both.

Q7. In Texas, having an open container of alcohol in the passenger area of a vehicle on a public road is:

The offense applies to the driver and to passengers, and it applies even when the vehicle is stopped on a public roadway.

Q8. When someone signals to merge into your lane, the safest response is to:

Letting a merge happen costs you a second and prevents a side-swipe.

Q9. Using a hand-held phone in a Texas school crossing zone is:

Hand-held use in an active school zone is prohibited, and drivers under 18 may not use a wireless device at all.

Q10. Driving after using marijuana is:

Legal to possess does not mean legal to drive on. Impaired driving laws cover cannabis the same way they cover alcohol.
