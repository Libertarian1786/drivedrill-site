Q1. A left turn on red from a one-way street onto another one-way street is:

Most states permit this turn after a complete stop and yielding, but it is banned in some cities, so obey any posted sign.

Q2. In Utah, a newly licensed driver under 18 generally may not drive between:

Documented exceptions usually cover work, school activities, emergencies and driving with a licensed adult. Carry the paperwork if you rely on one.

Q3. School zone speed limits usually apply:

Read the sign: many zones are time-based, others use flashing beacons or a when-children-are-present condition.

Q4. In Utah, a child must ride rear-facing in the back seat until at least:

Utah's handbook requires children to ride rear-facing in the back seat until at least two years of age and 30 pounds, or longer if the car seat allows.

Q5. Seat belts work best because they:

Belts keep you behind the wheel and away from the windshield, and they position you correctly for the airbag.

Q6. A steady red arrow means:

A red arrow prohibits that movement entirely. Wait for a green or flashing yellow arrow, even if no traffic is coming.

Q7. A standard drink of beer, wine and distilled spirits:

A 12 oz beer, a 5 oz glass of wine and 1.5 oz of spirits each contain roughly the same alcohol, which is why counting drinks does not make you safe to drive.

Q8. In Utah, a driver 21 or older who is not driving a commercial vehicle breaks the law with a blood alcohol concentration over:

Utah was the first state to set its limit at 0.05%, below the 0.08% used elsewhere. Commercial drivers are held to 0.04%, and you can be charged below the limit if you are impaired.

Q9. Before opening your door into traffic after parking, you should:

Dooring a cyclist is a common and serious crash. Check the mirror and over your shoulder, and open with the far hand so your body turns toward traffic.

Q10. In Utah, the minimum age to apply for a learner permit is:

Applicants under 18 also need parental consent, and the permit must be held for a set period before the road test.
