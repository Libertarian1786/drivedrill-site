Q1. You notice one headlight is out at night. You should:

From a distance one light reads as a motorcycle, and oncoming drivers misjudge how much room you take. Fix it before driving at night again.

Q2. In Iowa, an applicant under 18 must log how much supervised driving before the road test?

A parent or guardian certifies the hours. Night driving is where new drivers crash most, which is why a share of the total has to be after dark.

Q3. In Iowa, a driver under 18 who holds an instruction permit, intermediate license, or special minor restricted license may:

Iowa bars drivers under 18 who hold an instruction permit, intermediate license, or special minor restricted license from making or receiving phone calls while driving unless their vehicle is stopped off the traveled roadway.

Q4. In Iowa, a child under six years old must be secured in:

Iowa law requires a child under six years old to be secured in a child restraint system, such as a safety seat or booster seat, rather than a seat belt alone.

Q5. The only thing that reduces blood alcohol concentration is:

The liver processes alcohol at a fixed rate. Nothing speeds it up, so the only plan that works is not driving.

Q6. An unsecured load in the back of a pickup truck is:

You are responsible for anything that falls off your vehicle. Tie loads down and cover loose material.

Q7. You should check your mirrors:

Knowing what is behind you is what turns a sudden stop into a controlled one.

Q8. Before driving on a frosty morning you should:

Driving with a peephole is a citation in most states, and snow left on the roof slides onto your windshield or the car behind you.

Q9. In Iowa, when no speed limit is posted, the limit on an urban interstate highway is:

70 mph applies to rural interstates. A lower limit can be set, and the posted sign always governs.

Q10. A conviction for driving under the influence usually results in:

Besides the suspension, fines and possible jail, a DUI stays on your driving record for years, and insurers usually raise premiums sharply.

Q11. The recommended hand position on the steering wheel is:

States teach 9 and 3, 8 and 4, or lower still. Each gives good control and keeps your arms out of the airbag's path; 10 and 2 puts them where it deploys.

Q12. Driving after using marijuana is:

Legal to possess does not mean legal to drive on. Impaired driving laws cover cannabis the same way they cover alcohol.

Q13. You should switch on your headlights:

Headlights are about being seen as much as seeing. Most states also require them in rain, fog, snow or whenever wipers are on.

Q14. Excessive speeding, well above the limit, is treated more seriously because:

Kinetic energy rises with the square of speed, so a small increase in speed is a large increase in damage and stopping distance.

Q15. The safest way to deal with a distracting situation inside the car is to:

Nothing inside the car is worth your attention at speed. Find a safe place to stop.

Q16. In Iowa's manual, staying awake for 18 hours straight is said to impair you as much as a blood alcohol concentration of:

Iowa's manual states that being awake for 18 hours straight impairs a driver about as much as a BAC of 0.08 percent, which is legally drunk.

Q17. In Iowa, when may you pass a stopped school bus with its red lights flashing without stopping first?

On a road of four or more lanes, only traffic behind the bus must stop. On a narrower road, traffic in both directions stops -- a physical median does not by itself excuse you. Overtaking a bus from behind always requires a stop.

Q18. In Iowa, refusing a breathalyzer test when lawfully asked results in:

Refusing a breathalyzer test in Iowa carries consequences more severe than failing one, including an automatic one-year license revocation and a minimum $1,250 fine.

Q19. In Iowa, the Steer It-Clear It law requires that after a crash with no injuries, you:

Iowa's Steer It-Clear It law requires drivers involved in a crash with no injuries to move an operable vehicle out of the driving lanes if it can be done safely, reducing the risk of a secondary crash.

Q20. You are driving through a residential street where children are playing. You should:

Children act on impulse and are hidden by parked cars. Cover the brake and cut your speed well below the limit.

Q21. In Iowa, the maximum speed limit on rural interstate highways is:

That is the highest limit the state allows on a rural interstate. The posted sign always governs, and a lower speed can still be required by conditions.

Q22. Driving while your license is suspended usually results in:

It is one of the few traffic offenses that regularly carries jail, and it extends the original suspension.

Q23. Driving without the insurance your state requires can result in:

Financial responsibility is a condition of driving. The penalties apply whether or not you have a crash.

Q24. A red warning light appears on the dashboard while you are driving. You should:

Red generally means stop now: oil pressure, temperature, brakes or charging. Amber usually means service soon.

Q25. In Iowa, the minimum age to apply for a learner permit is:

Applicants under 18 also need parental consent, and the permit must be held for a set period before the road test.
