Q1. In Iowa, the Steer It-Clear It law requires that after a crash with no injuries, you:

Iowa's Steer It-Clear It law requires drivers involved in a crash with no injuries to move an operable vehicle out of the driving lanes if it can be done safely, reducing the risk of a secondary crash.

Q2. School zone speed limits usually apply:

Read the sign: many zones are time-based, others use flashing beacons or a when-children-are-present condition.

Q3. A sign reading NARROW BRIDGE means:

Narrow bridge warnings mean less room than usual. Slow down, keep to your side, and watch for oncoming vehicles that may drift toward the center.

Q4. A school bus ahead of you stops and puts out its red flashing lights and stop arm. You must:

Red flashing lights and an extended stop arm mean children are getting on or off. Stop well back and wait until the bus withdraws the arm and moves.

Q5. You are on a road that ends at a T intersection with no signs. You should:

At a T intersection the road that ends yields to the road that continues.

Q6. In Iowa's manual, staying awake for 18 hours straight is said to impair you as much as a blood alcohol concentration of:

Iowa's manual states that being awake for 18 hours straight impairs a driver about as much as a BAC of 0.08 percent, which is legally drunk.

Q7. When towing a trailer you should:

Extra weight lengthens every distance. Many states also set lower speed limits for vehicles that are towing.

Q8. In Iowa, a newly licensed driver under 18 generally may not drive between:

Documented exceptions usually cover work, school activities, emergencies and driving with a licensed adult. Carry the paperwork if you rely on one.

Q9. In Iowa, a driver under 21 found operating while intoxicated with a BAC of at least .02 but under .08 will have driving privileges revoked for a minimum of:

An underage Iowa driver counts as operating while intoxicated at a BAC of .02 or more; the minimum revocation is 60 days, rising to a minimum of 180 days if the BAC is .08 or higher.

Q10. A white rectangular sign with black letters or numbers is:

White rectangles are regulatory: speed limits, turn restrictions, lane use, parking rules. Ignoring one is a traffic violation.
