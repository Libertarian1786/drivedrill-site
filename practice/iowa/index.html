Q1. In Iowa, a driver under 18 who holds an instruction permit, intermediate license, or special minor restricted license may:

Iowa bars drivers under 18 who hold an instruction permit, intermediate license, or special minor restricted license from making or receiving phone calls while driving unless their vehicle is stopped off the traveled roadway.

Q2. Before opening your door into traffic after parking, you should:

Dooring a cyclist is a common and serious crash. Check the mirror and over your shoulder, and open with the far hand so your body turns toward traffic.

Q3. Brown signs on a highway point to:

Brown is the color for recreational and cultural interest areas: parks, campgrounds, ski areas, museums and historic sites, for example. Blue, by contrast, marks motorist services.

Q4. You must:

Driving without carrying the license is a separate violation in most states, even if you are licensed.

Q5. A curb painted with diagonal yellow lines next to a wide striped area means:

Striped buffer areas separate traffic from hazards, medians or opposing lanes. Driving or parking in them is prohibited.

Q6. In Iowa's manual, staying awake for 18 hours straight is said to impair you as much as a blood alcohol concentration of:

Iowa's manual states that being awake for 18 hours straight impairs a driver about as much as a BAC of 0.08 percent, which is legally drunk.

Q7. Before you turn or change lanes, you should signal:

Signal early and continuously. A signal given as you are already turning tells other drivers nothing they can use.

Q8. In Iowa, the minimum age to apply for a learner permit is:

Applicants under 18 also need parental consent, and the permit must be held for a set period before the road test.

Q9. In Iowa, when no speed limit is posted, the limit on an urban interstate highway is:

70 mph applies to rural interstates. A lower limit can be set, and the posted sign always governs.

Q10. A space cushion around your vehicle is useful because:

Space is time. The more of it you keep on all four sides, the more options you have when something goes wrong.
