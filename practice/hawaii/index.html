Q1. Two lanes merge into one because of road work. The most efficient approach is to:

The zipper merge keeps queues shorter and speeds more even. Take turns at the merge point rather than fighting over it.

Q2. Driving into low sun glare, the best response is to:

Glare hides brake lights and pedestrians. Slow down, clean the inside of your windshield, and leave more room.

Q3. Before changing lanes you should:

Mirrors do not cover the blind spot. A quick head check before moving is what catches the car that is already beside you.

Q4. In Hawaii, you must not pass another vehicle when approaching a hill or curve unless you have a clear view ahead of at least:

The handbook lists approaching a hill or curve where there is not 700 to 1000 feet of clear distance ahead as one of the situations where passing is prohibited.

Q5. In Hawaii, leaving a child unattended in a motor vehicle is prohibited by law when the child is under nine years old and left for:

Hawaii law prohibits a driver or any adult passenger from leaving a child under nine years of age unattended in a motor vehicle for five minutes or longer, because of risks like heat exposure.

Q6. In Hawaii, the minimum age to apply for a learner permit is:

Applicants under 18 also need parental consent, and the permit must be held for a set period before the road test.

Q7. In Hawaii, when you count seconds to check your following distance, the handbook says you should have at least:

The handbook's following-distance method says that if you reach the two-count before your vehicle reaches the marker the leading car passed, you have at least a two second interval, and you should never have less.

Q8. Driving on the shoulder to get around slow traffic is:

The shoulder is for emergencies and for stopped vehicles. Using it as a lane is both illegal and a common cause of crashes with people standing outside their cars.

Q9. On packed snow or ice, the safest braking technique in a vehicle with ABS is to:

With ABS, firm steady pressure is correct and the pedal will vibrate. Pumping defeats the system. Without ABS, threshold or squeeze braking is used instead.

Q10. Hazard lights should generally be used:

In most states hazard lights while driving normally are prohibited because they hide your turn signals.
