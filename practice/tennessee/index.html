Q1. Your brakes fail while driving. You should:

Pump the pedal to build pressure. If that does not work, apply the parking brake gradually and shift to lower gears for engine braking, and look for an open escape path or an uphill slope.

Q2. School zone speed limits usually apply:

Read the sign: many zones are time-based, others use flashing beacons or a when-children-are-present condition.

Q3. In Tennessee, an unmarked police car signals you to pull over on a dark, empty road. What may you do?

In Tennessee, if an unmarked police car signals you to stop, you may drive slowly a short distance to the nearest area where there are other people before stopping.

Q4. In Tennessee, who must wear a safety belt in a 15-passenger van?

Tennessee law requires all occupants of 15-passenger vans to wear safety belts at all times.

Q5. In Tennessee, a fourth or later DUI conviction is:

The Tennessee manual says a fourth or later DUI conviction is a Class E felony.

Q6. In Tennessee, a learner permit (Class PD) holder must drive with whom?

A Tennessee learner permit holder must be accompanied by a licensed driver who is at least 21 years old and is sitting in the seat beside the driver.

Q7. Tennessee law requires a yellow traffic light to show for at least how long before the red?

Tennessee law only requires the yellow light to be shown for a minimum of 3 seconds, so be ready to stop.

Q8. A highway has a posted minimum speed, but you cannot safely drive that fast. What should you do?

If you cannot or do not want to drive at or above the posted minimum speed, do not use that highway. Take another route.

Q9. The diagonal stripes on a road-work barricade slope down toward the right. Which way should you go?

Barricade stripes slope down toward the side traffic should pass on. Stripes that slope down to the right mean bear to the right.

Q10. In Tennessee's big cities, what are the yellow TDOT HELP trucks on the interstates?

TDOT's yellow HELP trucks are incident response units that assist motorists on interstates in Memphis, Nashville, Chattanooga and Knoxville, and may remove obstructions from the road.

Q11. You come to a flooded road, and you know a different way to get where you are going. What should you do?

Turn around and take another route when a road ahead is flooded. You cannot tell how deep the water is or whether the road is still there under it, and a vehicle can be swept away.

Q12. You are on an on-ramp joining a highway, and a yellow ADDED LANE sign is posted. What does it mean for you?

An ADDED LANE sign means two roads come together and a lane is added, so the entering lane keeps going and you are not required to merge. Still watch for traffic changing lanes.

Q13. As a condition of probation for a Tennessee DUI, what public service does the manual describe?

The Tennessee manual says DUI probation includes litter pick-up for three 8-hour shifts while wearing a vest that identifies the offender as a drunk driver.

Q14. In Tennessee, what can you dial from a cell phone to reach the Tennessee Highway Patrol for free?

In Tennessee, dial *THP (*847) from a cell phone for a free call to the nearest Tennessee Highway Patrol dispatch center.

Q15. You are following a bus or a truck with a hazardous materials placard toward a railroad crossing. What should you expect it to do?

Buses, school buses and vehicles carrying hazardous materials must stop at railroad crossings even when no train is in sight. Be ready to stop behind them.

Q16. Where should a child in a rear-facing car seat never ride?

An inflating front airbag can strike a rear-facing car seat and seriously injure or kill the child. A rear-facing seat belongs in the back seat.

Q17. A yellow warning sign reads SOFT SHOULDER. What does it warn you about?

SOFT SHOULDER warns that the dirt or gravel beside the pavement is soft. Stay on the pavement; if your wheels drift onto a soft shoulder, the car can be pulled off the road and you can lose control.

Q18. A deer suddenly steps into the road ahead of you. What is usually the safest choice?

Swerving to miss an animal can send you into oncoming traffic or off the road, which is often worse than hitting it. Brake firmly, stay in control and keep your lane.

Q19. Under Tennessee's hands-free law, a driver is prohibited from:

Tennessee's hands-free law makes it illegal for a driver to hold a cellphone or mobile device with any part of their body while driving.

Q20. A car moves into the space in front of you and is much too close. What should you do first?

If a vehicle merges in front of you too closely, ease off the accelerator to rebuild your following distance. Braking hard could cause the driver behind you to hit you.

Q21. Your car has airbags. Do you still need to wear your seat belt?

Airbags are designed to work together with seat belts, not to replace them. Without a belt you can be thrown into the opening airbag or out of the car.

Q22. Driving while your license is suspended usually results in:

It is one of the few traffic offenses that regularly carries jail, and it extends the original suspension.

Q23. A driver is following you too closely. The safest response is to:

Increase your own space cushion and give them a chance to go by. Brake-checking turns their mistake into your crash.

Q24. A new Tennessee resident with a license from another state must get a Tennessee license within how many days of establishing residency?

The Tennessee manual says new or returning residents must obtain a Tennessee driver license no later than 30 days after establishing residency.

Q25. You are driving through a residential street where children are playing. You should:

Children act on impulse and are hidden by parked cars. Cover the brake and cut your speed well below the limit.
