Q1. A green arrow pointing left means:

A green arrow is a protected turn. Still check the crosswalk for people before you move.

Q2. A funeral procession with its lights on is passing through an intersection. You should:

Do not cut into or interrupt a procession, even if you have a green light, unless directed by an officer.

Q3. In Tennessee, the law requires a car seat or booster seat until:

State law is a minimum. Safety experts recommend a booster until the adult belt fits properly: lap belt low across the hips and shoulder belt across the chest, not the neck.

Q4. Worn tire tread is dangerous mainly because it:

Tread channels water away. Once it is shallow, wet grip falls off sharply. Check with a tread gauge or the built-in wear bars.

Q5. In Tennessee, the move-over law requires you to change lanes, or slow down, when passing:

Move to a lane that is not next to the stopped vehicle when you safely can. When you cannot, slow down. People working at the roadside are struck every year by drivers who did neither.

Q6. You are driving through a residential street where children are playing. You should:

Children act on impulse and are hidden by parked cars. Cover the brake and cut your speed well below the limit.

Q7. In Tennessee, effective January 1, 2018, using a hand-held phone in a marked school zone while a warning flasher is operating is an offense for:

Tennessee's school zone cell phone law makes it an offense for a driver 18 or older to use a hand-held mobile phone in a marked school zone while a warning flasher is operating.

Q8. You are on a road that ends at a T intersection with no signs. You should:

At a T intersection the road that ends yields to the road that continues.

Q9. In Tennessee, the minimum age to apply for a learner permit is:

Applicants under 18 also need parental consent, and the permit must be held for a set period before the road test.

Q10. A bicycle on a public road is:

Riders must obey signals and signs, and drivers must yield to them the same way they would to another vehicle.
