Q1. An orange diamond-shaped sign means:

Orange is the work-zone color. Expect workers, equipment, lane shifts and reduced speed limits, and fines for speeding are usually higher.

Q2. In Tennessee, the minimum age to apply for a learner permit is:

Applicants under 18 also need parental consent, and the permit must be held for a set period before the road test.

Q3. In Tennessee, effective January 1, 2018, using a hand-held phone in a marked school zone while a warning flasher is operating is an offense for:

Tennessee's school zone cell phone law makes it an offense for a driver 18 or older to use a hand-held mobile phone in a marked school zone while a warning flasher is operating.

Q4. Passing on the right is allowed when:

You may pass on the right on multi-lane roads or when a vehicle is turning left, but never by driving onto the shoulder.

Q5. In Tennessee, the move-over law requires you to change lanes, or slow down, when passing:

Move to a lane that is not next to the stopped vehicle when you safely can. When you cannot, slow down. People working at the roadside are struck every year by drivers who did neither.

Q6. An anti-lock braking system is designed to:

ABS preserves steering control. On gravel or deep snow it may even lengthen the stop, but you can still steer around the hazard.

Q7. In Tennessee, the law requires a car seat or booster seat until:

State law is a minimum. Safety experts recommend a booster until the adult belt fits properly: lap belt low across the hips and shoulder belt across the chest, not the neck.

Q8. A school bus ahead of you stops and puts out its red flashing lights and stop arm. You must:

Red flashing lights and an extended stop arm mean children are getting on or off. Stop well back and wait until the bus withdraws the arm and moves.

Q9. A round yellow sign with a large X and the letters RR warns you of:

The round yellow RR sign is the advance warning for a railroad crossing. Slow down, look and listen, and be ready to stop before the tracks.

Q10. A flagger in a work zone holds up a sign reading SLOW. You should:

Flaggers have the same authority as signs and signals in a work zone. Follow their direction and watch for workers on foot.
