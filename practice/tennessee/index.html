Q1. You should switch on your headlights:

Headlights are about being seen as much as seeing. Most states also require them in rain, fog, snow or whenever wipers are on.

Q2. In Tennessee, the law requires a car seat or booster seat until:

State law is a minimum. Safety experts recommend a booster until the adult belt fits properly: lap belt low across the hips and shoulder belt across the chest, not the neck.

Q3. In Tennessee, a learner permit must be held for at least how long before the road test?

The holding period exists so new drivers practice across seasons and conditions rather than cramming a license in a weekend.

Q4. In Tennessee, using a hand-held cell phone while driving is:

The state requires hands-free operation for every driver. Novice drivers are usually barred from using a phone at all, even hands-free.

Q5. In Tennessee, the move-over law requires you to change lanes, or slow down, when passing:

Move to a lane that is not next to the stopped vehicle when you safely can. When you cannot, slow down. People working at the roadside are struck every year by drivers who did neither.

Q6. In Tennessee, the minimum age to apply for a learner permit is:

Applicants under 18 also need parental consent, and the permit must be held for a set period before the road test.

Q7. In Tennessee, a newly licensed driver under 18 generally may not drive between:

Documented exceptions usually cover work, school activities, emergencies and driving with a licensed adult. Carry the paperwork if you rely on one.

Q8. When a train is coming and you must stop at a railroad crossing, stop no closer to the nearest rail than:

Fifteen feet keeps you clear of the train's overhang and of the gate arm. Most states also say to stop no farther than 50 feet back, so you can still see the tracks.

Q9. Brown signs on a highway point to:

Brown is the color for recreational and cultural interest areas: parks, campgrounds, ski areas, museums and historic sites, for example. Blue, by contrast, marks motorist services.

Q10. You are in the left lane of a highway and a line of traffic builds up behind you. You should:

The left lane is for passing. Once traffic stacks up behind you, moving right is both safer and, in many states, required.

Q11. In Tennessee, you may not park within how many feet of a traffic signal or stop sign?

Tennessee prohibits parking within 30 feet of a traffic signal, stop sign, or other traffic control device.

Q12. In Tennessee, it is illegal to park within how many feet of a railroad crossing?

Tennessee law prohibits parking within 50 feet of a railroad crossing.

Q13. Driving without the insurance your state requires can result in:

Financial responsibility is a condition of driving. The penalties apply whether or not you have a crash.

Q14. A green arrow pointing left means:

A green arrow is a protected turn. Still check the crosswalk for people before you move.

Q15. The accelerator sticks open. You should:

Neutral disconnects the engine from the wheels so you keep power steering and brakes. Once stopped, shut the engine off.

Q16. The basic speed law means:

The posted limit is the maximum for perfect conditions. In rain, fog, traffic or at night, the legal safe speed is lower.

Q17. In Tennessee, the handbook says reading or sending a text takes your eyes off the road for a minimum of:

Tennessee's manual notes that reading or sending a text takes your eyes off the road for a minimum of 5 seconds.

Q18. The only thing that reduces blood alcohol concentration is:

The liver processes alcohol at a fixed rate. Nothing speeds it up, so the only plan that works is not driving.

Q19. After a crash with injury or significant damage you must usually:

Every state sets a damage threshold and a deadline for reporting. Exchange information at the scene and file the report on time.

Q20. Liability insurance pays for:

Liability is the coverage states require. Collision and comprehensive, which cover your own vehicle, are optional.

Q21. In Tennessee, an applicant under 18 must log how much supervised driving before the road test?

A parent or guardian certifies the hours. Night driving is where new drivers crash most, which is why a share of the total has to be after dark.

Q22. Speeding fines in a work zone are usually:

Most states increase work-zone fines and add penalties when workers are present, whether or not the crew is visible at that moment.

Q23. You may park in a space marked with the International Symbol of Access:

Accessible spaces need a valid placard or plate, and the person it belongs to must be involved in the trip. Fines are steep and vehicles are towed.

Q24. An exit number on a green sign tells you:

Exit numbers usually match the nearest mile marker, which makes it easy to judge how far away your exit still is.

Q25. On a long downgrade you should:

Continuous braking overheats the brakes and they fade. Let the engine hold the vehicle back and brake in short firm applications.
