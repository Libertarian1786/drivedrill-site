Q1. The pedestrian signal shows a flashing orange hand with a countdown while you wait to turn. It means:

The countdown is for people already crossing. Wait until the crosswalk is clear before completing your turn.

Q2. A standard drink of beer, wine and distilled spirits:

A 12 oz beer, a 5 oz glass of wine and 1.5 oz of spirits each contain roughly the same alcohol, which is why counting drinks does not make you safe to drive.

Q3. In Louisiana, to pass the OMV knowledge test for a driver's license, you must correctly answer:

The Louisiana driver's guide states you must correctly answer 80 percent of the knowledge test questions to receive a passing score.

Q4. In Louisiana, the move-over law requires you to change lanes, or slow down, when passing:

Move to a lane that is not next to the stopped vehicle when you safely can. When you cannot, slow down. People working at the roadside are struck every year by drivers who did neither.

Q5. Another driver is behaving aggressively toward you. You should:

Do not engage. Let them go, change your route if they follow, and drive to a police station rather than home.

Q6. In heavy fog you should drive with:

High beams reflect off fog and blind you. Use low beams or fog lights, slow down, and pull well off the road if you cannot see.

Q7. A curb painted with diagonal yellow lines next to a wide striped area means:

Striped buffer areas separate traffic from hazards, medians or opposing lanes. Driving or parking in them is prohibited.

Q8. Adjusting your side mirrors slightly outward compared with the usual setting:

Wider mirror angles shrink the blind spot, but a head check before changing lanes is still the move that catches what mirrors miss.

Q9. In Louisiana, when may you pass a stopped school bus with its red lights flashing without stopping first?

A four-lane road with a center turn lane counts as divided. A two-lane road with a center turn lane does not, so on that road you must still stop.

Q10. In Louisiana, the maximum speed limit on rural interstate highways is:

That is the highest limit the state allows on a rural interstate. The posted sign always governs, and a lower speed can still be required by conditions.

Q11. Before opening your door into traffic after parking, you should:

Dooring a cyclist is a common and serious crash. Check the mirror and over your shoulder, and open with the far hand so your body turns toward traffic.

Q12. An oncoming driver fails to dim their high beams. You should:

Watching the right edge line keeps you in your lane while your eyes recover. Retaliating with high beams blinds you both.

Q13. Driving on the shoulder to get around slow traffic is:

The shoulder is for emergencies and for stopped vehicles. Using it as a lane is both illegal and a common cause of crashes with people standing outside their cars.

Q14. You should check your mirrors:

Knowing what is behind you is what turns a sudden stop into a controlled one.

Q15. A green arrow pointing left means:

A green arrow is a protected turn. Still check the crosswalk for people before you move.

Q16. In Louisiana, the minimum age to apply for a learner permit is:

Applicants under 18 also need parental consent, and the permit must be held for a set period before the road test.

Q17. Unless a sign prohibits it, a right turn on red is allowed:

Right on red requires a full stop first, then yielding to everyone already entitled to go. A NO TURN ON RED sign removes the option entirely.

Q18. You are involved in a crash. You are required to:

Stopping, identifying yourself and rendering aid are legal duties everywhere. Leaving the scene turns a minor crash into a criminal charge.

Q19. An unrestrained pet in the car is a problem because:

Secure pets in a carrier or harness. Several states treat an animal in your lap as distracted driving.

Q20. In Louisiana, if you refuse to submit to a chemical test after a lawful DWI arrest, your driving privileges are suspended for:

Refusing the chemical test after a lawful DWI arrest results in a 365-day suspension of driving privileges on a first offense.

Q21. After you move, you must usually:

States set a deadline, often 10 to 30 days. Renewal notices and suspension notices go to the address on file.

Q22. In Louisiana, the maximum posted speed limit on urban interstate highways is:

Urban interstates are held to a lower maximum than open rural highway, because of interchange spacing and traffic volume.

Q23. In Louisiana, when driving behind a motorcycle, the manual recommends a following distance of at least:

The Louisiana driver's guide recommends leaving at least a four-second following distance behind a motorcycle.

Q24. In Louisiana, a child must ride in a rear-facing, federally approved car seat from birth until at least:

Louisiana's child passenger restraint law requires a rear-facing car seat from birth until at least age 2, after which a forward-facing seat is used.

Q25. In Louisiana, a learner permit must be held for at least how long before the road test?

The holding period exists so new drivers practice across seasons and conditions rather than cramming a license in a weekend.
