Q1. Can you be convicted of driving under the influence if no breath or blood test was done?

You can be arrested and convicted of DUI without a BAC reading. Officers and courts can rely on other evidence, such as how you drove and how you did on field sobriety tests.

Q2. How should you help a crash victim who may be going into shock?

To help someone in shock, have them lie flat, raise their legs slightly if they are not hurt there, and keep them warm. Do not give them anything to drink.

Q3. In Louisiana, a teen must keep the intermediate license until:

The intermediate license must be kept until the driver's 17th birthday before moving to a full license.

Q4. In Louisiana, the minimum age for a Class D (chauffeur) license is:

The Louisiana guide sets the minimum age for a Class D license at 17.

Q5. The best way to reduce glare from headlights behind you at night is to:

The night position deflects the bright image while keeping a usable view behind you.

Q6. When an airbag is fitted, you should sit:

Airbags deploy explosively. Handbooks ask for 10 inches or more, some 12. Distance plus a properly worn seat belt is what makes them protective rather than harmful.

Q7. The water covering a road ahead looks shallow. What is true about it?

Floodwater can hide dips and wash away the road surface beneath it, so you cannot judge safety by how shallow it looks. Turn around rather than try to cross.

Q8. You approach a hill or curve that you cannot see over or around. How fast should you be going?

Drive slowly enough that you could stop if a stalled vehicle is just over the hill or around the curve. Even a road you have driven many times can hide a problem.

Q9. In Louisiana, a school speed limit sign has a flashing light. When must you obey the school speed limit?

Where the school sign has flashers, the school limit applies only while they flash. Otherwise obey the road's normal speed limit.

Q10. In Louisiana, for a second DWI conviction, at least how much of the jail sentence must be served without parole, probation or suspension?

A second DWI in Louisiana brings 30 days to six months in jail. At least 48 hours of it must be served without parole, probation or suspension, and more at a high BAC.

Q11. About how long does it take your body to get rid of the alcohol in one standard drink?

Your body removes alcohol at about one drink per hour, and nothing speeds that up. Coffee, food or a cold shower will not make you sober faster.

Q12. What is a reversible lane?

Reversible lanes carry traffic one way at some times and the other way at other times, usually to handle rush-hour traffic. Check the signs and lane-use signals before you enter one.

Q13. On a Louisiana highway with more than one lane in your direction, the law says to drive in the right lane. Which is one of the times you may leave it?

Louisiana law says all vehicles should travel in the right lane except when preparing for a left turn, passing, or when the right lanes are congested.

Q14. You must slow down for a hazard that drivers behind you cannot see yet. How can you warn them?

Lightly tapping the brake pedal three or four times flashes your brake lights and warns drivers behind you. You can also use your emergency flashers.

Q15. The road is most slippery:

Oil and rubber build up on dry pavement and float to the surface when rain starts. Slow down and double your following distance.

Q16. In Louisiana, the law requires a car seat or booster seat until:

State law is a minimum. Safety experts recommend a booster until the adult belt fits properly: lap belt low across the hips and shoulder belt across the chest, not the neck.

Q17. You start driving on a snow-covered road. How can you find out how much traction your tires have?

Start out slowly, then test your brakes gently once the car is moving to feel how much traction you have. A gentle test warns you before you need to stop.

Q18. You miss your exit while driving in a roundabout. What should you do?

If you miss your exit, stay in the roundabout and go around again until you return to it. Never stop, back up or cut across the island.

Q19. Your lane has a painted arrow that shows both a curved arrow and a straight arrow. What may you do from this lane?

A lane marked with both a curved and a straight arrow is a shared lane: you may turn in the direction the curved arrow points or continue straight.

Q20. You need to move across several lanes of traffic on a highway. How should you do it?

Change lanes one at a time: signal, check your mirrors and blind spot, move into the next lane, then repeat. Cutting across several lanes at once surprises other drivers.

Q21. In Louisiana, you want to cancel the insurance on a vehicle you will no longer drive. What should you do first?

Remove the license plate and surrender it to the Office of Motor Vehicles before the insurance is canceled.

Q22. Which of these can be an early warning sign of carbon monoxide poisoning while you drive?

Sudden drowsiness, yawning, dizziness or nausea can be early signs of carbon monoxide poisoning. The gas has no smell, so these feelings may be your only warning.

Q23. In Louisiana, during the restricted period, a young driver may carry:

Crash risk for a teen driver climbs sharply with each additional teen passenger, which is what this rule is built around.

Q24. In Louisiana, when may you pass a stopped school bus with its red lights flashing without stopping first?

A four-lane road with a center turn lane counts as divided. A two-lane road with a center turn lane does not, so on that road you must still stop.

Q25. In Louisiana, how close to a crosswalk at an intersection may you park?

Louisiana does not allow parking within 20 feet of a crosswalk at an intersection, so drivers can see people crossing.
