Q1. In Colorado, passing is prohibited within what distance of an intersection or railroad crossing?

In Colorado, you cannot pass within 100 feet of an intersection or railroad crossing. You also cannot pass within 100 feet of a bridge, viaduct or tunnel when your view is obstructed.

Q2. What is a reversible lane?

Reversible lanes carry traffic one way at some times and the other way at other times, usually to handle rush-hour traffic. Check the signs and lane-use signals before you enter one.

Q3. You notice one headlight is out at night. You should:

From a distance one light reads as a motorcycle, and oncoming drivers misjudge how much room you take. Fix it before driving at night again.

Q4. A strong crosswind is pushing your vehicle. You should:

Wind gusts are strongest in open country and beside large vehicles. Slow down, hold the wheel firmly, and expect a push when a truck passes.

Q5. A pedestrian hybrid beacon over a mid-block crosswalk shows two steady red lights. What must you do?

A pedestrian hybrid beacon is dark until someone pushes the crossing button. Steady red means stop and stay stopped while pedestrians cross; only when it changes to flashing red may you go once your path is clear.

Q6. In Colorado, the open container law makes it unlawful to possess an open container of which of these in the passenger area of a vehicle on a public road?

Colorado's open container ban covers marijuana as well as alcohol. It applies to the passenger area of a motor vehicle on a public road.

Q7. In Colorado, when may you briefly cross a solid yellow center line to pass a bicyclist?

In Colorado, you may briefly cross a solid yellow line to pass a bicyclist when no traffic is coming and you have a clear view ahead, and you must still leave at least three feet. If you cannot give three feet, slow down and stay behind the bicyclist until it is safe to pass.

Q8. When the lights at a railroad crossing start to flash, about how much warning do you usually have before the train arrives?

Crossing signals are set to give at least about 20 seconds of warning before the train reaches the crossing. When the lights start flashing, stop; there is not enough time to try to beat the train.

Q9. In Colorado, which person is exempt from the seat belt law?

A person who carries a written statement from a physician that they are not physically or psychologically required to wear seat belts is exempt. Other exceptions are emergency personnel, passenger buses and school buses, farm equipment and delivery van drivers on the job.

Q10. In Colorado, unless another limit is posted, the speed limit on open mountain highways is:

Colorado's default speed limit on open mountain highways is 40 mph unless another limit is posted.

Q11. You should switch on your headlights:

Headlights are about being seen as much as seeing. The law often also requires them in rain, fog or snow, or whenever the wipers are on.

Q12. At a railroad crossing, a small blue sign shows a phone number and a crossing ID number. When should you use it?

The blue Emergency Notification System (ENS) sign gives the railroad's emergency number and the crossing's ID. Call it if a vehicle is stalled on the tracks or the signals or gates are not working, so trains can be warned; call 911 too if anyone is in danger.

Q13. When leaving a freeway you should:

Keep your speed until you are on the deceleration ramp, then slow. Braking in the travel lane surprises the traffic behind you.

Q14. In Colorado, the handbook says to break long driving periods into segments of how long?

Colorado's handbook suggests splitting a long trip into two-hour driving segments. If you get drowsy, pull over and rest.

Q15. You are stranded in a snowstorm. What helps rescuers find your vehicle?

Turn on your hazard lights and tie a bright flag or cloth to the antenna or door handle so rescuers can spot the vehicle. Stay inside and out of traffic.

Q16. In Colorado, in which zones are traffic fines doubled?

The Colorado handbook says fines are doubled in work zones, as they are in school zones.

Q17. Unless otherwise posted, the default Colorado speed limit in a residence district is:

This is the default limit Colorado law sets for residence districts when no other speed is posted.

Q18. In the United States, which way does traffic travel around a roundabout?

Traffic in a roundabout moves counterclockwise, to the right of the center island. Never drive around it the wrong way, even to reach an exit on your left.

Q19. A driver holds their left arm out the window, bent at the elbow and pointing down. What does that mean?

When a driver's left arm points down from the window, the driver is slowing down or stopping.

Q20. How does moving floodwater affect SUVs and pickup trucks?

Moving floodwater about two feet deep can carry away most vehicles, including SUVs and pickup trucks. Do not assume a heavy vehicle is safe in it.

Q21. A motorcyclist has been hurt in a crash. What should you do about the rider's helmet?

Do not remove an injured rider's helmet. If the neck or spine is hurt, moving the head can cause more damage, so wait for trained help.

Q22. In Colorado, you must yield the right-of-way to service vehicles and maintenance equipment that are in use and showing which warning lights?

Colorado drivers must give way to working service vehicles and maintenance equipment with flashing yellow and blue warning lights. Take extra care when you approach, overtake or pass them.

Q23. In Colorado, you are a passenger in a crash with injuries, and the driver is physically unable to report it. What must you do?

In Colorado, if the driver is physically unable to report an injury crash, a passenger is required to report it and stay if police say so.

Q24. Seat belts work best because they:

Belts keep you behind the wheel and away from the windshield, and they position you correctly for the airbag.

Q25. When an airbag is fitted, you should sit:

Airbags deploy explosively. Handbooks ask for 10 inches or more, some 12. Distance plus a properly worn seat belt is what makes them protective rather than harmful.
