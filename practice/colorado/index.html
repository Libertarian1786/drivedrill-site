Q1. In Colorado, if you cannot safely move out of the lane next to a stopped emergency vehicle, you must slow to:

Changing lanes is the first choice. The speed rule is what applies when traffic or the road makes a lane change unsafe.

Q2. After you move, you must usually:

States set a deadline, often 10 to 30 days. Renewal notices and suspension notices go to the address on file.

Q3. The best way to reduce glare from headlights behind you at night is to:

The night position deflects the bright image while keeping a usable view behind you.

Q4. In Colorado, a first conviction for Driving While Ability Impaired (DWAI) applies at a blood alcohol content of:

Colorado's DWAI standard applies at a lower blood alcohol level than a full DUI conviction.

Q5. A funeral procession with its lights on is passing through an intersection. You should:

Do not cut into or interrupt a procession, even if you have a green light, unless directed by an officer.

Q6. In Colorado, if your high beams are on, you must dim them before coming within this distance of an oncoming vehicle:

Failing to dim high beams in time can blind an oncoming driver with glare.

Q7. You should never park:

Hydrant clearance is set by state law, commonly 10 to 15 feet. Blocked hydrants delay firefighters and are towed.

Q8. A three-point turn should be used only:

The manoeuvre blocks the whole road for several seconds, so it needs a low-traffic street and a clear view both ways.

Q9. A green arrow pointing left means:

A green arrow is a protected turn. Still check the crosswalk for people before you move.

Q10. In Colorado, using a hand-held cell phone while driving is:

The state requires hands-free operation for every driver. Novice drivers are usually barred from using a phone at all, even hands-free.
