Q1. In South Carolina, using a hand-held cell phone while driving is:

The state requires hands-free operation for every driver. Novice drivers are usually barred from using a phone at all, even hands-free.

Q2. A sign at a crosswalk reading STATE LAW: YIELD TO PEDESTRIANS placed in the middle of the road means:

In-street yield signs mark crossings where drivers regularly fail to stop. Yield to anyone in the crosswalk, and never pass a vehicle stopped at one.

Q3. An orange diamond-shaped sign means:

Orange is the work-zone color. Expect workers, equipment, lane shifts and reduced speed limits, and fines for speeding are usually higher.

Q4. In South Carolina, the law requires a car seat or booster seat until:

State law is a minimum. Safety experts recommend a booster until the adult belt fits properly: lap belt low across the hips and shoulder belt across the chest, not the neck.

Q5. A prescription medicine that makes you drowsy:

Impaired driving laws cover any drug, prescribed or not. Read the label and do not drive when it warns you about machinery.

Q6. In South Carolina, if you fail the driving skills test on your first attempt, you must wait before being retested:

South Carolina requires a wait of at least two business days after a first failed skills test before you can retest; the wait gets longer on later failed attempts.

Q7. A yellow sign showing a traffic light means:

Signal-ahead signs appear where the light is hard to see in time. Ease off the accelerator and cover the brake so you can stop if it changes.

Q8. In South Carolina, a regular driver's license (age 17 or older) will be suspended once your point total reaches:

South Carolina suspends a regular license once the point total reaches 12 or more; six or more points only brings a warning letter.

Q9. A funeral procession with its lights on is passing through an intersection. You should:

Do not cut into or interrupt a procession, even if you have a green light, unless directed by an officer.

Q10. To make a right turn from a two-way street, you should start the turn from:

Turn from the lane closest to the curb and finish in the lane closest to the curb, so you do not swing wide into another lane.
