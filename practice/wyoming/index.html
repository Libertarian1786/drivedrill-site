Q1. An anti-lock braking system is designed to:

ABS preserves steering control. On gravel or deep snow it may even lengthen the stop, but you can still steer around the hazard.

Q2. Black ice is most likely to form:

Bridges and shaded stretches lose heat fastest. If the road looks wet but spray has stopped coming off other cars, suspect ice.

Q3. An unsecured load in the back of a pickup truck is:

You are responsible for anything that falls off your vehicle. Tie loads down and cover loose material.

Q4. In Wyoming, during the restricted period, a young driver may carry:

Crash risk for a teen driver climbs sharply with each additional teen passenger, which is what this rule is built around.

Q5. In Wyoming, to meet the vision standard for a driver's license, an applicant must have what visual acuity and field of vision?

Wyoming requires 20/40 acuity with both eyes, with or without glasses, and a combined horizontal field of vision of at least 120 degrees.

Q6. Speeding fines in a work zone are usually:

Most states increase work-zone fines and add penalties when workers are present, whether or not the crew is visible at that moment.

Q7. On a long downgrade you should:

Continuous braking overheats the brakes and they fade. Let the engine hold the vehicle back and brake in short firm applications.

Q8. In Wyoming, when licensees change their address, they must notify the department in writing within:

Wyoming requires licensees to notify the department in writing of an address change within 10 days.

Q9. The recommended hand position on the steering wheel is:

States teach 9 and 3, 8 and 4, or lower still. Each gives good control and keeps your arms out of the airbag's path; 10 and 2 puts them where it deploys.

Q10. In Wyoming, a learner permit must be held for at least how long before the road test?

The holding period makes sure there is time for supervised practice before the road test.

Q11. In Wyoming, an approved driver education certification card must show that the student completed how much classroom and behind-the-wheel instruction?

Wyoming's driver education certification card must show 30 hours of classroom instruction and six hours of behind-the-wheel driving.

Q12. A green arrow pointing left means:

A green arrow is a protected turn. Still check the crosswalk for people before you move.

Q13. Overdriving your headlights means:

High beams let you see about 350 feet ahead, and low beams much less. At highway speed you can outrun that, which is why night speeds should be lower.

Q14. In Wyoming, if you fail the written knowledge test, the manual says applicants are allowed to:

Wyoming allows applicants for a driver's license to take the written test twice in one day.

Q15. In Wyoming, a driver under 21 found in physical control of a vehicle with an alcohol concentration of 0.02 percent or more faces a first-offense Youthful Driver Suspension of:

Wyoming's Youthful Driver Suspension for a driver under 21 with an alcohol concentration of 0.02 percent or more carries a 90-day suspension for a first offense.

Q16. In Wyoming, the law requires a car seat or booster seat until:

State law is a minimum. Safety experts recommend a booster until the adult belt fits properly: lap belt low across the hips and shoulder belt across the chest, not the neck.

Q17. You come up behind slow-moving farm equipment displaying an orange triangle. You should:

The orange triangle marks a vehicle traveling under 25 mph. Closing speed is higher than it looks, so slow early and pass only with a long clear view.

Q18. In Wyoming, to qualify for the "extreme inconvenience" restricted license at age 14-15, the applicant must live how far from school and/or employment?

Wyoming's extreme-inconvenience restricted license requires the 14-15 year old applicant to live five or more miles from school and/or employment.

Q19. Implied consent means that by driving in the state you have agreed to:

Refusing the test carries its own suspension, usually longer than the one for failing it, and the refusal can be used against you.

Q20. You notice one headlight is out at night. You should:

From a distance one light reads as a motorcycle, and oncoming drivers misjudge how much room you take. Fix it before driving at night again.

Q21. You should never park:

Hydrant clearance is set by state law, commonly 10 to 15 feet. Blocked hydrants delay firefighters and are towed.

Q22. At night, pedestrians are hardest to see:

Most pedestrian deaths happen after dark. Lower your speed so your stopping distance fits inside your headlight range.

Q23. In Wyoming, a 14-15 year old's "extreme inconvenience" restricted license is only valid:

Wyoming's extreme-inconvenience restricted license for 14-15 year olds is valid only between 5 a.m. and 8 p.m. and within 50 miles of the licensee's residence.

Q24. An unrestrained pet in the car is a problem because:

Secure pets in a carrier or harness. Several states treat an animal in your lap as distracted driving.

Q25. The best response to feeling drowsy while driving is to:

Drowsy driving impairs you much like alcohol, and micro-sleeps happen without warning. Only sleep fixes it.
