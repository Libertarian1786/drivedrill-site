Q1. A pedestrian starts across the street, and your light turns green before they reach the other side. What must you do?

If the light changes after a pedestrian has entered the crosswalk, the pedestrian still has the right-of-way. Wait until they have finished crossing before you go.

Q2. A very heavy downpour makes it too hard to see the road even with your wipers on. What should you do?

Get off the road to a safe spot and wait until the downpour eases, because you cannot drive safely when you cannot see. Pressing on blind puts you and other drivers at risk.

Q3. Implied consent means that by driving in the state you have agreed to:

Refusing the test carries its own suspension, usually longer than the one for failing it, and the refusal can be used against you.

Q4. In Wyoming, how many motorcycles may ride side by side in the same lane?

The Wyoming manual says no more than two motorcycles may be driven abreast in the same lane, and only by consent of both riders.

Q5. In Wyoming, an instruction permit holder must be accompanied by whom?

The Wyoming manual says the permit holder must be accompanied by a person 18 or older who has a valid license for the type of vehicle, and that person must sit in the front passenger seat.

Q6. A bicyclist is riding toward an intersection and has not signaled. What should you assume?

At intersections, assume a bicyclist is going straight unless they signal otherwise. Do not turn across their path.

Q7. You may not pass another vehicle within a certain distance of the crest of a hill because:

A blind crest hides an oncoming vehicle until it is far too late to abort the pass.

Q8. In Wyoming, an intermediate license holder may carry more than one non-family passenger under 18 only if:

A Wyoming intermediate license holder can exceed the one-passenger limit for non-family minors only when also accompanied by a licensed driver who is 18 or older.

Q9. A large truck is backing up into a loading dock across your path. What should you do?

Truck drivers have a large blind spot behind the trailer and may not see you at all. Never cross behind a truck that is backing up; wait until it is done.

Q10. In Wyoming, by how much does the manual say to reduce your speed on a wet road?

The Wyoming manual says to reduce your speed by at least 5 to 10 mph on a wet road.

Q11. In Wyoming, is a bicycle helmet required by law?

The Wyoming manual says a bicycle helmet is not legally required, but a properly fitted, safety-certified helmet is highly recommended.

Q12. The basic speed law means:

The posted limit is the maximum for perfect conditions. In rain, fog, traffic or at night, the legal safe speed is lower.

Q13. Fog becomes so thick that you cannot see well enough to drive safely. What is the safest choice?

When fog is too thick to see the road, pull well off the road and wait for it to lift. Stopping or creeping in a travel lane risks being hit by drivers who cannot see you.

Q14. In Wyoming, a learner permit must be held for at least how long before the road test?

The holding period makes sure there is time for supervised practice before the road test.

Q15. In Wyoming, you may not park within how many feet of a fire hydrant?

The Wyoming manual says you may not park within 15 feet of a fire hydrant.

Q16. In Wyoming, an approved driver education certification card must show that the student completed how much classroom and behind-the-wheel instruction?

Wyoming's driver education certification card must show 30 hours of classroom instruction and six hours of behind-the-wheel driving.

Q17. Why is it easy to misjudge an oncoming motorcycle?

Because a motorcycle is small, it can look farther away than it is. Assume it is closer than it looks before you turn or pull out in front of it.

Q18. You are at a flashing red light. What should you remember about the traffic on the cross street?

Traffic on the cross street often has a flashing yellow light and may not have to stop at all. Do not pull out until there is a safe gap.

Q19. In Wyoming, when can a young driver get full driving privileges?

The Wyoming manual says that for full driving privileges you must be at least 17, or be 16 and a half and have completed an approved driver education course and held an intermediate license for six months.

Q20. A left turn on red from a one-way street onto another one-way street is:

Where the law allows this turn, you must first stop completely and yield. Some states and cities ban it, so learn your state's rule and obey any posted sign.

Q21. You are inside a roundabout when an ambulance with its siren on comes up behind you. What should you do?

Do not stop inside a roundabout for an emergency vehicle. Continue to your exit or the next one, leave the roundabout, and then pull over to the right and stop.

Q22. A car moves into the space in front of you and is much too close. What should you do first?

If a vehicle merges in front of you too closely, ease off the accelerator to rebuild your following distance. Braking hard could cause the driver behind you to hit you.

Q23. Black ice is most likely to form:

Bridges and shaded stretches lose heat fastest. If the road looks wet but spray has stopped coming off other cars, suspect ice.

Q24. In Wyoming, when ice covers the road, what following distance does the manual give for cars?

The Wyoming manual says to quadruple the number of seconds when ice covers the road, which is eight seconds for cars.

Q25. Where a state uses a point system, it is used to:

Points accumulate with each conviction and are removed over time. Reaching the threshold brings a suspension. A few states, such as Minnesota, act on specific violations instead of points.
