Q1. In Wyoming, an approved driver education certification card must show that the student completed how much classroom and behind-the-wheel instruction?

Wyoming's driver education certification card must show 30 hours of classroom instruction and six hours of behind-the-wheel driving.

Q2. In Wyoming, a learner permit must be held for at least how long before the road test?

The holding period exists so new drivers practise across seasons and conditions rather than cramming a licence in a weekend.

Q3. Your vehicle registration must be:

Expired registration is an easy stop for an officer and, in many states, grounds for towing.

Q4. You are turning left at an intersection with a green light and a car is coming straight toward you. You must:

A left turn always yields to oncoming traffic that is close enough to be a hazard, and to people in the crosswalk you are turning into.

Q5. In Wyoming, the law requires a car seat or booster seat until:

State law is a minimum. Safety experts recommend a booster until the adult belt fits properly: lap belt low across the hips and shoulder belt across the chest, not the neck.

Q6. In Wyoming, when licensees change their address, they must notify the department in writing within:

Wyoming requires licensees to notify the department in writing of an address change within 10 days.

Q7. When a train is coming and you must stop at a railroad crossing, stop no closer to the nearest rail than:

Fifteen feet keeps you clear of the train's overhang and of the gate arm. Most states also say to stop no farther than 50 feet back, so you can still see the tracks.

Q8. A bicycle on a public road is:

Riders must obey signals and signs, and drivers must yield to them the same way they would to another vehicle.

Q9. To make a right turn from a two-way street, you should start the turn from:

Turn from the lane closest to the curb and finish in the lane closest to the curb, so you do not swing wide into another lane.

Q10. A space cushion around your vehicle is useful because:

Space is time. The more of it you keep on all four sides, the more options you have when something goes wrong.
