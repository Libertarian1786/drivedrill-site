Q1. Parking is prohibited:

Never park in a crosswalk, on a sidewalk, in an intersection, or where you would block a curb ramp.

Q2. In Wyoming, a learner permit must be held for at least how long before the road test?

The holding period makes sure there is time for supervised practice before the road test.

Q3. Your vehicle registration must be:

Expired registration is an easy stop for an officer and, in many states, grounds for towing.

Q4. In Wyoming, the law requires a car seat or booster seat until:

State law is a minimum. Safety experts recommend a booster until the adult belt fits properly: lap belt low across the hips and shoulder belt across the chest, not the neck.

Q5. In Wyoming, when licensees change their address, they must notify the department in writing within:

Wyoming requires licensees to notify the department in writing of an address change within 10 days.

Q6. When towing a trailer you should:

Extra weight lengthens every distance. Many states also set lower speed limits for vehicles that are towing.

Q7. In Wyoming, an approved driver education certification card must show that the student completed how much classroom and behind-the-wheel instruction?

Wyoming's driver education certification card must show 30 hours of classroom instruction and six hours of behind-the-wheel driving.

Q8. Seat belts work best because they:

Belts keep you behind the wheel and away from the windshield, and they position you correctly for the airbag.

Q9. You should never park:

Hydrant clearance is set by state law, commonly 10 to 15 feet. Blocked hydrants delay firefighters and are towed.

Q10. An anti-lock braking system is designed to:

ABS preserves steering control. On gravel or deep snow it may even lengthen the stop, but you can still steer around the hazard.
