Q1. A funeral procession with its lights on is passing through an intersection. You should:

Do not cut into or interrupt a procession, even if you have a green light, unless directed by an officer.

Q2. In Pennsylvania, after you move or change your name, you must notify PennDOT within:

The rule applies even when you are moving out of Pennsylvania.

Q3. You are in the left lane of a highway and a line of traffic builds up behind you. You should:

The left lane is for passing. Once traffic stacks up behind you, moving right is both safer and, in many states, required.

Q4. You arrive at an intersection where the traffic signal is completely dark because of a power failure. You should:

A dark signal is treated as an all-way stop. Stop, then take turns in the order vehicles arrived.

Q5. At night, pedestrians are hardest to see:

Most pedestrian deaths happen after dark. Lower your speed so your stopping distance fits inside your headlight range.

Q6. The minimum age for a Pennsylvania learner permit is:

The permit requires a vision screening and the knowledge test, plus parental consent for applicants under 18.

Q7. Black ice is most likely to form:

Bridges and shaded stretches lose heat fastest. If the road looks wet but spray has stopped coming off other cars, suspect ice.

Q8. Under Pennsylvania's Act 18, using a hand-held mobile device while driving is:

Pennsylvania moved to a hands-free standard for all drivers. Texting was already banned as a primary offense.

Q9. When parking along a curb in Pennsylvania, your vehicle may be no more than:

Parking as close as possible keeps your car out of the travel lane and your doors out of passing traffic.

Q10. Seat belts work best because they:

Belts keep you behind the wheel and away from the windshield, and they position you correctly for the airbag.
