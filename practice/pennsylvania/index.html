Q1. A school bus ahead has its yellow or amber flashing lights on. This means:

Amber lights come before red. Start slowing then, because the red lights and stop arm follow within seconds.

Q2. When entering a roundabout you must:

Traffic in the circle has the right of way. Enter when there is a safe gap, keep moving, and signal right as you leave.

Q3. In Pennsylvania, after you move or change your name, you must notify PennDOT within:

The rule applies even when you are moving out of Pennsylvania.

Q4. Liability insurance pays for:

Liability is the coverage states require. Collision and comprehensive, which cover your own vehicle, are optional.

Q5. Brown signs on a highway point to:

Brown guide signs mark recreational and cultural destinations such as parks, trailheads, campgrounds and historic sites.

Q6. Under Pennsylvania's Act 18, using a hand-held mobile device while driving is:

Pennsylvania moved to a hands-free standard for all drivers. Texting was already banned as a primary offense.

Q7. The minimum age for a Pennsylvania learner permit is:

The permit requires a vision screening and the knowledge test, plus parental consent for applicants under 18.

Q8. When parking along a curb in Pennsylvania, your vehicle may be no more than:

Parking as close as possible keeps your car out of the travel lane and your doors out of passing traffic.

Q9. Driving far below the speed limit when conditions are good:

Traffic that moves at very different speeds causes crashes. If you must go slow, stay right and let others pass.

Q10. When you park facing downhill on a road with no curb, turn your wheels:

With no curb to catch the tire, point the wheels so a rolling vehicle goes off the road rather than into traffic.
