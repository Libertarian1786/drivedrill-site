Q1. Before backing out of a parking space you should:

Cameras miss low objects and fast-moving children. Look behind the vehicle before you get in, then back slowly while checking all around.

Q2. A curb painted with diagonal yellow lines next to a wide striped area means:

Striped buffer areas separate traffic from hazards, medians or opposing lanes. Driving or parking in them is prohibited.

Q3. In Pennsylvania, after you move or change your name, you must notify PennDOT within:

The rule applies even when you are moving out of Pennsylvania.

Q4. The accelerator sticks open. You should:

Neutral disconnects the engine from the wheels so you keep power steering and brakes. Once stopped, shut the engine off.

Q5. You should never stop your vehicle:

Never start across tracks unless there is room for your whole vehicle on the far side. Trains overhang the rails by about three feet on each side.

Q6. Under Pennsylvania's Act 18, using a hand-held mobile device while driving is:

Pennsylvania moved to a hands-free standard for all drivers. Texting was already banned as a primary offense.

Q7. Refusing chemical testing after a DUI arrest in Pennsylvania results in:

The refusal suspension is imposed by PennDOT regardless of what happens in court, and a second refusal brings 18 months.

Q8. When parking along a curb in Pennsylvania, your vehicle may be no more than:

Parking as close as possible keeps your car out of the travel lane and your doors out of passing traffic.

Q9. Unless a sign prohibits it, a right turn on red is allowed:

Right on red requires a full stop first, then yielding to everyone already entitled to go. A NO TURN ON RED sign removes the option entirely.

Q10. The only thing that reduces blood alcohol concentration is:

The liver processes alcohol at a fixed rate. Nothing speeds it up, so the only plan that works is not driving.
