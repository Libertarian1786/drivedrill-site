Q1. An anti-lock braking system is designed to:

ABS preserves steering control. On gravel or deep snow it may even lengthen the stop, but you can still steer around the hazard.

Q2. Unless otherwise posted, the speed limit in a California residential or business district is:

25 mph is the default for residential and business districts, and it also applies in school zones when children are present unless posted lower.

Q3. Driving through a work zone at night, you should:

Lane patterns change day to day in a work zone, and workers may be close to traffic. Read the signs rather than relying on memory.

Q4. Refusing a chemical test after a lawful DUI arrest in California results in:

Refusal carries its own suspension under implied consent, separate from anything the court does, and the refusal can be used as evidence.

Q5. The basic speed law means:

The posted limit is the maximum for perfect conditions. In rain, fog, traffic or at night, the legal safe speed is lower.

Q6. The recommended hand position on the steering wheel is:

States teach 9 and 3, 8 and 4, or lower still. Each gives good control and keeps your arms out of the airbag's path; 10 and 2 puts them where it deploys.

Q7. In California, the speed limit at a blind intersection, in an alley, or within 100 feet of a railroad crossing where you cannot see the track is:

15 mph covers blind intersections, alleys and blind railroad crossings. A blind intersection is one where you cannot see 100 feet in each direction in the last 100 feet before crossing.

Q8. Combining alcohol with another drug usually:

Interactions are unpredictable and often far stronger than the sum of the parts.

Q9. Before backing out of a parking space you should:

Cameras miss low objects and fast-moving children. Look behind the vehicle before you get in, then back slowly while checking all around.

Q10. During the first 12 months with a California provisional license, a driver under 18 may not:

The first 12 months also restrict driving between 11 p.m. and 5 a.m. without a licensed adult 25 or older in the vehicle.
