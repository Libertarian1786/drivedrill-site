Q1. A sign showing two lanes separated by a median tells you:

Divided highway signs mark where traffic is separated by a median. The matching sign for the end of the divided section warns you that two-way traffic resumes.

Q2. In Vermont, unless a lower speed is posted, the maximum speed for any vehicle on a non-Interstate Vermont road is:

Vermont's manual sets the maximum speed for any vehicle on all Vermont roads at 50 mph or as posted, with a higher 65 mph maximum allowed on Interstates.

Q3. Counting seconds after the vehicle ahead passes a fixed point is a way to:

Pick a sign or pole; when the vehicle ahead passes it, start counting. If you reach it before your count is up, drop back. States set the minimum anywhere from two to four seconds in good conditions, and every state says to add time for rain, night and large vehicles.

Q4. In Vermont, if you are unable to safely change lanes away from a stopped emergency, tow, or repair vehicle with flashing lights, you must:

Vermont's manual says that if you are unable to change lanes away from a stopped vehicle with warning lights, you must slow down to 20 mph below the speed limit to go by it safely.

Q5. In Vermont, a driver who reaches a total of how many points on their driving record will be notified that their license is to be suspended?

Vermont's point system sends a suspension notice once a driver accumulates a total of 10 points on their driving record.

Q6. A bicycle on a public road is:

Riders must obey signals and signs, and drivers must yield to them the same way they would to another vehicle.

Q7. Worn tire tread is dangerous mainly because it:

Tread channels water away. Once it is shallow, wet grip falls off sharply. Check with a tread gauge or the built-in wear bars.

Q8. In Vermont, if an adult in the front seat is not wearing a seat belt:

This is a secondary enforcement state, so the belt alone is not grounds for the stop. That is a rule about enforcement, not about safety -- the crash risk is the same, and the ticket still follows any other stop.

Q9. A red circle with a slash through a symbol means:

The red circle and slash always prohibits whatever is pictured inside, such as no left turn, no U-turn, no trucks or no bicycles.

Q10. For drivers 21 and older, it is illegal to drive with a blood alcohol concentration at or above:

0.08% is the per-se limit for adult drivers in most of the country. You can still be arrested below it if your driving is impaired.
