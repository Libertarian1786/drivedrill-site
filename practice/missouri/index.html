Q1. Two lanes merge into one because of road work. The most efficient approach is to:

The zipper merge keeps queues shorter and speeds more even. Take turns at the merge point rather than fighting over it.

Q2. In Missouri, the law requires a car seat or booster seat until:

State law is a minimum. Safety experts recommend a booster until the adult belt fits properly: lap belt low across the hips and shoulder belt across the chest, not the neck.

Q3. Your vehicle stalls on railroad tracks and a train is coming. You should:

Leave the vehicle immediately and move toward the train at an angle, so flying debris from the impact travels away from you.

Q4. In Missouri, how far before making a turn or lane change should you signal?

Missouri's guide advises signaling at least 100 feet before you make your move so other drivers can react.

Q5. Counting seconds after the vehicle ahead passes a fixed point is a way to:

Pick a sign or pole; when the vehicle ahead passes it, start counting. If you reach it before your count is up, drop back. States set the minimum anywhere from two to four seconds in good conditions, and every state says to add time for rain, night and large vehicles.

Q6. After a crash with injury or significant damage you must usually:

Every state sets a damage threshold and a deadline for reporting. Exchange information at the scene and file the report on time.

Q7. In Missouri, a driver under 16 with an instruction permit may be accompanied by someone other than a parent or guardian only if that person is:

Missouri allows an instruction permit holder under 16 to be accompanied by a person who is at least 25 years old, has been licensed for a minimum of three years, and has received written permission from the parent or legal guardian.

Q8. The only thing that reduces blood alcohol concentration is:

The liver processes alcohol at a fixed rate. Nothing speeds it up, so the only plan that works is not driving.

Q9. Under Missouri law, which vehicle occupants are required to wear a seat belt?

Missouri law requires the operator and front seat occupants to wear a seat belt, and all passengers must be restrained when the driver holds an intermediate license.

Q10. A motorcycle is entitled to:

Never share a lane with a motorcycle. Riders move within the lane to avoid road hazards and to stay visible.
