Q1. An open container of alcohol in the passenger area of the vehicle is:

Open container laws apply to the whole passenger compartment. Keep sealed alcohol in the trunk or a locked area.

Q2. In Missouri, a newly licensed driver under 18 generally may not drive between:

Documented exceptions usually cover work, school activities, emergencies and driving with a licensed adult. Carry the paperwork if you rely on one.

Q3. You should never park:

Hydrant clearance is set by state law, commonly 10 to 15 feet. Blocked hydrants delay firefighters and are towed.

Q4. Under Missouri law, which vehicle occupants are required to wear a seat belt?

Missouri law requires the operator and front seat occupants to wear a seat belt, and all passengers must be restrained when the driver holds an intermediate license.

Q5. In Missouri, if you refuse to take a chemical test to determine your BAC, your driver license is taken away for:

Missouri's guide states that refusing a chemical test results in your driver license being taken away for one year.

Q6. An unsecured load in the back of a pickup truck is:

You are responsible for anything that falls off your vehicle. Tie loads down and cover loose material.

Q7. Driving far below the speed limit when conditions are good:

Traffic that moves at very different speeds causes crashes. If you must go slow, stay right and let others pass.

Q8. In Missouri, a driver under 16 with an instruction permit may be accompanied by someone other than a parent or guardian only if that person is:

Missouri allows an instruction permit holder under 16 to be accompanied by a person who is at least 25 years old, has been licensed for a minimum of three years, and has received written permission from the parent or legal guardian.

Q9. You are involved in a crash. You are required to:

Stopping, identifying yourself and rendering aid are legal duties everywhere. Leaving the scene turns a minor crash into a criminal charge.

Q10. Driving into low sun glare, the best response is to:

Glare hides brake lights and pedestrians. Slow down, clean the inside of your windshield, and leave more room.

Q11. In Missouri, unless otherwise posted, the speed limit in any city, town, or village is:

Missouri's guide sets the default speed limit in any city, town, or village at 25 mph unless posted otherwise.

Q12. The recommended hand position on the steering wheel is:

States teach 9 and 3, 8 and 4, or lower still. Each gives good control and keeps your arms out of the airbag's path; 10 and 2 puts them where it deploys.

Q13. Driving after using marijuana is:

Legal to possess does not mean legal to drive on. Impaired driving laws cover cannabis the same way they cover alcohol.

Q14. Under Missouri's hands-free driving law, which of the following is a driver still allowed to do?

Missouri's hands-free law bans physically holding a phone while driving but still allows placing or receiving voice calls using voice-operated or hands-free functions engaged with a single touch or swipe.

Q15. An anti-lock braking system is designed to:

ABS preserves steering control. On gravel or deep snow it may even lengthen the stop, but you can still steer around the hazard.

Q16. The best response to feeling drowsy while driving is to:

Drowsy driving impairs you much like alcohol, and micro-sleeps happen without warning. Only sleep fixes it.

Q17. A flashing yellow arrow at a left-turn lane means:

A flashing yellow arrow is an unprotected turn. Oncoming traffic has the green, so you may only go when there is a safe gap.

Q18. In Missouri, a driver may not move to the left side of the roadway to pass within how many feet of a bridge, viaduct, or tunnel where the view is obstructed?

Missouri law prohibits passing on the left when the view is obstructed upon approaching within 100 feet of a bridge, viaduct, or tunnel.

Q19. You are in the left lane of a highway and a line of traffic builds up behind you. You should:

The left lane is for passing. Once traffic stacks up behind you, moving right is both safer and, in many states, required.

Q20. Driving while your license is suspended usually results in:

It is one of the few traffic offenses that regularly carries jail, and it extends the original suspension.

Q21. In Missouri, how far before making a turn or lane change should you signal?

Missouri's guide advises signaling at least 100 feet before you make your move so other drivers can react.

Q22. You smell smoke and see flames under the hood. You should:

Do not open the hood; air feeds the fire. Get people at least 100 feet away and let the fire department handle it.

Q23. Texting while driving is dangerous mainly because:

Five seconds looking at a phone at 55 mph covers the length of a football field blind. Every state restricts phone use for new drivers, and most restrict it for everyone.

Q24. In Missouri, the law requires a car seat or booster seat until:

State law is a minimum. Safety experts recommend a booster until the adult belt fits properly: lap belt low across the hips and shoulder belt across the chest, not the neck.

Q25. In Missouri, under normal roadway conditions, you may not drive slower than what speed on an interstate highway?

Missouri's guide states that interstate highways have minimum speed limits and you may not drive slower than 40 mph under normal conditions.
