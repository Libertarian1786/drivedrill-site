Q1. You should never stop your vehicle:

Never start across tracks unless there is room for your whole vehicle on the far side. Trains overhang the rails by about three feet on each side.

Q2. Under Missouri law, a child younger than what age must ride in a child passenger restraint system (car seat), whatever the child weighs?

Children younger than 4, whatever their weight, must ride in a child restraint system that fits them. So must children under 40 pounds.

Q3. You smell smoke and see flames under the hood. You should:

Do not open the hood; air feeds the fire. Get people at least 100 feet away and let the fire department handle it.

Q4. Outside a metro area in Missouri, what number can you dial from a cell phone to report road rage or an emergency on the highway?

Pull off to a safe area and dial *55 (or 911 in a metropolitan area) to report road rage, a drunk driver or a highway emergency.

Q5. Missouri's four-part driver examination is made up of:

The four-part exam is a written test, a vision test, a road sign test and a driving skills test.

Q6. When you glance over your shoulder to check your blind spot, what should you avoid?

When you check your blind spot, turn only your head. Turning your whole body or the steering wheel can pull the car out of its lane.

Q7. Fluorescent yellow-green warning signs usually warn you about:

Fluorescent yellow-green is used for warning signs about schools, school buses, pedestrians and bicycles, so you notice where these people may be.

Q8. May a moped be ridden on a Missouri interstate highway?

Missouri law does not allow mopeds on any part of the federal interstate highway system.

Q9. On a highway, about how much longer does it take to pass a large truck than to pass a car?

A truck is much longer than a car, so passing it takes about three to five seconds more. Make sure you have that extra room before you start to pass.

Q10. Why should you check your mirrors more often while driving down a long, steep hill?

Check your mirrors on a long, steep hill because vehicles behind you often build up speed on the way down. Watch especially for large trucks and buses that may be going too fast.

Q11. In Missouri, how long is a driver's first point suspension?

Point suspensions last 30 days the first time, 60 days the second time and 90 days for a third or later one.

Q12. The basic speed law means:

The posted limit is the maximum for perfect conditions. In rain, fog, traffic or at night, the legal safe speed is lower.

Q13. An open container of alcohol in the passenger area of the vehicle is:

Open container laws apply to the whole passenger compartment. Keep sealed alcohol in the trunk or a locked area.

Q14. Missouri's minimum liability insurance is:

Missouri requires at least $25,000 per person and $50,000 per crash for bodily injury, and $25,000 per crash for property damage.

Q15. A conviction for driving under the influence usually results in:

Besides the suspension, fines and possible jail, a DUI stays on your driving record for years, and insurers usually raise premiums sharply.

Q16. A police officer pulls you over and walks up to your car. What should you do with your hands?

Stay in the car and keep both hands where the officer can see them, such as on top of the steering wheel. Do not reach for your license or anything else until the officer asks.

Q17. A large truck is backing up into a loading dock across your path. What should you do?

Truck drivers have a large blind spot behind the trailer and may not see you at all. Never cross behind a truck that is backing up; wait until it is done.

Q18. Outside a city in Missouri, you may pass on the right on a road that is clearly marked for:

Outside cities, you may pass on the right on unobstructed pavement clearly marked for four or more lanes, never by driving onto the shoulder.

Q19. You leave a brightly lit gas station and pull onto a dark road. What should you do?

Your eyes need time to adjust after bright light. Drive slowly until they do, so you can see the road and anything on it.

Q20. A vehicle is coming straight at you in your lane. What should you do?

If a vehicle comes at you in your lane, sound your horn and flash your lights, brake, and steer to the right. Do not swerve left, because the other driver may move back into their own lane.

Q21. When you tow another vehicle with a towline in Missouri, the vehicles must be no more than how far apart?

Your towline must keep the vehicles within 15 feet of each other, and both need lights on at night.

Q22. What is a reversible lane?

Reversible lanes carry traffic one way at some times and the other way at other times, usually to handle rush-hour traffic. Check the signs and lane-use signals before you enter one.

Q23. Your car is stuck in snow. What is the best way to try to free it?

Rock the car slowly forward and back without spinning the tires. Spinning the wheels only digs the car in deeper.

Q24. In Missouri, the standard minimum vision acuity required to pass the driver license vision test is:

Missouri requires a standard minimum acuity of at least 20/40 with either or both eyes to pass the vision test.

Q25. In Missouri, a moped has an engine no bigger than:

A moped has an automatic transmission and a motor of no more than 50 cubic centimeters. Riding one requires a valid driver license.
