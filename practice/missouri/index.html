Q1. In Missouri, the law requires a car seat or booster seat until:

State law is a minimum. Safety experts recommend a booster until the adult belt fits properly: lap belt low across the hips and shoulder belt across the chest, not the neck.

Q2. Driving after using marijuana is:

Legal to possess does not mean legal to drive on. Impaired driving laws cover cannabis the same way they cover alcohol.

Q3. In Missouri, how far before making a turn or lane change should you signal?

Missouri's guide advises signaling at least 100 feet before you make your move so other drivers can react.

Q4. You are driving through a residential street where children are playing. You should:

Children act on impulse and are hidden by parked cars. Cover the brake and cut your speed well below the limit.

Q5. In Missouri, a driver under 16 with an instruction permit may be accompanied by someone other than a parent or guardian only if that person is:

Missouri allows an instruction permit holder under 16 to be accompanied by a person who is at least 25 years old, has been licensed for a minimum of three years, and has received written permission from the parent or legal guardian.

Q6. Your brakes fail while driving. You should:

Pump to build pressure, downshift for engine braking, apply the parking brake gradually, and look for an open escape path or an uphill slope.

Q7. Your car stalls on a busy highway. You should:

Off the road, hazards on, and out of the traffic side of the car. Standing beside a disabled vehicle on the traffic side is one of the most dangerous places on a highway.

Q8. Under Missouri law, which vehicle occupants are required to wear a seat belt?

Missouri law requires the operator and front seat occupants to wear a seat belt, and all passengers must be restrained when the driver holds an intermediate license.

Q9. A driver is following you too closely. The safest response is to:

Increase your own space cushion and give them a chance to go by. Brake-checking turns their mistake into your crash.

Q10. The safest way to deal with a distracting situation inside the car is to:

Nothing inside the car is worth your attention at speed. Find a safe place to stop.
