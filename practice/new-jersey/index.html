Q1. A crash with the vehicle ahead is unavoidable. The safest choice is usually to:

Reduce speed as much as possible and choose a glancing impact with something soft over a head-on with something solid.

Q2. In New Jersey, the maximum posted speed limit on urban interstate highways is:

Urban interstates are held to a lower maximum than open rural highway, because of interchange spacing and traffic volume.

Q3. In New Jersey, a Basic license applicant must pass a vision screening showing at least:

New Jersey requires Basic license applicants to show at least 20/50 vision, with or without corrective lenses.

Q4. A driver is following you too closely. The safest response is to:

Increase your own space cushion and give them a chance to go by. Brake-checking turns their mistake into your crash.

Q5. In New Jersey, an applicant under 18 must log how much supervised driving before the road test?

A parent or guardian certifies the hours. Night driving is where new drivers crash most, which is why a share of the total has to be after dark.

Q6. You smell smoke and see flames under the hood. You should:

Do not open the hood; air feeds the fire. Get people at least 100 feet away and let the fire department handle it.

Q7. You may not pass another vehicle within a certain distance of the crest of a hill because:

A blind crest hides an oncoming vehicle until it is far too late to abort the pass.

Q8. Brown signs on a highway point to:

Brown is the color for recreational and cultural interest areas: parks, campgrounds, ski areas, museums and historic sites, for example. Blue, by contrast, marks motorist services.

Q9. Driving without the insurance your state requires can result in:

Financial responsibility is a condition of driving. The penalties apply whether or not you have a crash.

Q10. In New Jersey, can a GDL permit or probationary license holder use a hands-free cell phone while driving?

New Jersey bars GDL permit and probationary license holders from using any handheld or hands-free cellular telephone while driving; doing so violates GDL restrictions.
