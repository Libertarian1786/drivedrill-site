Q1. A yellow diamond sign showing a bicycle means:

The bicycle warning sign marks a place where riders cross or join traffic. Slow down, check your mirrors before turning, and give riders room.

Q2. In Michigan, a newly licensed driver under 18 generally may not drive between:

Documented exceptions usually cover work, school activities, emergencies and driving with a licensed adult. Carry the paperwork if you rely on one.

Q3. After you move, you must usually:

States set a deadline, often 10 to 30 days. Renewal notices and suspension notices go to the address on file.

Q4. In Michigan, when parking along a curb, your vehicle must not be parked:

Michigan law says the curb should never be more than a foot (12 inches) from your parked vehicle.

Q5. The most common cause of freeway merging crashes is:

Match the speed of traffic before you merge; joining at 40 mph into 65 mph traffic is what causes the problem.

Q6. In Michigan, the minimum age to apply for a learner permit is:

Applicants under 18 also need parental consent, and the permit must be held for a set period before the road test.

Q7. Your car stalls on a busy highway. You should:

Off the road, hazards on, and out of the traffic side of the car. Standing beside a disabled vehicle on the traffic side is one of the most dangerous places on a highway.

Q8. In Michigan, you may not park within how many feet of the nearest rail of a railroad crossing?

Michigan prohibits parking within 50 feet of the nearest rail of a railroad crossing.

Q9. You are involved in a crash. You are required to:

Stopping, identifying yourself and rendering aid are legal duties everywhere. Leaving the scene turns a minor crash into a criminal charge.

Q10. You come to a white square sign with a red circle reading DO NOT ENTER. You should:

DO NOT ENTER marks a road, ramp or lane you would be travelling the wrong way on. Find another route rather than entering at all.
