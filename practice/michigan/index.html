Q1. In Michigan, you may not park within how many feet of a stop sign, traffic light or flashing beacon?

Michigan prohibits parking within 30 feet of a stop sign, traffic light or flashing beacon.

Q2. In Michigan, you may not park within how many feet of a fire station driveway, on the same side of the street?

Michigan prohibits parking within 20 feet of a fire station driveway on the same side of the street.

Q3. The best response to feeling drowsy while driving is to:

Drowsy driving impairs you much like alcohol, and micro-sleeps happen without warning. Only sleep fixes it.

Q4. A crash with the vehicle ahead is unavoidable. The safest choice is usually to:

Reduce speed as much as possible and choose a glancing impact with something soft over a head-on with something solid.

Q5. In Michigan, a learner permit must be held for at least how long before the road test?

The holding period exists so new drivers practice across seasons and conditions rather than cramming a license in a weekend.

Q6. Driving on the shoulder to get around slow traffic is:

The shoulder is for emergencies and for stopped vehicles. Using it as a lane is both illegal and a common cause of crashes with people standing outside their cars.

Q7. In Michigan, during the restricted period, a young driver may carry:

Crash risk for a teen driver climbs sharply with each additional teen passenger, which is what this rule is built around.

Q8. The safest way to deal with a distracting situation inside the car is to:

Nothing inside the car is worth your attention at speed. Find a safe place to stop.

Q9. School zone speed limits usually apply:

Read the sign: many zones are time-based, others use flashing beacons or a when-children-are-present condition.

Q10. In many states, whenever your windshield wipers are on continuously you must also:

Wipers-on means lights-on in most states. It is about being seen by other drivers as much as your own vision.
