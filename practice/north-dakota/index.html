Q1. Hydroplaning happens when:

Hydroplaning starts with speed, water depth and worn tread. Ease off the accelerator, keep the wheel straight, and do not brake hard.

Q2. In North Dakota, an applicant under 18 must log how much supervised driving before the road test?

A parent or guardian certifies the hours. Night driving is where new drivers crash most, which is why a share of the total has to be after dark.

Q3. You should slow down before entering a curve because:

Slow in, steady through, accelerate gently out. Braking mid-corner shifts weight and can break traction.

Q4. In North Dakota, unless otherwise posted, the speed limit when passing a school during recess or during opening or closing hours is:

North Dakota sets a default 20 mph limit when passing a school during recess or while children are arriving or leaving, unless a different limit is posted.

Q5. A three-point turn should be used only:

The manoeuvre blocks the whole road for several seconds, so it needs a low-traffic street and a clear view both ways.

Q6. You should dim your high beams when:

High beams reflect off mirrors and blind the driver ahead as well as the one coming toward you. Dim for both.

Q7. In North Dakota, a new resident may operate a Class D vehicle on an out-of-state operator's license for:

A new North Dakota resident may drive a Class D vehicle on their out-of-state license for 60 days before they are required to obtain a North Dakota operator's license.

Q8. You notice one headlight is out at night. You should:

From a distance one light reads as a motorcycle, and oncoming drivers misjudge how much room you take. Fix it before driving at night again.

Q9. You arrive at an intersection where the traffic signal is completely dark because of a power failure. You should:

A dark signal is treated as an all-way stop. Stop, then take turns in the order vehicles arrived.

Q10. In North Dakota, using a hand-held cell phone while driving is:

There is no statewide hands-free law, so holding a phone to talk is not itself an offence. Texting is banned for all drivers, and new drivers face tighter limits.
