Q1. You notice one headlight is out at night. You should:

From a distance one light reads as a motorcycle, and oncoming drivers misjudge how much room you take. Fix it before driving at night again.

Q2. A green arrow pointing left means:

A green arrow is a protected turn. Still check the crosswalk for people before you move.

Q3. Parking is prohibited:

Never park in a crosswalk, on a sidewalk, in an intersection, or where you would block a curb ramp.

Q4. In Maine, if an adult in the front seat is not wearing a seat belt:

This is a primary enforcement state: no other violation is needed. The belt is also the single most effective thing in the car for surviving a crash.

Q5. Driving after using marijuana is:

Legal to possess does not mean legal to drive on. Impaired driving laws cover cannabis the same way they cover alcohol.

Q6. In Maine, the manual recommends keeping your vehicle how far behind the one in front under normal conditions?

Maine's manual recommends keeping at least a 4-second gap behind the vehicle ahead.

Q7. In Maine, demerit points on your driving record are erased when they:

Maine erases demerit points from a driver's record once they become one year old.

Q8. In Maine, a provisional license holder who is convicted of a moving violation while driving on that license faces a suspension of:

A moving violation conviction while holding a provisional license in Maine results in a 30-day suspension.

Q9. Your hood flies open and blocks your view. You should:

Use the gap under the hood or the side window, signal, slow gradually and stop off the road.

Q10. A red warning light appears on the dashboard while you are driving. You should:

Red generally means stop now: oil pressure, temperature, brakes or charging. Amber usually means service soon.
