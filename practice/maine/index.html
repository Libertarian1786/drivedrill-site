Q1. Before opening your door into traffic after parking, you should:

Dooring a cyclist is a common and serious crash. Check the mirror and over your shoulder, and open with the far hand so your body turns toward traffic.

Q2. Before driving on a frosty morning you should:

Driving with a peephole is a citation in most states, and snow left on the roof slides onto your windshield or the car behind you.

Q3. After passing another vehicle, you should return to your lane when:

Seeing both headlights of the vehicle you passed in your mirror means you have enough room to move back over without cutting the driver off.

Q4. In Maine, a driver under the age of 18 is specifically prohibited from:

Maine law specifically bars drivers under 18 from using a mobile telephone or handheld electronic device at all while driving, not just texting.

Q5. An anti-lock braking system is designed to:

ABS preserves steering control. On gravel or deep snow it may even lengthen the stop, but you can still steer around the hazard.

Q6. You must never pass another vehicle when:

You need to see far enough ahead to complete the pass and return safely. Hills, curves, intersections and railroad crossings take that view away.

Q7. Overdriving your headlights means:

High beams let you see about 350 feet ahead, and low beams much less. At highway speed you can outrun that, which is why night speeds should be lower.

Q8. In Maine, the manual recommends keeping your vehicle how far behind the one in front under normal conditions?

Maine's manual recommends keeping at least a 4-second gap behind the vehicle ahead.

Q9. In Maine, being convicted of speeding 30 mph or more over the posted limit can lead to a license suspension of:

Speeding 30 mph or more over the posted limit is one of the offenses that can carry a 30-day license suspension in Maine.

Q10. In Maine, demerit points on your driving record are erased when they:

Maine erases demerit points from a driver's record once they become one year old.

Q11. Driving without the insurance your state requires can result in:

Financial responsibility is a condition of driving. The penalties apply whether or not you have a crash.

Q12. In Maine, during the restricted period, a young driver may carry:

Crash risk for a teen driver climbs sharply with each additional teen passenger, which is what this rule is built around.

Q13. In Maine, refusing a chemical test after being lawfully asked to take one results in an immediate license suspension of up to:

Refusing a lawfully requested chemical test in Maine leads to an immediate administrative suspension of up to six years.

Q14. In Maine, a vehicle's horn must be loud enough to be heard from:

Maine requires a horn loud enough to be heard from 200 feet away.

Q15. A U-turn should never be made:

You need to be visible for the whole maneuver. Blind curves and crests hide you from traffic that arrives at speed.

Q16. Liability insurance pays for:

Liability is the coverage states require. Collision and comprehensive, which cover your own vehicle, are optional.

Q17. In Maine, if an adult in the front seat is not wearing a seat belt:

This is a primary enforcement state: no other violation is needed. The belt is also the single most effective thing in the car for surviving a crash.

Q18. In Maine, a provisional license holder who is convicted of a moving violation while driving on that license faces a suspension of:

A moving violation conviction while holding a provisional license in Maine results in a 30-day suspension.

Q19. On a long downgrade you should:

Continuous braking overheats the brakes and they fade. Let the engine hold the vehicle back and brake in short firm applications.

Q20. Where a state uses a point system, it is used to:

Points accumulate with each conviction and are removed over time. Reaching the threshold brings a suspension. A few states, such as Minnesota, act on specific violations instead of points.

Q21. In Maine, the first license issued to a new applicant under 21 years of age is provisional for:

A first license issued to an applicant under 21 in Maine is provisional for two years, compared with one year for older applicants.

Q22. The pedestrian signal shows a flashing orange hand with a countdown while you wait to turn. It means:

The countdown is for people already crossing. Wait until the crosswalk is clear before completing your turn.

Q23. A bicycle on a public road is:

Riders must obey signals and signs, and drivers must yield to them the same way they would to another vehicle.

Q24. In heavy fog you should drive with:

High beams reflect off fog and blind you. Use low beams or fog lights, slow down, and pull well off the road if you cannot see.

Q25. The only thing that reduces blood alcohol concentration is:

The liver processes alcohol at a fixed rate. Nothing speeds it up, so the only plan that works is not driving.
