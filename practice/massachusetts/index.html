Q1. In Massachusetts, an applicant under 18 must log how much supervised driving before the road test?

A parent or guardian certifies the hours.

Q2. Your vehicle has four-wheel drive. How should you drive on an icy road?

Four-wheel drive does not make ice safe, because the vehicle can still skid and slide, so drive slowly and carefully. It can give a false sense of security.

Q3. A Massachusetts driver age 18 to 21 fails a chemical test. How long is the license suspended?

A driver 18 to 21 who fails the test is suspended for 30 days plus another 180 days. For a first case, the 180 days can be waived by entering an approved alcohol education program.

Q4. In Massachusetts, it is illegal to drive by or park within how many feet of a fire?

It is illegal to drive by or park within 800 feet of a fire.

Q5. Driving into low sun glare, the best response is to:

Glare hides brake lights and pedestrians. Slow down, clean the inside of your windshield, and leave more room.

Q6. In Massachusetts, the fine for a first offense of parking in a disability (HP-DV) space without plates or a placard is:

Parking in an HP-DV space without disability plates or a placard costs $300 for a first offense. Wrongful use of a placard costs at least $500.

Q7. On a long trip, how often should you stop to take a break?

On long trips, stop at least every two hours or every 100 miles to rest and stretch. Do not wait until you feel drowsy.

Q8. When you parallel park in a Massachusetts business or residential district, your vehicle may be no more than how far from the curb?

In a business or residential district, a parked vehicle must be no more than 12 inches from the curb, unless angled parking is allowed.

Q9. At a railroad crossing, the gate goes up, but the red lights are still flashing. What should you do?

Do not cross the tracks until the red lights stop flashing, even if the gate rises. Another train may be coming.

Q10. You start driving on a snow-covered road. How can you find out how much traction your tires have?

Start out slowly, then test your brakes gently once the car is moving to feel how much traction you have. A gentle test warns you before you need to stop.

Q11. A snowplow is working on the road ahead of you. What is the best choice?

Stay well back from a working snowplow, because it throws sand and snow and may stop or back up. Never pass it on the shoulder side.

Q12. A Massachusetts driver over 21 with one prior OUI offense refuses a chemical test. The license is suspended for:

For a driver over 21 with one prior OUI offense, a refusal brings a 3-year suspension.

Q13. Good drivers look:

Looking far ahead gives you time to spot brake lights, debris and merging traffic while you still have choices.

Q14. You may park in a space marked with the International Symbol of Access:

Accessible spaces need a valid placard or plate, and the person it belongs to must be involved in the trip. Fines are steep and vehicles are towed.

Q15. A Massachusetts driver over 21 with no prior OUI offenses refuses a breath or blood test. The license is suspended for:

For a driver over 21 with no prior OUI offenses, refusing the chemical test brings a 180-day suspension.

Q16. A Massachusetts Junior Operator is found responsible for a first speeding offense. The license is suspended for:

A Junior Operator's first speeding offense brings a 90-day suspension. A later speeding offense brings a 1-year suspension.

Q17. You are waiting to turn left, and an oncoming car has its right turn signal on. When should you start your turn in front of it?

A driver may have left a signal on by mistake or may change their mind. Do not assume the car will turn; wait until it actually starts its turn before you turn left.

Q18. In Massachusetts, a family sedan with a SCHOOL BUS sign and flashing red lights stops to let a child out. What must you do?

School pupil transport vehicles, such as vans, station wagons and sedans with flashing red lights and SCHOOL BUS signs, must be stopped for like a school bus.

Q19. Strong, gusty winds are blowing across the highway. What should you do about cruise control?

In high winds, do not use cruise control. Keep your own foot on the accelerator so you can react right away when a gust hits.

Q20. When a train is coming and you must stop at a railroad crossing, stop no closer to the nearest rail than:

Fifteen feet keeps you clear of the train's overhang and of the gate arm. Stopping no farther than 50 feet back also lets you still see the tracks.

Q21. A police officer pulls you over at night. What should you do after you stop?

At night, turn on the inside light so the officer can see into your car. Stay inside with your hands in view unless you are told to get out.

Q22. Where should a child in a rear-facing car seat never ride?

An inflating front airbag can strike a rear-facing car seat and seriously injure or kill the child. A rear-facing seat belongs in the back seat.

Q23. A 19-year-old in Massachusetts tries to buy alcohol at a store. What happens to the license?

If you are under 21 and buy, or try to buy, alcohol, your license will be suspended for 180 days, whether or not you were driving.

Q24. After you move, you must usually:

Each state's law sets a short deadline. Renewal notices and suspension notices go to the address on file.

Q25. A driver holds their left arm out the window, bent at the elbow and pointing down. What does that mean?

When a driver's left arm points down from the window, the driver is slowing down or stopping.
