Q1. For drivers 21 and older, it is illegal to drive with a blood alcohol concentration at or above:

0.08% is the per-se limit for adult drivers in most of the country. You can still be arrested below it if your driving is impaired.

Q2. In Massachusetts, when driving behind heavy equipment such as dump trucks or tractors, you should keep a following distance of at least:

Massachusetts recommends keeping at least ten seconds of following distance between your vehicle and heavy equipment like dump trucks or tractors.

Q3. In Massachusetts, an applicant under 18 must log how much supervised driving before the road test?

A parent or guardian certifies the hours.

Q4. Switching on your hazard lights while parked illegally:

Hazard lights warn other drivers; they do not create a parking permit.

Q5. In Massachusetts, if an adult in the front seat is not wearing a seat belt:

This is a secondary enforcement state, so the belt alone is not grounds for the stop. That is a rule about enforcement, not about safety -- the crash risk is the same, and the ticket still follows any other stop.

Q6. In Massachusetts, it is illegal to follow closer than what distance behind an emergency vehicle that is responding to an alarm with its lights and siren on:

Massachusetts law makes it illegal to follow closer than 300 feet behind a responding emergency vehicle.

Q7. In Massachusetts, when following a motorcycle, you should use a following distance of at least:

Massachusetts recommends a four-second following distance behind a motorcycle, more than the three-second rule used for other motor vehicles.

Q8. The only thing that reduces blood alcohol concentration is:

The liver processes alcohol at a fixed rate. Nothing speeds it up, so the only plan that works is not driving.

Q9. Implied consent means that by driving in the state you have agreed to:

Refusing the test carries its own suspension, usually longer than the one for failing it, and the refusal can be used against you.

Q10. You arrive at an intersection where the traffic signal is completely dark because of a power failure. You should:

A dark signal is treated as an all-way stop. Stop, then take turns in the order vehicles arrived.

Q11. In Massachusetts, a newly licensed driver under 18 generally may not drive between:

Documented exceptions usually cover work, school activities, emergencies and driving with a licensed adult. Carry the paperwork if you rely on one.

Q12. Driving on the shoulder to get around slow traffic is:

The shoulder is for emergencies and for stopped vehicles. Using it as a lane is both illegal and a common cause of crashes with people standing outside their cars.

Q13. A prescription medicine that makes you drowsy:

Impaired driving laws cover any drug, prescribed or not. Read the label and do not drive when it warns you about machinery.

Q14. In Massachusetts, a driver under 18 may use a mobile electronic device while driving only to:

Drivers under 18 in Massachusetts cannot use any mobile electronic device for any reason while driving, except to report an emergency.

Q15. A bicycle on a public road is:

Riders must obey signals and signs, and drivers must yield to them the same way they would to another vehicle.

Q16. The recommended hand position on the steering wheel is:

States teach 9 and 3, 8 and 4, or lower still. Each gives good control and keeps your arms out of the airbag's path; 10 and 2 puts them where it deploys.

Q17. In Massachusetts, when passing a vulnerable road user such as a pedestrian, bicyclist, or person using a wheelchair, you must leave a passing distance of at least:

Massachusetts law requires drivers to leave a safe passing distance of at least four feet between their vehicle and a vulnerable road user.

Q18. A flashing yellow traffic light means:

Flashing yellow means proceed with care. You do not have to stop, but you must slow down and watch for cross traffic and pedestrians.

Q19. Your brakes fail while driving. You should:

Pump to build pressure, downshift for engine braking, apply the parking brake gradually, and look for an open escape path or an uphill slope.

Q20. Hazard lights should generally be used:

In most states hazard lights while driving normally are prohibited because they hide your turn signals.

Q21. In Massachusetts, using a hand-held cell phone while driving is:

The state requires hands-free operation for every driver. Novice drivers are usually barred from using a phone at all, even hands-free.

Q22. Driving through a work zone at night, you should:

Lane patterns change day to day in a work zone, and workers may be close to traffic. Read the signs rather than relying on memory.

Q23. You should never stop your vehicle:

Never start across tracks unless there is room for your whole vehicle on the far side. Trains overhang the rails by about three feet on each side.

Q24. In Massachusetts, a learner permit must be held for at least how long before the road test?

The holding period exists so new drivers practice across seasons and conditions rather than cramming a license in a weekend.

Q25. Overdriving your headlights means:

High beams let you see about 350 feet ahead, and low beams much less. At highway speed you can outrun that, which is why night speeds should be lower.
