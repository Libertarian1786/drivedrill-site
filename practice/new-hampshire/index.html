Q1. In New Hampshire, during the restricted period, a young driver may carry:

Crash risk for a teen driver climbs sharply with each additional teen passenger, which is what this rule is built around.

Q2. In New Hampshire, a posted school zone speed limit is set at:

New Hampshire's posted school zones reduce the usual speed limit by 10 mph, in effect from 45 minutes before school opens until opening, and from closing until 45 minutes after closing.

Q3. Grooves cut into the shoulder that make a loud noise when you drive over them are there to:

Rumble strips are a drowsiness and drift warning. If you hit one, ease back into the lane smoothly and consider stopping to rest.

Q4. A yellow sign showing a red octagon means:

STOP AHEAD gives you distance to slow down for a stop sign you cannot see yet, often around a curve or over a crest.

Q5. A sign showing two lanes separated by a median tells you:

Divided highway signs mark where traffic is separated by a median. The matching sign for the end of the divided section warns you that two-way traffic resumes.

Q6. In New Hampshire, how far in advance should a driver signal before turning, and before changing lanes on a highway?

The New Hampshire manual directs drivers to signal at least 100 feet before a turn, and at least 500 feet before changing lanes or exiting on the highway, so other drivers have time to react.

Q7. In New Hampshire, how close may you approach a school bus that is stopped with its red lights flashing and stop arm extended, on an undivided road?

New Hampshire requires drivers to stop at least 25 feet away in any direction from a school bus stopped with its red lights flashing and stop arm extended, unless the roadway is separated by a physical barrier.

Q8. You are on a road that ends at a T intersection with no signs. You should:

At a T intersection the road that ends yields to the road that continues.

Q9. An unsecured load in the back of a pickup truck is:

You are responsible for anything that falls off your vehicle. Tie loads down and cover loose material.

Q10. On a multi-lane highway, slower traffic should use:

Keep right except to pass. Blocking the left lane forces other drivers to pass on the right, which is where crashes happen.
