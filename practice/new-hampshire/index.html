Q1. In New Hampshire, during the restricted period, a young driver may carry:

Crash risk for a teen driver climbs sharply with each additional teen passenger, which is what this rule is built around.

Q2. In New Hampshire, a posted school zone speed limit is set at:

New Hampshire's posted school zones reduce the usual speed limit by 10 mph, in effect from 45 minutes before school opens until opening, and from closing until 45 minutes after closing.

Q3. In New Hampshire, how close may you approach a school bus that is stopped with its red lights flashing and stop arm extended, on an undivided road?

New Hampshire requires drivers to stop at least 25 feet away in any direction from a school bus stopped with its red lights flashing and stop arm extended, unless the roadway is separated by a physical barrier.

Q4. You may park in a space marked with the International Symbol of Access:

Accessible spaces need a valid placard or plate, and the person it belongs to must be involved in the trip. Fines are steep and vehicles are towed.

Q5. Compared with a car, a loaded truck needs:

Weight means distance. Never cut in front of a truck and then brake; the driver may have no room left to stop.

Q6. In New Hampshire, how far in advance should a driver signal before turning, and before changing lanes on a highway?

The New Hampshire manual directs drivers to signal at least 100 feet before a turn, and at least 500 feet before changing lanes or exiting on the highway, so other drivers have time to react.

Q7. A flashing red traffic light means:

Treat a flashing red exactly like a stop sign: full stop, yield to traffic and pedestrians, then proceed when the way is clear.

Q8. A curb painted with diagonal yellow lines next to a wide striped area means:

Striped buffer areas separate traffic from hazards, medians or opposing lanes. Driving or parking in them is prohibited.

Q9. A flagger in a work zone holds up a sign reading SLOW. You should:

Flaggers have the same authority as signs and signals in a work zone. Follow their direction and watch for workers on foot.

Q10. Driving after using marijuana is:

Legal to possess does not mean legal to drive on. Impaired driving laws cover cannabis the same way they cover alcohol.
