Free Minnesota permit practice test: 25 questions

25 sample questions from DriveDrill's 511-question Minnesota bank. Pick an answer to see if you got it right, and why.

Q1. Combining alcohol with another drug usually:

Interactions are unpredictable and often far stronger than the sum of the parts.

Q2. In Minnesota, you want to turn right across a bicycle lane. Once the bike lane is clear and you have signaled, what should you do?

Yield to approaching bicyclists. When the bike lane is clear, signal and then move into the bicycle lane before making the turn.

Q3. You are stranded in a snowstorm and run the engine for heat. What should you check to avoid carbon monoxide poisoning?

Keep the tailpipe clear of snow whenever the engine is running, because a blocked pipe can send carbon monoxide into the car. Run the engine only for short periods, with a window open slightly.

Q4. You may park in a space marked with the International Symbol of Access:

Accessible spaces need a valid placard or plate, and the person it belongs to must be involved in the trip. Fines are steep and vehicles are towed.

Q5. You are stranded in a snowstorm. What helps rescuers find your vehicle?

Turn on your hazard lights and tie a bright flag or cloth to the antenna or door handle so rescuers can spot the vehicle. Stay inside and out of traffic.

Q6. You leave a brightly lit gas station and pull onto a dark road. What should you do?

Your eyes need time to adjust after bright light. Drive slowly until they do, so you can see the road and anything on it.

Q7. What should you know about wearing sunglasses or tinted lenses at night?

Avoid sunglasses and dark or tinted lenses at night, because they cut down the light you need to see clearly. This holds even if you think they help with glare.

Q8. The first ability alcohol affects in a driver is:

Judgment goes first, which is why an impaired driver often believes they are fine. Reaction time, vision and coordination follow.

Q9. At a Minnesota freeway ramp meter, how many cars may go each time the light turns green?

Ramp meters let one car go each time the light is green. Stop when it is red and take turns entering the freeway.

Q10. In Minnesota, after a crash, the officer asks for your insurance information but you do not have it with you. When must you provide it?

Give your insurance company's name and policy number to the investigating officer. If you do not have them with you, provide them within 72 hours.

Q11. In Minnesota, what insurance must the owner of a vehicle driven on public roads carry?

Minnesota vehicle owners must carry no-fault and liability insurance and keep valid proof of insurance in the vehicle at all times.

Q12. In Minnesota, where may you keep an opened package of cannabis or hemp products in a vehicle?

Keep opened cannabis or hemp products in the trunk, or where there is no trunk, in an area not normally occupied by the driver and passengers. Not in the glove or utility compartment.

Q13. You are stopped, waiting for a gap in oncoming traffic so you can turn left. How should you keep your front wheels?

Keep your wheels straight until it is safe to start your turn. If you are hit from behind while your wheels are turned left, you could be pushed into oncoming traffic.

Q14. In Minnesota, you approach an intersection faster than the legal speed. What happens to your right-of-way?

If you approach an intersection at an unlawful speed, you lose the right-of-way you would have had at a lawful speed.

Q15. During the Minnesota road test, who may ride in the car besides you and the examiner?

No passengers are allowed during the road test, including pets, children or interpreters.

Q16. In Minnesota, you may not park within how many feet of the nearest rail of a railroad crossing?

Minnesota bans parking within 50 feet of the nearest rail of a railroad crossing.

Q17. At a two-lane roundabout with no signs showing otherwise, you plan to take the first exit to the right. Which lane should you use?

To turn right at a multi-lane roundabout, enter in the right lane and exit from the right lane. Signs and lane arrows before the roundabout show which lane goes where.

Q18. You should switch on your headlights:

Headlights are about being seen as much as seeing. The law often also requires them in rain, fog or snow, or whenever the wipers are on.

Q19. You are inside a roundabout when an ambulance with its siren on comes up behind you. What should you do?

Do not stop inside a roundabout for an emergency vehicle. Continue to your exit or the next one, leave the roundabout, and then pull over to the right and stop.

Q20. On a road with no sidewalk, where should people walk?

Where there is no sidewalk, pedestrians should walk on the left side or shoulder, facing traffic, so they can see what is coming. Drivers should expect people walking there, especially at night.

Q21. In Minnesota, when may you drive in a bicycle lane?

It is illegal to drive in a bicycle lane except to park where allowed, to enter or leave the road, or to prepare for a turn.

Q22. You stop at a red light far behind the stop line. Why can that be a problem at some signals?

Many signals use sensors in the pavement near the stop line to detect waiting cars. If you stop too far back, the signal may not know you are there.

Q23. Your brakes fail while driving. You should:

Pump the pedal to build pressure. If that does not work, apply the parking brake gradually and shift to lower gears for engine braking, and look for an open escape path or an uphill slope.

Q24. In Minnesota, for safety around air bags, children under what age must ride in the back seat if one is available?

Minnesota's manual says children under age 13 must ride in the back seat if one is available, since airbags are not designed to protect young children in the front.

Q25. You should never stop your vehicle:

Never start across tracks unless there is room for your whole vehicle on the far side. Trains overhang the rails by about three feet on each side.
