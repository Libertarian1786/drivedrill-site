Q1. Absent a posted sign, District of Columbia law caps a school zone's speed at:

DC's manual sets the unposted school zone speed limit at 15 miles per hour.

Q2. Driving into low sun glare, the best response is to:

Glare hides brake lights and pedestrians. Slow down, clean the inside of your windshield, and leave more room.

Q3. A flashing red traffic light means:

Treat a flashing red exactly like a stop sign: full stop, yield to traffic and pedestrians, then proceed when the way is clear.

Q4. Your vehicle registration must be:

Expired registration is an easy stop for an officer and, in many states, grounds for towing.

Q5. In the District of Columbia, when a school vehicle has stopped with its alternating flashing red lights on, you must stop:

DC's manual requires all vehicles to stop at least 20 feet from the front or rear of a stopped school vehicle operating its alternating flashing red lights.

Q6. A flashing yellow arrow at a left-turn lane means:

A flashing yellow arrow is an unprotected turn. Oncoming traffic has the green, so you may only go when there is a safe gap.

Q7. In the District of Columbia, before turning you must signal continuously for at least:

DC law requires a turn signal, arm, or hand signal continuously for at least 100 feet before turning.

Q8. After passing another vehicle, you should return to your lane when:

Seeing both headlights of the vehicle you passed in your mirror means you have enough room to move back over without cutting the driver off.

Q9. The recommended hand position on the steering wheel is:

States teach 9 and 3, 8 and 4, or lower still. Each gives good control and keeps your arms out of the airbag's path; 10 and 2 puts them where it deploys.

Q10. A flashing yellow traffic light means:

Flashing yellow means proceed with care. You do not have to stop, but you must slow down and watch for cross traffic and pedestrians.

Q11. In the District of Columbia, an applicant under 18 must log how much supervised driving before the road test?

A parent or guardian certifies the hours.

Q12. A funeral procession with its lights on is passing through an intersection. You should:

Do not cut into or interrupt a procession, even if you have a green light, unless directed by an officer.

Q13. In heavy fog you should drive with:

High beams reflect off fog and blind you. Use low beams or fog lights, slow down, and pull well off the road if you cannot see.

Q14. Worn tire tread is dangerous mainly because it:

Tread channels water away. Once it is shallow, wet grip falls off sharply. Check with a tread gauge or the built-in wear bars.

Q15. In the District of Columbia, before advancing from a provisional license to a full driver license, an applicant must complete:

DC's manual requires certifying at least 10 hours of night driving experience, accompanied by a licensed driver 21 or older, before advancing to a full driver license.

Q16. In the District of Columbia, the maximum posted speed limit on urban interstate highways is:

Urban interstates are held to a lower maximum than open rural highway, because of interchange spacing and traffic volume.

Q17. You come up behind slow-moving farm equipment displaying an orange triangle. You should:

The orange triangle marks a vehicle traveling under 25 mph. Closing speed is higher than it looks, so slow early and pass only with a long clear view.

Q18. In the District of Columbia, if no sign is posted, an alley carries a maximum speed of:

DC's manual sets the unposted alley speed limit at 15 miles per hour.

Q19. In the District of Columbia, using a hand-held cell phone while driving is:

The state requires hands-free operation for every driver. Novice drivers are usually barred from using a phone at all, even hands-free.

Q20. In the District of Columbia, a driver under 21 is over the limit with:

This is a true zero tolerance state: any measurable alcohol is enough for a suspension, and mouthwash or cough syrup can reach it.

Q21. Driving while your license is suspended usually results in:

It is one of the few traffic offenses that regularly carries jail, and it extends the original suspension.

Q22. You arrive at an intersection where the traffic signal is completely dark because of a power failure. You should:

A dark signal is treated as an all-way stop. Stop, then take turns in the order vehicles arrived.

Q23. In the District of Columbia, the minimum age to apply for a learner permit is:

Applicants under 18 also need parental consent, and the permit must be held for a set period before the road test.

Q24. Unless a sign prohibits it, a right turn on red is allowed:

Right on red requires a full stop first, then yielding to everyone already entitled to go. A NO TURN ON RED sign removes the option entirely.

Q25. Combining alcohol with another drug usually:

Interactions are unpredictable and often far stronger than the sum of the parts.
