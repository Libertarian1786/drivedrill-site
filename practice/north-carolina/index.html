Q1. When the lights at a railroad crossing start to flash, about how much warning do you usually have before the train arrives?

Crossing signals are set to give at least about 20 seconds of warning before the train reaches the crossing. When the lights start flashing, stop; there is not enough time to try to beat the train.

Q2. Where must your North Carolina vehicle registration card be kept?

The registration card must be kept in the vehicle at all times and shown to an officer on request.

Q3. You are being passed on a North Carolina two-lane road, and the passing driver honks. What should you do?

If the passing driver blows the horn, move to the right edge of the road, and never increase your speed.

Q4. A car moves into the space in front of you and is much too close. What should you do first?

If a vehicle merges in front of you too closely, ease off the accelerator to rebuild your following distance. Braking hard could cause the driver behind you to hit you.

Q5. You are stranded in your car in a snowstorm and no help is in sight. What should you do?

Stay with your vehicle, because most deaths in these situations happen when people leave their cars, get lost and are exposed to the cold. The car is shelter and is easier for rescuers to find.

Q6. Who must wear a motorcycle safety helmet in North Carolina?

All operators and passengers on motorcycles and mopeds must wear a safety helmet that meets federal standard FMVSS 218.

Q7. On a slippery road you turn the steering wheel, but the car keeps going straight ahead. The front tires have lost their grip. What should you do?

If the car keeps going straight while you steer, the front tires have lost grip, so ease off the gas and wait for traction to return before you steer more. Turning the wheel harder only keeps the front tires sliding.

Q8. In North Carolina, accumulating this many driver license points within a three-year period can lead to your license being suspended:

North Carolina may suspend a driver's license once 12 points accumulate within a three-year period.

Q9. A left turn on red from a one-way street onto another one-way street is:

Where the law allows this turn, you must first stop completely and yield. Some states and cities ban it, so learn your state's rule and obey any posted sign.

Q10. In North Carolina, tinted windows must let through at least what share of light?

The allowable light transmittance for tinted windows is no less than 35%.

Q11. A motorcyclist has been hurt in a crash. What should you do about the rider's helmet?

Do not remove an injured rider's helmet. If the neck or spine is hurt, moving the head can cause more damage, so wait for trained help.

Q12. In North Carolina, failing to properly restrain a child in a car seat or seat belt adds:

The NC point table lists 2 points for failing to properly restrain a child in a child restraint or seat belt.

Q13. In North Carolina, what does signing a traffic ticket mean?

The NC handbook says accepting and signing a traffic ticket is not an admission of guilt.

Q14. Which Level One and Level Two limits still apply to a North Carolina Full Provisional (Level Three) license?

The time-of-driving, supervision and passenger limits of Levels One and Two do not apply to a Full Provisional License.

Q15. You approach a hill or curve that you cannot see over or around. How fast should you be going?

Drive slowly enough that you could stop if a stalled vehicle is just over the hill or around the curve. Even a road you have driven many times can hide a problem.

Q16. Driving while your license is suspended usually results in:

It is one of the few traffic offenses that regularly carries jail, and it extends the original suspension.

Q17. A deer suddenly steps into the road ahead of you. What is usually the safest choice?

Swerving to miss an animal can send you into oncoming traffic or off the road, which is often worse than hitting it. Brake firmly, stay in control and keep your lane.

Q18. In North Carolina, the minimum age to apply for a learner permit is:

Applicants under 18 also need parental consent, and the permit must be held for a set period before the road test.

Q19. A North Carolina student under 18 is suspended from school for more than 10 days in a row for certain discipline. The license can be suspended for:

A student who loses eligibility because of school discipline of more than 10 consecutive days can lose the license; that suspension stays in effect for 12 months.

Q20. The water covering a road ahead looks shallow. What is true about it?

Floodwater can hide dips and wash away the road surface beneath it, so you cannot judge safety by how shallow it looks. Turn around rather than try to cross.

Q21. You must never pass another vehicle when:

You need to see far enough ahead to complete the pass and return safely. Hills, curves, intersections and railroad crossings take that view away.

Q22. Before you turn or change lanes, you should signal:

Signal early and continuously. A signal given as you are already turning tells other drivers nothing they can use.

Q23. You come to a flooded road, and you know a different way to get where you are going. What should you do?

Turn around and take another route when a road ahead is flooded. You cannot tell how deep the water is or whether the road is still there under it, and a vehicle can be swept away.

Q24. A large truck is backing up into a loading dock across your path. What should you do?

Truck drivers have a large blind spot behind the trailer and may not see you at all. Never cross behind a truck that is backing up; wait until it is done.

Q25. What are the striped areas next to disabled parking spaces for?

The striped access aisle beside a disabled parking space gives room for wheelchairs, ramps and lifts. Parking there is not allowed, even with a disability placard.
