Q1. In North Carolina, the maximum speed limit on rural interstate highways is:

That is the highest limit the state allows on a rural interstate. The posted sign always governs, and a lower speed can still be required by conditions.

Q2. In North Carolina, a tractor-trailer's blind spot, or No-Zone, can extend behind the truck for up to:

Truckers have serious blind spots, or No-Zones, that reach up to 200 feet behind a tractor-trailer, so lingering there makes you invisible to the driver.

Q3. A prescription medicine that makes you drowsy:

Impaired driving laws cover any drug, prescribed or not. Read the label and do not drive when it warns you about machinery.

Q4. Counting seconds after the vehicle ahead passes a fixed point is a way to:

Pick a sign or pole; when the vehicle ahead passes it, start counting. If you reach it before your count is up, drop back. States set the minimum anywhere from two to four seconds in good conditions, and every state says to add time for rain, night and large vehicles.

Q5. In North Carolina, an applicant under 18 must log how much supervised driving before the road test?

A parent or guardian certifies the hours. Night driving is where new drivers crash most, which is why a share of the total has to be after dark.

Q6. You should dim your high beams when:

High beams reflect off mirrors and blind the driver ahead as well as the one coming toward you. Dim for both.

Q7. In North Carolina, the handbook's rule of thumb for following distance behind the vehicle ahead is called:

North Carolina's handbook teaches drivers to allow two seconds between the time the vehicle ahead passes a point and the time their own vehicle reaches that same point.

Q8. You must never pass another vehicle when:

You need to see far enough ahead to complete the pass and return safely. Hills, curves, intersections and railroad crossings take that view away.

Q9. You arrive at an intersection where the traffic signal is completely dark because of a power failure. You should:

A dark signal is treated as an all-way stop. Stop, then take turns in the order vehicles arrived.

Q10. You are driving through a residential street where children are playing. You should:

Children act on impulse and are hidden by parked cars. Cover the brake and cut your speed well below the limit.
