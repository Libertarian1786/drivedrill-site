Q4. In North Carolina, an applicant under 18 must log how much supervised driving before the road test?

A parent or guardian certifies the hours. Night driving is where new drivers crash most, which is why a share of the total has to be after dark.

Q5. In North Carolina, a one-year driver license revocation applies to a person convicted of:

North Carolina requires a one-year license revocation for alcohol-related violations involving underage people, including giving alcohol to someone underage.

Q6. Speeding fines in a work zone are usually:

Most states increase work-zone fines and add penalties when workers are present, whether or not the crew is visible at that moment.

Q7. In North Carolina, a tractor-trailer's blind spot, or No-Zone, can extend behind the truck for up to:

Truckers have serious blind spots, or No-Zones, that reach up to 200 feet behind a tractor-trailer, so lingering there makes you invisible to the driver.

Q8. Worn tire tread is dangerous mainly because it:

Tread channels water away. Once it is shallow, wet grip falls off sharply. Check with a tread gauge or the built-in wear bars.

Q9. A U-turn should never be made:

Q10. Adjusting your side mirrors slightly outward compared with the usual setting:

Wider mirror angles shrink the blind spot, but a head check before changing lanes is still the move that catches what mirrors miss.
