Q1. You are pulling out of a driveway onto a street. You must:

Traffic already on the roadway, and anyone on the sidewalk you cross, has the right of way over a vehicle entering from a driveway or alley.

Q2. In South Dakota, the minimum age to apply for a learner permit is:

Applicants under 18 also need parental consent, and the permit must be held for a set period before the road test.

Q3. In South Dakota, the law requires a car seat or booster seat until:

State law is a minimum. Safety experts recommend a booster until the adult belt fits properly: lap belt low across the hips and shoulder belt across the chest, not the neck.

Q4. Adjusting your side mirrors slightly outward compared with the usual setting:

Wider mirror angles shrink the blind spot, but a head check before changing lanes is still the move that catches what mirrors miss.

Q5. Green highway signs give you:

Green is the guide color: exits, route numbers, distances and destinations. It never carries a warning or a restriction.

Q6. Driving far below the speed limit when conditions are good:

Traffic that moves at very different speeds causes crashes. If you must go slow, stay right and let others pass.

Q7. A blue sign along a highway usually marks:

Blue is the motorist-services color. Brown, by contrast, marks parks and recreation areas.

Q8. The only thing that reduces blood alcohol concentration is:

The liver processes alcohol at a fixed rate. Nothing speeds it up, so the only plan that works is not driving.

Q9. In South Dakota, when may you pass a stopped school bus with its red lights flashing without stopping first?

A center turn lane does not count toward the four. On anything narrower, traffic in both directions stops, and overtaking a bus from behind always requires a stop.

Q10. In South Dakota, under the point system, a driver's first license suspension lasts:

South Dakota's point system sets escalating suspension periods, starting with 60 days for a first suspension.
