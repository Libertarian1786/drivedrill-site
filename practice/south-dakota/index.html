Q1. At a railroad crossing, a small blue sign shows a phone number and a crossing ID number. When should you use it?

The blue Emergency Notification System (ENS) sign gives the railroad's emergency number and the crossing's ID. Call it if a vehicle is stalled on the tracks or the signals or gates are not working, so trains can be warned; call 911 too if anyone is in danger.

Q2. The road is most slippery:

Oil and rubber build up on dry pavement and float to the surface when rain starts. Slow down and double your following distance.

Q3. Your car is stuck in snow. What is the best way to try to free it?

Rock the car slowly forward and back without spinning the tires. Spinning the wheels only digs the car in deeper.

Q4. On a highway, about how much longer does it take to pass a large truck than to pass a car?

A truck is much longer than a car, so passing it takes about three to five seconds more. Make sure you have that extra room before you start to pass.

Q5. A yellow warning sign reads SOFT SHOULDER. What does it warn you about?

SOFT SHOULDER warns that the dirt or gravel beside the pavement is soft. Stay on the pavement; if your wheels drift onto a soft shoulder, the car can be pulled off the road and you can lose control.

Q6. A U-turn should never be made:

You need to be visible for the whole maneuver. Blind curves and crests hide you from traffic that arrives at speed.

Q7. What happens to a South Dakota Restricted Minor's Permit when the holder turns 18?

Upon reaching age 18, the Restricted Minor's Permit automatically converts to an Operator's License.

Q8. You start driving on a snow-covered road. How can you find out how much traction your tires have?

Start out slowly, then test your brakes gently once the car is moving to feel how much traction you have. A gentle test warns you before you need to stop.

Q9. After a DWI conviction in South Dakota, what proof must you file with the Department of Public Safety?

After a DWI conviction you must present an SR22 insurance filing to the Department of Public Safety and keep it for 3 years.

Q10. Driving while your license is suspended usually results in:

It is one of the few traffic offenses that regularly carries jail, and it extends the original suspension.

Q11. In South Dakota, you may not make a U-turn on a curve or near a hilltop where other drivers cannot see your vehicle from within:

South Dakota bans turning around on a curve or near a hilltop where drivers approaching from any direction cannot see your vehicle within 500 feet.

Q12. A highway has a posted minimum speed, but you cannot safely drive that fast. What should you do?

If you cannot or do not want to drive at or above the posted minimum speed, do not use that highway. Take another route.

Q13. A South Dakota police officer signals you to pull over. How should you show you have seen the officer?

Acknowledge the officer by turning on your right turn signal, then pull over to the right when it is safe.

Q14. After parallel parking in South Dakota, your vehicle should be within how far of the curb or edge of the road?

A parallel-parked vehicle should be within 12 inches of the curb or edge of the road, and at least 2 feet from other cars.

Q15. The South Dakota manual says that at higher speeds, it is better to signal a turn at least how long before you turn?

Signal at least 100 feet before an intersection; at higher speeds, signaling 4 to 5 seconds before the turn is better.

Q16. A bicyclist is riding toward an intersection and has not signaled. What should you assume?

At intersections, assume a bicyclist is going straight unless they signal otherwise. Do not turn across their path.

Q17. In South Dakota, one test fee gives you:

The South Dakota manual says the fee allows 3 attempts to pass a test within a 6-month period.

Q18. Compared with ice at a much colder temperature, ice at about the freezing point is:

Ice near the freezing point is more slippery than much colder ice, because a thin layer of water sits on top of it. Take extra care when the temperature hovers around freezing.

Q19. In the United States, which way does traffic travel around a roundabout?

Traffic in a roundabout moves counterclockwise, to the right of the center island. Never drive around it the wrong way, even to reach an exit on your left.

Q20. You should check your mirrors:

Knowing what is behind you is what turns a sudden stop into a controlled one.

Q21. At a two-lane roundabout with no signs showing otherwise, you plan to take the first exit to the right. Which lane should you use?

To turn right at a multi-lane roundabout, enter in the right lane and exit from the right lane. Signs and lane arrows before the roundabout show which lane goes where.

Q22. In South Dakota, can you be arrested for DWI while sitting impaired in a parked car you are not driving?

If you have physical control of a vehicle, you can be arrested even if you are not driving it.

Q23. In South Dakota, under the point system, a driver's first license suspension lasts:

South Dakota's point system sets escalating suspension periods, starting with 60 days for a first suspension.

Q24. You are waiting to turn left, and an oncoming car has its right turn signal on. When should you start your turn in front of it?

A driver may have left a signal on by mistake or may change their mind. Do not assume the car will turn; wait until it actually starts its turn before you turn left.

Q25. In South Dakota, a minor's Instruction Permit hold period can be shortened from 275 days to 180 days if the applicant:

South Dakota shortens the required permit-holding period to 180 days for minors who score 80% or better on both the written and driving portions of an approved driver education course.
