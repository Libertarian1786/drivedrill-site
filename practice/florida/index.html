Q1. A sign reading NARROW BRIDGE means:

Narrow bridge warnings mean less room than usual. Slow down, keep to your side, and watch for oncoming vehicles that may drift toward the center.

Q2. Compared with a car, a loaded truck needs:

Weight means distance. Never cut in front of a truck and then brake; the driver may have no room left to stop.

Q3. The minimum age for a Florida learner license is:

A learner license at 15 requires the traffic law and substance abuse course plus the knowledge exam and parental consent.

Q4. You must report a Florida crash to law enforcement immediately if:

Florida raised the property-damage threshold from $500 to $2,000 on October 1, 2026 (SB 488). If an officer does not investigate, the driver must file a self-report with the department within 10 days.

Q5. A flashing yellow arrow at a left-turn lane means:

A flashing yellow arrow is an unprotected turn. Oncoming traffic has the green, so you may only go when there is a safe gap.

Q6. Before driving on a frosty morning you should:

Driving with a peephole is a citation in most states, and snow left on the roof slides onto your windshield or the car behind you.

Q7. In Florida you may not park:

Florida also prohibits parking within 20 feet of a crosswalk at an intersection and within 30 feet of a flashing signal or stop sign.

Q8. Before taking the Florida driving skills test, a learner must hold the permit for:

The 50 hours of supervised driving, 10 of them after dark, are certified by a parent or guardian.

Q9. You come to a white square sign with a red circle reading DO NOT ENTER. You should:

DO NOT ENTER marks a road, ramp or lane you would be travelling the wrong way on. Find another route rather than entering at all.

Q10. Texting while driving is dangerous mainly because:

Five seconds looking at a phone at 55 mph covers the length of a football field blind. Every state restricts phone use for new drivers, and most restrict it for everyone.
