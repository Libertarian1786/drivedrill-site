Q1. In Kansas, a new resident who moves to the state must obtain a Kansas driver's license within:

Kansas requires anyone moving to the state to obtain a Kansas driver's license within 90 days of moving there.

Q2. A flashing yellow traffic light means:

Flashing yellow means proceed with care. You do not have to stop, but you must slow down and watch for cross traffic and pedestrians.

Q3. In Kansas, a driver under 21 is over the limit with:

The under-21 threshold is far below the 0.08% adult limit, so roughly one drink can reach it. The licence consequence is administrative and starts before any court date.

Q4. On a long downgrade you should:

Continuous braking overheats the brakes and they fade. Let the engine hold the vehicle back and brake in short firm applications.

Q5. In Kansas, the maximum speed limit on rural interstate highways is:

That is the highest limit the state allows on a rural interstate. The posted sign always governs, and a lower speed can still be required by conditions.

Q6. You are involved in a crash. You are required to:

Stopping, identifying yourself and rendering aid are legal duties everywhere. Leaving the scene turns a minor crash into a criminal charge.

Q7. Your hood flies open and blocks your view. You should:

Use the gap under the hood or the side window, signal, slow gradually and stop off the road.

Q8. Driving far below the speed limit when conditions are good:

Traffic that moves at very different speeds causes crashes. If you must go slow, stay right and let others pass.

Q9. The pedestrian signal shows a flashing orange hand with a countdown while you wait to turn. It means:

The countdown is for people already crossing. Wait until the crosswalk is clear before completing your turn.

Q10. In Kansas, you should not make a U-turn on a curve or near the top of a hill unless other drivers can see your vehicle from at least:

Kansas law prohibits U-turns on a curve, near the top of a hill, or anywhere you cannot be seen by other drivers for at least 500 feet.
