Q1. In Idaho, when may you pass a stopped school bus with its red lights flashing without stopping first?

On a road of four or more lanes, only traffic behind the bus must stop. On a narrower road, traffic in both directions stops -- a physical median does not by itself excuse you. Overtaking a bus from behind always requires a stop.

Q2. When a train is coming and you must stop at a railroad crossing, stop no closer to the nearest rail than:

Fifteen feet keeps you clear of the train's overhang and of the gate arm. Most states also say to stop no farther than 50 feet back, so you can still see the tracks.

Q3. You may not pass another vehicle within a certain distance of the crest of a hill because:

A blind crest hides an oncoming vehicle until it is far too late to abort the pass.

Q4. Switching on your hazard lights while parked illegally:

Hazard lights warn other drivers; they do not create a parking permit.

Q5. Driving without the insurance your state requires can result in:

Financial responsibility is a condition of driving. The penalties apply whether or not you have a crash.

Q6. The safest way to deal with a distracting situation inside the car is to:

Nothing inside the car is worth your attention at speed. Find a safe place to stop.

Q7. Blood alcohol concentration depends on:

Two people drinking the same amount can end up at very different levels, so nobody can judge their own BAC reliably.

Q8. In Idaho, you may not park within what distance of a fire hydrant?

Idaho's no-parking rules prohibit parking within 15 feet of a fire hydrant.

Q9. In Idaho, the maximum speed limit on rural interstate highways is:

That is the highest limit the state allows on a rural interstate. The posted sign always governs, and a lower speed can still be required by conditions.

Q10. School zone speed limits usually apply:

Read the sign: many zones are time-based, others use flashing beacons or a when-children-are-present condition.

Q11. A U-turn should never be made:

You need to be visible for the whole maneuver. Blind curves and crests hide you from traffic that arrives at speed.

Q12. Hazard lights should generally be used:

In most states hazard lights while driving normally are prohibited because they hide your turn signals.

Q13. Another driver is behaving aggressively toward you. You should:

Do not engage. Let them go, change your route if they follow, and drive to a police station rather than home.

Q14. A flagger in a work zone holds up a sign reading SLOW. You should:

Flaggers have the same authority as signs and signals in a work zone. Follow their direction and watch for workers on foot.

Q15. In Idaho, the minimum age to apply for a learner permit is:

Applicants under 18 also need parental consent, and the permit must be held for a set period before the road test.

Q16. In Idaho, the one-passenger-under-17 restriction during the first 6 months of a license does not apply if the passenger is:

Idaho's restriction limiting a newly licensed driver to one passenger under 17 during the first 6 months does not apply when the passenger is related to the driver by blood, adoption, or marriage.

Q17. In Idaho, if you fail the knowledge test, how long must you wait before you can retest?

The handbook states that if you fail a knowledge test, you must wait three days to retest, and you will also have to pay the test fee again.

Q18. In Idaho, a licensed driver under 16 generally may not drive between:

Documented exceptions usually cover work, school activities, emergencies and driving with a licensed adult. Carry the paperwork if you rely on one.

Q19. In Idaho, a vehicle traveling slower than the normal speed of traffic on a rural or two-lane highway and delaying three or more vehicles behind it must:

Idaho law requires a vehicle traveling slower than the normal speed of traffic that is delaying three or more other vehicles on a rural area or two-lane highway to turn off the road wherever it is safe to let them pass.

Q20. A green arrow pointing left means:

A green arrow is a protected turn. Still check the crosswalk for people before you move.

Q21. In Idaho, a moving traffic violation adds how many points to your driving record?

Idaho's point system assigns one to four points for each moving traffic violation on a driver's record.

Q22. Brown signs on a highway point to:

Brown is the color for recreational and cultural interest areas: parks, campgrounds, ski areas, museums and historic sites, for example. Blue, by contrast, marks motorist services.

Q23. Overdriving your headlights means:

High beams let you see about 350 feet ahead, and low beams much less. At highway speed you can outrun that, which is why night speeds should be lower.

Q24. In Idaho, an applicant under 18 must log how much supervised driving before the road test?

A parent or guardian certifies the hours. Night driving is where new drivers crash most, which is why a share of the total has to be after dark.

Q25. Adjusting your side mirrors slightly outward compared with the usual setting:

Wider mirror angles shrink the blind spot, but a head check before changing lanes is still the move that catches what mirrors miss.
