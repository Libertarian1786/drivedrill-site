Q1. An unrestrained pet in the car is a problem because:

Secure pets in a carrier or harness. Several states treat an animal in your lap as distracted driving.

Q2. In Idaho, a licensed driver under 16 generally may not drive between:

Documented exceptions usually cover work, school activities, emergencies and driving with a licensed adult. Carry the paperwork if you rely on one.

Q3. In Idaho, a vehicle traveling slower than the normal speed of traffic on a rural or two-lane highway and delaying three or more vehicles behind it must:

Idaho law requires a vehicle traveling slower than the normal speed of traffic that is delaying three or more other vehicles on a rural area or two-lane highway to turn off the road wherever it is safe to let them pass.

Q4. Driving far below the speed limit when conditions are good:

Traffic that moves at very different speeds causes crashes. If you must go slow, stay right and let others pass.

Q5. In Idaho, the maximum speed limit on rural interstate highways is:

That is the highest limit the state allows on a rural interstate. The posted sign always governs, and a lower speed can still be required by conditions.

Q6. Worn tire tread is dangerous mainly because it:

Tread channels water away. Once it is shallow, wet grip falls off sharply. Check with a tread gauge or the built-in wear bars.

Q7. The pedestrian signal shows a flashing orange hand with a countdown while you wait to turn. It means:

The countdown is for people already crossing. Wait until the crosswalk is clear before completing your turn.

Q8. You should check your mirrors:

Knowing what is behind you is what turns a sudden stop into a controlled one.

Q9. Liability insurance pays for:

Liability is the coverage states require. Collision and comprehensive, which cover your own vehicle, are optional.

Q10. In Idaho, if you fail the knowledge test, how long must you wait before you can retest?

The handbook states that if you fail a knowledge test, you must wait three days to retest, and you will also have to pay the test fee again.
