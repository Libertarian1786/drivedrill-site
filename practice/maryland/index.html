Q1. Unless a sign prohibits it, a right turn on red is allowed:

Right on red requires a full stop first, then yielding to everyone already entitled to go. A NO TURN ON RED sign removes the option entirely.

Q2. In Maryland, an applicant who is 25 years old or older must hold a Learner's Instructional Permit for at least:

Maryland applicants age 25 or older must hold their Learner's Instructional Permit for at least 45 days before they are eligible for a Provisional License, a shorter period than the 9 months required for applicants under 18.

Q3. Your vehicle registration must be:

Expired registration is an easy stop for an officer and, in many states, grounds for towing.

Q4. In Maryland, a learner permit must be held for at least how long before the road test?

The holding period exists so new drivers practice across seasons and conditions rather than cramming a license in a weekend.

Q5. In Maryland, a driver under 21 is over the limit with:

This is a true zero tolerance state: any measurable alcohol is enough for a suspension, and mouthwash or cough syrup can reach it.

Q6. A funeral procession with its lights on is passing through an intersection. You should:

Do not cut into or interrupt a procession, even if you have a green light, unless directed by an officer.

Q7. A wide white line across your lane at an intersection is:

Stop at the line, not past it. If your view is blocked once stopped, edge forward slowly only after stopping at the line.

Q8. In Maryland, when passing a bicyclist, you must leave a clearance of at least:

Maryland law requires drivers to leave at least 3 feet of space when passing a bicyclist.

Q9. A flagger in a work zone holds up a sign reading SLOW. You should:

Flaggers have the same authority as signs and signals in a work zone. Follow their direction and watch for workers on foot.

Q10. In Maryland, a bicycle helmet is legally required for riders and bike passengers under age:

Maryland law requires anyone under age 16 riding a bicycle, including passengers who are not steering, to wear a helmet.

Q11. In Maryland, the minimum age to apply for a learner permit is:

Applicants under 18 also need parental consent, and the permit must be held for a set period before the road test.

Q12. In Maryland, you may not pass another vehicle if your view is obstructed within:

Maryland prohibits passing when your view is obstructed as you come within 100 feet of a bridge, elevated road, or tunnel.

Q13. In Maryland, a newly licensed driver under 18 generally may not drive between:

Documented exceptions usually cover work, school activities, emergencies and driving with a licensed adult. Carry the paperwork if you rely on one.

Q14. An anti-lock braking system is designed to:

ABS preserves steering control. On gravel or deep snow it may even lengthen the stop, but you can still steer around the hazard.

Q15. Overdriving your headlights means:

High beams let you see about 350 feet ahead, and low beams much less. At highway speed you can outrun that, which is why night speeds should be lower.

Q16. A space cushion around your vehicle is useful because:

Space is time. The more of it you keep on all four sides, the more options you have when something goes wrong.

Q17. Before driving on a frosty morning you should:

Driving with a peephole is a citation in most states, and snow left on the roof slides onto your windshield or the car behind you.

Q18. An open container of alcohol in the passenger area of the vehicle is:

Open container laws apply to the whole passenger compartment. Keep sealed alcohol in the trunk or a locked area.

Q19. In Maryland, to qualify for a driver's license, your vision screening must show visual acuity of at least:

Maryland requires binocular vision and visual acuity of at least 20/40 in each eye to qualify for a driver's license.

Q20. After a school bus turns off its red lights, you should still:

Children are unpredictable right after they get off a bus. Move off slowly and keep scanning both sides.

Q21. Driving far below the speed limit when conditions are good:

Traffic that moves at very different speeds causes crashes. If you must go slow, stay right and let others pass.

Q22. After passing another vehicle, you should return to your lane when:

Seeing both headlights of the vehicle you passed in your mirror means you have enough room to move back over without cutting the driver off.

Q23. Driving on the shoulder to get around slow traffic is:

The shoulder is for emergencies and for stopped vehicles. Using it as a lane is both illegal and a common cause of crashes with people standing outside their cars.

Q24. In Maryland, the maximum speed limit on rural interstate highways is:

That is the highest limit the state allows on a rural interstate. The posted sign always governs, and a lower speed can still be required by conditions.

Q25. A flashing red traffic light means:

Treat a flashing red exactly like a stop sign: full stop, yield to traffic and pedestrians, then proceed when the way is clear.
