Q1. In Maryland, a learner permit must be held for at least how long before the road test?

The holding period exists so new drivers practice across seasons and conditions rather than cramming a license in a weekend.

Q2. Unless a sign prohibits it, a right turn on red is allowed:

Right on red requires a full stop first, then yielding to everyone already entitled to go. A NO TURN ON RED sign removes the option entirely.

Q3. In Maryland, you may not pass another vehicle if your view is obstructed within:

Maryland prohibits passing when your view is obstructed as you come within 100 feet of a bridge, elevated road, or tunnel.

Q4. When leaving a freeway you should:

Keep your speed until you are on the deceleration ramp, then slow. Braking in the travel lane surprises the traffic behind you.

Q5. In Maryland, the minimum age to apply for a learner permit is:

Applicants under 18 also need parental consent, and the permit must be held for a set period before the road test.

Q6. In Maryland, an applicant who is 25 years old or older must hold a Learner's Instructional Permit for at least:

Maryland applicants age 25 or older must hold their Learner's Instructional Permit for at least 45 days before they are eligible for a Provisional License, a shorter period than the 9 months required for applicants under 18.

Q7. Before opening your door into traffic after parking, you should:

Dooring a cyclist is a common and serious crash. Check the mirror and over your shoulder, and open with the far hand so your body turns toward traffic.

Q8. In heavy fog you should drive with:

High beams reflect off fog and blind you. Use low beams or fog lights, slow down, and pull well off the road if you cannot see.

Q9. Overdriving your headlights means:

High beams let you see about 350 feet ahead, and low beams much less. At highway speed you can outrun that, which is why night speeds should be lower.

Q10. You should never stop your vehicle:

Never start across tracks unless there is room for your whole vehicle on the far side. Trains overhang the rails by about three feet on each side.
