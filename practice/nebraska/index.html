Q1. A pedestrian hybrid beacon over a mid-block crosswalk shows two steady red lights. What must you do?

A pedestrian hybrid beacon is dark until someone pushes the crossing button. Steady red means stop and stay stopped while pedestrians cross; only when it changes to flashing red may you go once your path is clear.

Q2. In Nebraska, who must wear a seat belt when a permit holder is driving?

All occupants riding with a Nebraska permit holder, including the driver, must be buckled up. Breaking this rule carries a $25 fine.

Q3. Where a state uses a point system, it is used to:

Points accumulate with each conviction and are removed over time. Reaching the threshold brings a suspension. A few states, such as Minnesota, act on specific violations instead of points.

Q4. What is the most common kind of crash between a car and a motorcycle?

The most common car-motorcycle crash happens at an intersection when a driver turns left in front of a motorcycle coming the other way. Look carefully for motorcycles before you turn left.

Q5. A large truck is backing up into a loading dock across your path. What should you do?

Truck drivers have a large blind spot behind the trailer and may not see you at all. Never cross behind a truck that is backing up; wait until it is done.

Q6. In Nebraska, a driver whose license is revoked under the point system twice in five years loses the license for:

Two point-system revocations within five years mean the license is lost for three years.

Q7. In Nebraska, finishing an approved driver improvement course on your own can earn a credit of how many points?

Nebraska can grant a 2-point credit for completing a DMV-approved driver improvement course, and the credit is allowed only once every five years.

Q8. When does a Nebraska license issued to someone 21 or older expire?

Licenses issued to drivers 21 or older expire on the holder's birthday in the fifth year after issuance. Licenses issued under 21 expire at 21.

Q9. In Nebraska, you may not park within how many feet of a flashing signal, stop sign or yield sign at the side of the road?

Nebraska prohibits standing or parking within 30 feet of a flashing signal, stop sign, yield sign or other traffic control device at the side of the roadway.

Q10. You are inside a roundabout when an ambulance with its siren on comes up behind you. What should you do?

Do not stop inside a roundabout for an emergency vehicle. Continue to your exit or the next one, leave the roundabout, and then pull over to the right and stop.

Q11. What is a reversible lane?

Reversible lanes carry traffic one way at some times and the other way at other times, usually to handle rush-hour traffic. Check the signs and lane-use signals before you enter one.

Q12. A motorcyclist ahead of you slows down, but no brake light comes on. Why can that happen?

Motorcyclists often slow down by downshifting or rolling off the throttle, which does not turn on the brake light. Leave extra space and watch the rider, not just the light.

Q13. Your car has a problem and you need to stop. Which place should you avoid?

Never stop just over a hill or just around a curve. Drivers coming up behind you cannot see your car in time to stop or go around it.

Q14. Unless a different limit is posted, what is the speed limit in a Nebraska residential district?

Nebraska's speed limit in residential districts is 25 mph unless another limit is posted.

Q15. In Nebraska, a driver under 21 is over the limit with:

The under-21 threshold is far below the 0.08% adult limit, so even a small amount of alcohol can reach it.

Q16. In Nebraska, you may not pass another vehicle within how many feet of an intersection?

Nebraska prohibits passing within 100 feet of any intersection or railroad crossing.

Q17. Nebraska law also sets a floor on how slowly you may legally travel on the interstate. That lowest lawful speed is:

Nebraska sets a minimum speed of 40 mph on the interstate, since driving far below the flow of traffic can also be a hazard.

Q18. Compared with daylight, how does darkness change when you can see an object on the road?

At night you must be closer to an object than in daylight before you can see it. Slow down so you still have time to react when you do see it.

Q19. In the United States, which way does traffic travel around a roundabout?

Traffic in a roundabout moves counterclockwise, to the right of the center island. Never drive around it the wrong way, even to reach an exit on your left.

Q20. You are on an on-ramp joining a highway, and a yellow ADDED LANE sign is posted. What does it mean for you?

An ADDED LANE sign means two roads come together and a lane is added, so the entering lane keeps going and you are not required to merge. Still watch for traffic changing lanes.

Q21. An oncoming driver fails to dim their high beams. You should:

Watching the right edge line keeps you in your lane while your eyes recover. Retaliating with high beams blinds you both.

Q22. A driver holds their left arm out the window, bent at the elbow and pointing down. What does that mean?

When a driver's left arm points down from the window, the driver is slowing down or stopping.

Q23. On a highway, about how much longer does it take to pass a large truck than to pass a car?

A truck is much longer than a car, so passing it takes about three to five seconds more. Make sure you have that extra room before you start to pass.

Q24. In Nebraska, the minimum liability insurance coverage required is:

Nebraska requires minimum liability coverage of $25,000 for property damage and $50,000 for personal injury.

Q25. Your car has airbags. Do you still need to wear your seat belt?

Airbags are designed to work together with seat belts, not to replace them. Without a belt you can be thrown into the opening airbag or out of the car.
