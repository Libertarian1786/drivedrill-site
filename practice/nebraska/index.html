Q1. Good drivers look:

Handbooks give figures from 10 to 30 seconds ahead. Looking far ahead gives you time to spot brake lights, debris and merging traffic while you still have choices.

Q2. In Nebraska, an applicant under 18 must log how much supervised driving before the road test?

A parent or guardian certifies the hours. Night driving is where new drivers crash most, which is why a share of the total has to be after dark.

Q3. You are in the left lane of a highway and a line of traffic builds up behind you. You should:

The left lane is for passing. Once traffic stacks up behind you, moving right is both safer and, in many states, required.

Q4. A bicycle on a public road is:

Riders must obey signals and signs, and drivers must yield to them the same way they would to another vehicle.

Q5. In Nebraska, a driver under 21 is over the limit with:

The under-21 threshold is far below the 0.08% adult limit, so even a small amount of alcohol can reach it.

Q6. In Nebraska, unless a different limit is posted, the maximum speed limit in a business district is:

Nebraska's statutory default speed limit for a business district, unless otherwise posted, is 20 mph.

Q7. A red warning light appears on the dashboard while you are driving. You should:

Red generally means stop now: oil pressure, temperature, brakes or charging. Amber usually means service soon.

Q8. You may park in a space marked with the International Symbol of Access:

Accessible spaces need a valid placard or plate, and the person it belongs to must be involved in the trip. Fines are steep and vehicles are towed.

Q9. An open container of alcohol in the passenger area of the vehicle is:

Open container laws apply to the whole passenger compartment. Keep sealed alcohol in the trunk or a locked area.

Q10. Driving through a work zone at night, you should:

Lane patterns change day to day in a work zone, and workers may be close to traffic. Read the signs rather than relying on memory.

Q11. In Nebraska, accumulating how many points in a two-year period causes automatic revocation of your operator's license for six months?

Nebraska's Point System Law automatically revokes an operator's license for six months once a driver accumulates 12 points in a two-year period.

Q12. A three-point turn should be used only:

The maneuver blocks the whole road for several seconds, so it needs a low-traffic street and a clear view both ways.

Q13. You must never pass another vehicle when:

You need to see far enough ahead to complete the pass and return safely. Hills, curves, intersections and railroad crossings take that view away.

Q14. In Nebraska, using a hand-held cell phone while driving is:

There is no statewide hands-free law, so holding a phone to talk is not itself an offense. Texting is banned for all drivers, and new drivers face tighter limits.

Q15. In Nebraska, you are on a multi-lane controlled-access highway approaching a stopped highway maintenance vehicle with flashing lights, and traffic will not let you change lanes. The Move Over Law requires you to:

When a Nebraska driver cannot safely move into another lane away from a stopped vehicle covered by the Move Over Law, the law requires slowing down and maintaining a safe speed while passing.

Q16. In Nebraska, a conviction for driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs adds how many points to your driving record?

Nebraska's point system assesses 6 points against the driving record for driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs.

Q17. In Nebraska, the move-over law requires you to change lanes, or slow down, when passing:

Move to a lane that is not next to the stopped vehicle when you safely can. When you cannot, slow down. People working at the roadside are struck every year by drivers who did neither.

Q18. In Nebraska, the law requires a car seat or booster seat until:

State law is a minimum. Safety experts recommend a booster until the adult belt fits properly: lap belt low across the hips and shoulder belt across the chest, not the neck.

Q19. Your brakes fail while driving. You should:

Pump to build pressure, downshift for engine braking, apply the parking brake gradually, and look for an open escape path or an uphill slope.

Q20. You come up behind slow-moving farm equipment displaying an orange triangle. You should:

The orange triangle marks a vehicle traveling under 25 mph. Closing speed is higher than it looks, so slow early and pass only with a long clear view.

Q21. Driving while your license is suspended usually results in:

It is one of the few traffic offenses that regularly carries jail, and it extends the original suspension.

Q22. The pedestrian signal shows a flashing orange hand with a countdown while you wait to turn. It means:

The countdown is for people already crossing. Wait until the crosswalk is clear before completing your turn.

Q23. A green arrow pointing left means:

A green arrow is a protected turn. Still check the crosswalk for people before you move.

Q24. When towing a trailer you should:

Extra weight lengthens every distance. Many states also set lower speed limits for vehicles that are towing.

Q25. In Nebraska, a driver under age 21 who accumulates six or more points within one year must complete a driver improvement course within:

Nebraska requires drivers under 21 who reach six or more points in a year to complete a driver improvement course within three months.
