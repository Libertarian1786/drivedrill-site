Q1. In Nebraska, the law requires a car seat or booster seat until:

State law is a minimum. Safety experts recommend a booster until the adult belt fits properly: lap belt low across the hips and shoulder belt across the chest, not the neck.

Q2. An open container of alcohol in the passenger area of the vehicle is:

Open container laws apply to the whole passenger compartment. Keep sealed alcohol in the trunk or a locked area.

Q3. In Nebraska, an applicant under 18 must log how much supervised driving before the road test?

A parent or guardian certifies the hours. Night driving is where new drivers crash most, which is why a share of the total has to be after dark.

Q4. Excessive speeding, well above the limit, is treated more seriously because:

Kinetic energy rises with the square of speed, so a small increase in speed is a large increase in damage and stopping distance.

Q5. Before opening your door into traffic after parking, you should:

Dooring a cyclist is a common and serious crash. Check the mirror and over your shoulder, and open with the far hand so your body turns toward traffic.

Q6. Driving after using marijuana is:

Legal to possess does not mean legal to drive on. Impaired driving laws cover cannabis the same way they cover alcohol.

Q7. You should switch on your headlights:

Headlights are about being seen as much as seeing. Most states also require them in rain, fog, snow or whenever wipers are on.

Q8. You must never pass another vehicle when:

You need to see far enough ahead to complete the pass and return safely. Hills, curves, intersections and railroad crossings take that view away.

Q9. In Nebraska, a conviction for driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs adds how many points to your driving record?

Nebraska's point system assesses 6 points against the driving record for driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs.

Q10. In Nebraska, using a hand-held cell phone while driving is:

There is no statewide hands-free law, so holding a phone to talk is not itself an offense. Texting is banned for all drivers, and new drivers face tighter limits.
