Q1. In Indiana, the minimum age to apply for a learner permit is:

Applicants under 18 also need parental consent, and the permit must be held for a set period before the road test.

Q2. A conviction for driving under the influence usually results in:

Besides the suspension, fines and possible jail, a DUI stays on your driving record for years, and insurers usually raise premiums sharply.

Q3. You are involved in a crash. You are required to:

Stopping, identifying yourself and rendering aid are legal duties everywhere. Leaving the scene turns a minor crash into a criminal charge.

Q4. When leaving a freeway you should:

Keep your speed until you are on the deceleration ramp, then slow. Braking in the travel lane surprises the traffic behind you.

Q5. On a long downgrade you should:

Continuous braking overheats the brakes and they fade. Let the engine hold the vehicle back and brake in short firm applications.

Q6. Driving after using marijuana is:

Legal to possess does not mean legal to drive on. Impaired driving laws cover cannabis the same way they cover alcohol.

Q7. In Indiana, using a hand-held cell phone while driving is:

The state requires hands-free operation for every driver. Novice drivers are usually barred from using a phone at all, even hands-free.

Q8. Driving while your license is suspended usually results in:

It is one of the few traffic offenses that regularly carries jail, and it extends the original suspension.

Q9. In Indiana, during the first 180 days of a probationary license, which passengers may still ride along without a supervising adult in the front seat?

The general rule bars passengers during the first 180 days of a probationary license, but Indiana carves out an exception letting the driver carry a child, stepchild, sibling, step- or half-sibling, or spouse without another accompanying adult.

Q10. Outside of an interstate, a rural divided state highway in Indiana caps vehicles at:

Indiana caps speed on a rural state divided highway at 60 mph or the posted limit, lower than the 70 mph allowed on rural interstates.
