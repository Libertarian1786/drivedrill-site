Q1. You are in the left lane of a highway and a line of traffic builds up behind you. You should:

The left lane is for passing. Once traffic stacks up behind you, moving right is both safer and, in many states, required.

Q2. A downward-pointing triangle sign tells you to:

The downward triangle is the YIELD sign. Slow down, be ready to stop, and let cross traffic and pedestrians go first. You only have to stop if traffic requires it.

Q3. In Indiana, the move-over law requires you to change lanes, or slow down, when passing:

Move to a lane that is not next to the stopped vehicle when you safely can. When you cannot, slow down. People working at the roadside are struck every year by drivers who did neither.

Q4. The pedestrian signal shows a flashing orange hand with a countdown while you wait to turn. It means:

The countdown is for people already crossing. Wait until the crosswalk is clear before completing your turn.

Q5. You may not pass another vehicle within a certain distance of the crest of a hill because:

A blind crest hides an oncoming vehicle until it is far too late to abort the pass.

Q6. You are pulling out of a driveway onto a street. You must:

Traffic already on the roadway, and anyone on the sidewalk you cross, has the right of way over a vehicle entering from a driveway or alley.

Q7. When leaving a freeway you should:

Keep your speed until you are on the deceleration ramp, then slow. Braking in the travel lane surprises the traffic behind you.

Q8. In Indiana, a driver's license issued to someone younger than 75 years old is valid for:

Indiana driver's licenses are valid for six years for holders younger than 75; the renewal period shortens for older drivers.

Q9. In Indiana, once issued, a learner's permit remains valid for:

Indiana learner's permits are valid for two years from the date they are issued.

Q10. In Indiana, the maximum speed limit on rural interstate highways is:

That is the highest limit the state allows on a rural interstate. The posted sign always governs, and a lower speed can still be required by conditions.
