Q2. Parking is prohibited:

Q3. In Rhode Island, a supervising driver for a Limited Learner Permit holder must be:

Rhode Island requires a supervising driver to have held a license for at least five years and to be at least 21 years old.

Q4. In Rhode Island, you may not park within how many feet of a crosswalk at an intersection?

Rhode Island's parking rules set a 20-foot no-parking zone next to a crosswalk at an intersection.

Q5. A curb painted with diagonal yellow lines next to a wide striped area means:

Striped buffer areas separate traffic from hazards, medians or opposing lanes. Driving or parking in them is prohibited.

Q6. After you move, you must usually:

States set a deadline, often 10 to 30 days. Renewal notices and suspension notices go to the address on file.

Q7. A car in the lane next to you stops at a crosswalk. You should:

Passing a vehicle stopped at a crosswalk is how pedestrians get hit by the second car. Stop and look before moving.

Q8. In Rhode Island, a learner permit must be held for at least how long before the road test?

The holding period exists so new drivers practise across seasons and conditions rather than cramming a licence in a weekend.

Q9. In Rhode Island, once issued, a driver's license is generally valid for up to:

Rhode Island driver's licenses are generally valid for a maximum of five years before renewal is required.

Q10. A sign reading NARROW BRIDGE means:

Narrow bridge warnings mean less room than usual. Slow down, keep to your side, and watch for oncoming vehicles that may drift toward the center.
