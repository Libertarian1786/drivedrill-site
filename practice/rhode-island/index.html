25 sample questions from DriveDrill's 238-question Rhode Island bank. Pick an answer to see if you got it right, and why.

Q1. After passing another vehicle, you should return to your lane when:

Seeing both headlights of the vehicle you passed in your mirror means you have enough room to move back over without cutting the driver off.

Q2. You are involved in a crash. You are required to:

Stopping, identifying yourself and rendering aid are legal duties everywhere. Leaving the scene turns a minor crash into a criminal charge.

Q3. A flashing yellow traffic light means:

Flashing yellow means proceed with care. You do not have to stop, but you must slow down and watch for cross traffic and pedestrians.

Q4. The road is most slippery:

Oil and rubber build up on dry pavement and float to the surface when rain starts. Slow down and double your following distance.

Q5. In Rhode Island, you may not park within how many feet of a crosswalk at an intersection?

Rhode Island's parking rules set a 20-foot no-parking zone next to a crosswalk at an intersection.

Q6. In Rhode Island, you may not park within how many feet of a fire hydrant?

Rhode Island's parking rules set an 8-foot no-parking zone around a fire hydrant.

Q7. In Rhode Island, using a hand-held cell phone while driving is:

The state requires hands-free operation for every driver. Novice drivers are usually barred from using a phone at all, even hands-free.

Q8. In Rhode Island, a driver under the age of 18 who wants to use a cell phone while driving:

Rhode Island law bars drivers under 18 from using a personal wireless communication device at all while driving, including hands-free or speakerphone use, except in an emergency.

Q9. Good drivers look:

Handbooks give figures from 10 to 30 seconds ahead. Looking far ahead gives you time to spot brake lights, debris and merging traffic while you still have choices.

Q10. You smell smoke and see flames under the hood. You should:

Do not open the hood; air feeds the fire. Get people at least 100 feet away and let the fire department handle it.

Q11. A flashing yellow arrow at a left-turn lane means:

A flashing yellow arrow is an unprotected turn. Oncoming traffic has the green, so you may only go when there is a safe gap.

Q12. Driving on the shoulder to get around slow traffic is:

The shoulder is for emergencies and for stopped vehicles. Using it as a lane is both illegal and a common cause of crashes with people standing outside their cars.

Q13. In Rhode Island, a learner permit must be held for at least how long before the road test?

The holding period exists so new drivers practice across seasons and conditions rather than cramming a license in a weekend.

Q14. Driving through a work zone at night, you should:

Lane patterns change day to day in a work zone, and workers may be close to traffic. Read the signs rather than relying on memory.

Q15. A bicycle on a public road is:

Riders must obey signals and signs, and drivers must yield to them the same way they would to another vehicle.

Q16. You must never pass another vehicle when:

You need to see far enough ahead to complete the pass and return safely. Hills, curves, intersections and railroad crossings take that view away.

Q17. In Rhode Island, a newly licensed driver under 18 generally may not drive between:

Documented exceptions usually cover work, school activities, emergencies and driving with a licensed adult. Carry the paperwork if you rely on one.

Q18. In Rhode Island, the maximum posted speed limit on urban interstate highways is:

Urban interstates are held to a lower maximum than open rural highway, because of interchange spacing and traffic volume.

Q19. A funeral procession with its lights on is passing through an intersection. You should:

Do not cut into or interrupt a procession, even if you have a green light, unless directed by an officer.

Q20. Driving after using marijuana is:

Legal to possess does not mean legal to drive on. Impaired driving laws cover cannabis the same way they cover alcohol.

Q21. A green arrow pointing left means:

A green arrow is a protected turn. Still check the crosswalk for people before you move.

Q22. In Rhode Island, the move-over law requires you to change lanes, or slow down, when passing:

Move to a lane that is not next to the stopped vehicle when you safely can. When you cannot, slow down. People working at the roadside are struck every year by drivers who did neither.

Q23. In Rhode Island, the maximum speed limit on rural interstate highways is:

That is the highest limit the state allows on a rural interstate. The posted sign always governs, and a lower speed can still be required by conditions.

Q24. In Rhode Island, it is illegal to drive on a tire with tread worn to less than:

Rhode Island's vehicle equipment rules set 2/32 of an inch as the minimum legal tire tread depth.

Q25. The best response to feeling drowsy while driving is to:

Drowsy driving impairs you much like alcohol, and micro-sleeps happen without warning. Only sleep fixes it.
