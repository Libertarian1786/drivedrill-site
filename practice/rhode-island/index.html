Free Rhode Island permit practice test: 25 questions

25 sample questions from DriveDrill's 454-question Rhode Island bank. Pick an answer to see if you got it right, and why.

Q1. The first ability alcohol affects in a driver is:

Judgment goes first, which is why an impaired driver often believes they are fine. Reaction time, vision and coordination follow.

Q2. You hit a parked car and cannot find its owner. What must you do?

If you damage an unattended vehicle, you must find the owner or leave a written note where it will be seen, with your name and how to reach you. Some states also require a police report.

Q3. Where should a child in a rear-facing car seat never ride?

An inflating front airbag can strike a rear-facing car seat and seriously injure or kill the child. A rear-facing seat belongs in the back seat.

Q4. How soon after heavy rain can a flash flood develop?

A flash flood can build within minutes or a few hours of heavy rain, often with little warning. Stay alert for water on roads, bridges and low areas during and after a downpour.

Q5. In Rhode Island, an applicant under 18 must log how much supervised driving before the road test?

A parent or guardian signs a notarized affidavit of the hours. Night driving is where new drivers crash most, which is why a share of the total has to be after dark.

Q6. In Rhode Island, a child who still needs a child restraint by law must ride:

Rhode Island law says a child who must use a child restraint is carried in a rear seating position, properly restrained. The exception is a car with no rear seat, or one whose rear seats are all used by other children.

Q7. In Rhode Island, the maximum speed limit on rural interstate highways is:

That is the highest limit posted on the state's rural interstates. The posted sign always governs, and a lower speed can still be required by conditions.

Q8. During a traffic stop in Rhode Island, a police officer asks you to get out of your car. You must:

The Rhode Island manual says that if a law enforcement officer asks you to exit your vehicle, you must do so.

Q9. What is the most common kind of crash between a car and a motorcycle?

The most common car-motorcycle crash happens at an intersection when a driver turns left in front of a motorcycle coming the other way. Look carefully for motorcycles before you turn left.

Q10. Before driving on a frosty morning you should:

Driving with a peephole is unsafe and often against the law, and snow left on the roof slides onto your windshield or the car behind you.

Q11. A lane-use signal hanging over a highway lane shows a steady red X. What does it mean?

Lane-use signals hang over lanes whose use can change, such as reversible lanes. A steady red X means the lane is closed to you: do not drive in it.

Q12. A snowplow is working on the road ahead of you. What is the best choice?

Stay well back from a working snowplow, because it throws sand and snow and may stop or back up. Never pass it on the shoulder side.

Q13. In Rhode Island, leaving the scene of a crash that injures or kills someone, without stopping, is:

The Rhode Island manual lists failing to stop at a collision with personal injury or death as a felony.

Q14. Which hand signal tells other drivers that you are going to turn right?

To signal a right turn by hand, put your left arm out the window and bend it up at the elbow.

Q15. A motorcyclist ahead of you slows down, but no brake light comes on. Why can that happen?

Motorcyclists often slow down by downshifting or rolling off the throttle, which does not turn on the brake light. Leave extra space and watch the rider, not just the light.

Q16. A crash with the vehicle ahead is unavoidable. The safest choice is usually to:

Reduce speed as much as possible and choose a glancing impact with something soft over a head-on with something solid.

Q17. When you park on a two-lane road in Rhode Island, your right wheels may be no more than how far from the curb or right side of the road?

Rhode Island requires a car parked on a two-lane road to have its right wheels a maximum of 12 inches from the curb or the right side of the road.

Q18. In Rhode Island, a crash with no injuries must still be reported when property damage is more than:

Rhode Island requires a crash report when anyone is hurt or killed, or when property damage is over $1,000.

Q19. You are stopped, waiting for a gap in oncoming traffic so you can turn left. How should you keep your front wheels?

Keep your wheels straight until it is safe to start your turn. If you are hit from behind while your wheels are turned left, you could be pushed into oncoming traffic.

Q20. Which driver mistake is a common cause of crashes with bicyclists?

A common driver-caused bicycle crash happens when a driver turns left in front of a bicyclist coming the other way. Look for bicycles before you turn and give them the right-of-way.

Q21. What is a reversible lane?

Reversible lanes carry traffic one way at some times and the other way at other times, usually to handle rush-hour traffic. Check the signs and lane-use signals before you enter one.

Q22. The Rhode Island manual says that when you pass a bicyclist you should allow as much clearance as possible, about:

Rhode Island tells drivers to give as much room as possible, 3 to 5 feet, when passing a bicyclist.

Q23. When a Rhode Island learner permit holder drives, who must be buckled in?

A Rhode Island permit condition is that every person in the vehicle is properly fastened by a seat belt or child restraint system.

Q24. In Rhode Island, you may not park within how many feet of a fire hydrant?

Rhode Island's parking rules set an 8-foot no-parking zone around a fire hydrant.

Q25. You should never stop your vehicle:

Never start across tracks unless there is room for your whole vehicle on the far side. Trains overhang the rails by about three feet on each side.
