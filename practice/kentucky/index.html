Q1. Your car is stuck in snow. What is the best way to try to free it?

Rock the car slowly forward and back without spinning the tires. Spinning the wheels only digs the car in deeper.

Q2. You have just driven through deep water, and your brakes feel weak. How can you dry them?

After driving through water, test your brakes. If they are wet, drive slowly in a low gear and press the brake pedal lightly until they work normally again.

Q3. An exit number on a green sign tells you:

Exit numbers usually match the nearest mile marker, which makes it easy to judge how far away your exit still is.

Q4. At night, pedestrians are hardest to see:

Most pedestrian deaths happen after dark. Lower your speed so your stopping distance fits inside your headlight range.

Q5. You are in the left lane of a highway and a line of traffic builds up behind you. You should:

The left lane is for passing. Once traffic stacks up behind you, moving right is safer, and the law often requires it.

Q6. A left turn on red from a one-way street onto another one-way street is:

Where the law allows this turn, you must first stop completely and yield. Some states and cities ban it, so learn your state's rule and obey any posted sign.

Q7. When towing a trailer you should:

Extra weight lengthens every distance. Speed limits are sometimes lower for vehicles that are towing.

Q8. In Kentucky, failing to yield to a blind pedestrian with a white cane or assistance dog can bring a fine of up to:

Kentucky law requires yielding to a blind pedestrian with a white cane or assistance dog, and failing to do so can bring a fine of up to $250.

Q9. You are stranded in a snowstorm and run the engine for heat. What should you check to avoid carbon monoxide poisoning?

Keep the tailpipe clear of snow whenever the engine is running, because a blocked pipe can send carbon monoxide into the car. Run the engine only for short periods, with a window open slightly.

Q10. In Kentucky, driving without the required insurance can bring:

Kentucky's penalty for driving without required insurance is a fine of $500 to $1,000, up to 90 days in jail, or both, and the registration can be revoked.

Q11. How much regular beer counts as one standard drink?

One standard drink is a 12-ounce beer, which has about as much alcohol as a shot of liquor or a glass of wine. A larger can or glass of beer counts as more than one drink.

Q12. In Kentucky, you must dim your high beams when you come within what distance of an oncoming vehicle?

Kentucky says to dim your lights whenever you come within 500 feet of oncoming vehicles.

Q13. In general, may an all-terrain vehicle (ATV) be driven on a Kentucky public highway?

Kentucky law says a person shall not operate an ATV on any public highway or its right-of-way, though there are some exceptions.

Q14. School zone speed limits usually apply:

Read the sign: many zones are time-based, others use flashing beacons or a when-children-are-present condition.

Q15. For a first DUI conviction within 10 years in Kentucky, the fine is:

Kentucky fines a first DUI within 10 years at $200 to $500 plus court costs. Jail of 48 hours to 30 days can also be ordered.

Q16. In Kentucky, where may bicyclists never ride?

Kentucky's manual tells bicyclists never to ride on interstates and parkways.

Q17. You should never stop your vehicle:

Never start across tracks unless there is room for your whole vehicle on the far side. Trains overhang the rails by about three feet on each side.

Q18. At a railroad crossing, the gate goes up, but the red lights are still flashing. What should you do?

Do not cross the tracks until the red lights stop flashing, even if the gate rises. Another train may be coming.

Q19. What is an escape or runaway ramp beside a long downgrade for?

Escape or runaway ramps are for vehicles that are out of control or cannot stop because their brakes have failed. Never stop or park on or near one.

Q20. A lane-use signal hanging over a highway lane shows a steady red X. What does it mean?

Lane-use signals hang over lanes whose use can change, such as reversible lanes. A steady red X means the lane is closed to you: do not drive in it.

Q21. In Kentucky, a learner permit must be held for at least how long before the road test?

The holding period exists so new drivers practice across seasons and conditions rather than cramming a license in a weekend.

Q22. In Kentucky, a driver under 21 is over the limit with:

The under-21 threshold is far below the 0.08% adult limit, so even a small amount of alcohol can reach it.

Q23. If you fail the road skills test in Kentucky, how long must you wait before trying again?

Kentucky requires at least one week from the date of a failed road test before you try again.

Q24. In Kentucky, if you plan to turn at an intersection, signal at least how far before the turn?

Kentucky's manual says to signal 100 feet before turning at an intersection.

Q25. A signal at the end of a freeway on-ramp lets cars onto the freeway one at a time, switching between red and green. What must you do?

A ramp meter controls how quickly cars join the freeway. Stop at the line while it is red and go when it turns green.
