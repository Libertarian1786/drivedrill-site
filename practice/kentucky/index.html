Q1. A crash with the vehicle ahead is unavoidable. The safest choice is usually to:

Reduce speed as much as possible and choose a glancing impact with something soft over a head-on with something solid.

Q2. Driving through a work zone at night, you should:

Lane patterns change day to day in a work zone, and workers may be close to traffic. Read the signs rather than relying on memory.

Q3. You are in the left lane of a highway and a line of traffic builds up behind you. You should:

The left lane is for passing. Once traffic stacks up behind you, moving right is both safer and, in many states, required.

Q4. In Kentucky, the minimum age to apply for a learner permit is:

Applicants under 18 also need parental consent, and the permit must be held for a set period before the road test.

Q5. Your vehicle registration must be:

Expired registration is an easy stop for an officer and, in many states, grounds for towing.

Q6. In Kentucky, it is unlawful to follow an emergency vehicle responding to a call any closer than:

Kentucky's handbook states that it is unlawful to follow any emergency vehicle closer than 500 feet.

Q7. Implied consent means that by driving in the state you have agreed to:

Refusing the test carries its own suspension, usually longer than the one for failing it, and the refusal can be used against you.

Q8. Unless a sign prohibits it, a right turn on red is allowed:

Right on red requires a full stop first, then yielding to everyone already entitled to go. A NO TURN ON RED sign removes the option entirely.

Q9. In Kentucky, an applicant under 18 must log how much supervised driving before the road test?

A parent or guardian certifies the hours. Night driving is where new drivers crash most, which is why a share of the total has to be after dark.

Q10. In Kentucky, unless posted otherwise, the speed limit in an off-street parking facility open to the public is:

Kentucky sets the default speed limit in an off-street parking facility offered for public use at fifteen miles per hour.
