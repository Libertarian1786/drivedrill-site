Q1. A green arrow pointing left means:

A green arrow is a protected turn. Still check the crosswalk for people before you move.

Q2. In Washington, the minimum age to apply for a learner permit is:

Applicants under 18 also need parental consent, and the permit must be held for a set period before the road test.

Q3. In Washington, the maximum posted speed limit on urban interstate highways is:

Urban interstates are held to a lower maximum than open rural highway, because of interchange spacing and traffic volume.

Q4. In heavy fog you should drive with:

High beams reflect off fog and blind you. Use low beams or fog lights, slow down, and pull well off the road if you cannot see.

Q5. You come up behind slow-moving farm equipment displaying an orange triangle. You should:

The orange triangle marks a vehicle traveling under 25 mph. Closing speed is higher than it looks, so slow early and pass only with a long clear view.

Q6. A space cushion around your vehicle is useful because:

Space is time. The more of it you keep on all four sides, the more options you have when something goes wrong.

Q7. A prescription medicine that makes you drowsy:

Impaired driving laws cover any drug, prescribed or not. Read the label and do not drive when it warns you about machinery.

Q8. In Washington, once you are in a shared center left-turn lane, you should not travel farther than:

Washington's guide says drivers shouldn't travel farther than 300 feet in a shared center turn lane, since the lane is only for entering or leaving traffic, not for passing.

Q9. Combining alcohol with another drug usually:

Interactions are unpredictable and often far stronger than the sum of the parts.

Q10. On a long downgrade you should:

Continuous braking overheats the brakes and they fade. Let the engine hold the vehicle back and brake in short firm applications.

Q11. Implied consent means that by driving in the state you have agreed to:

Refusing the test carries its own suspension, usually longer than the one for failing it, and the refusal can be used against you.

Q12. Driving while your license is suspended usually results in:

It is one of the few traffic offenses that regularly carries jail, and it extends the original suspension.

Q13. Driving through a work zone at night, you should:

Lane patterns change day to day in a work zone, and workers may be close to traffic. Read the signs rather than relying on memory.

Q14. Switching on your hazard lights while parked illegally:

Hazard lights warn other drivers; they do not create a parking permit.

Q15. In Washington, if you refuse a BAC or THC test after being lawfully arrested for DUI, how long could you lose your driving privilege?

Washington law suspends the driving privilege of someone who refuses testing for 90 to 730 days, or until they turn 21, whichever period is longer.

Q16. The best response to feeling drowsy while driving is to:

Drowsy driving impairs you much like alcohol, and micro-sleeps happen without warning. Only sleep fixes it.

Q17. Children must ride in a child restraint or booster:

Requirements differ by state, and the back seat is safest for younger children. A belt that crosses the neck or belly means the child still needs a booster.

Q18. A U-turn should never be made:

You need to be visible for the whole maneuver. Blind curves and crests hide you from traffic that arrives at speed.

Q19. In Washington, the move-over law requires you to change lanes, or slow down, when passing:

Move to a lane that is not next to the stopped vehicle when you safely can. When you cannot, slow down. People working at the roadside are struck every year by drivers who did neither.

Q20. In Washington, if an adult in the front seat is not wearing a seat belt:

This is a primary enforcement state: no other violation is needed. The belt is also the single most effective thing in the car for surviving a crash.

Q21. In Washington, leaving a child under age 12 unattended in a parked vehicle is a crime:

Washington makes it a crime to leave a child under 12 unattended in a parked vehicle at all, and separately a crime to leave a child under 16 unattended with the motor running.

Q22. In Washington, you may not park within how many feet of a traffic signal, stop sign, or yield sign?

Washington prohibits parking within 30 feet of a traffic signal, stop sign, or yield sign so drivers and pedestrians can see clearly.

Q23. In Washington, an applicant under 18 must log how much supervised driving before the road test?

A parent or guardian certifies the hours. Night driving is where new drivers crash most, which is why a share of the total has to be after dark.

Q24. In Washington, during the restricted period, a young driver may carry:

Crash risk for a teen driver climbs sharply with each additional teen passenger, which is what this rule is built around.

Q25. A standard drink of beer, wine and distilled spirits:

A 12 oz beer, a 5 oz glass of wine and 1.5 oz of spirits each contain roughly the same alcohol, which is why counting drinks does not make you safe to drive.
