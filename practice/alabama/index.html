25 sample questions from DriveDrill's 485-question Alabama bank. Pick an answer to see if you got it right, and why.

Q1. A bicycle on a public road is:

Riders must obey signals and signs, and drivers must yield to them the same way they would to another vehicle.

Q2. In Alabama, window tinting on the front windshield is allowed:

Alabama restricts front windshield tint to a narrow strip at the top so the driver's forward view stays clear.

Q3. An exit number on a green sign tells you:

Exit numbers usually match the nearest mile marker, which makes it easy to judge how far away your exit still is.

Q4. In Alabama, before making a turn you must signal your intentions:

The Alabama Driver Manual requires signaling at least 100 feet before making a turn so other drivers and pedestrians know your intentions in time.

Q5. How soon after heavy rain can a flash flood develop?

A flash flood can build within minutes or a few hours of heavy rain, often with little warning. Stay alert for water on roads, bridges and low areas during and after a downpour.

Q6. Alabama's statutory speed limit for other locations, outside urban districts and off county paved and unpaved roads, when nothing else is posted, is:

Alabama's statutory speed limit in other locations is 55 mph unless another limit is posted.

Q7. Good drivers look:

Looking far ahead gives you time to spot brake lights, debris and merging traffic while you still have choices.

Q8. In Alabama, what can happen to the license of a 16-year-old who drops out of school?

The Alabama manual lists withdrawing from school at ages 15 through 18 as a reason your license can be suspended.

Q9. The road is most slippery:

Oil and rubber build up on dry pavement and float to the surface when rain starts. Slow down and double your following distance.

Q10. How does driving faster change what you can see?

The faster you drive, the narrower your field of vision becomes, so hazards at the sides of the road are harder to notice. Slow down where people or cars may come from the side.

Q11. In Alabama, using a hand-held cell phone while driving is:

The hand-held ban applies to everyone, but an officer generally needs another violation to make the stop. Texting is still banned outright.

Q12. In Alabama, a child who has outgrown the rear-facing seat must ride in a forward-facing car seat until:

Alabama requires a forward-facing child seat until the child is at least 5 years old or 40 pounds.

Q13. How should you help a crash victim who may be going into shock?

To help someone in shock, have them lie flat, raise their legs slightly if they are not hurt there, and keep them warm. Do not give them anything to drink.

Q14. You are at a flashing red light. What should you remember about the traffic on the cross street?

Traffic on the cross street often has a flashing yellow light and may not have to stop at all. Do not pull out until there is a safe gap.

Q15. When a train is coming and you must stop at a railroad crossing, stop no closer to the nearest rail than:

Fifteen feet keeps you clear of the train's overhang and of the gate arm. Stopping no farther than 50 feet back also lets you still see the tracks.

Q16. You drive on a paved county road in Alabama, and no speed limit is posted. How fast may you go?

Alabama's statutory speed limit on a paved county road is 45 mph unless another limit is posted.

Q17. A signal at the end of a freeway on-ramp lets cars onto the freeway one at a time, switching between red and green. What must you do?

A ramp meter controls how quickly cars join the freeway. Stop at the line while it is red and go when it turns green.

Q18. Your car has a problem and you need to stop. Which place should you avoid?

Never stop just over a hill or just around a curve. Drivers coming up behind you cannot see your car in time to stop or go around it.

Q19. In Alabama, a 16-year-old goes to take the road test. Who must come along?

The Alabama manual says a 16-year-old road test applicant must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.

Q20. In heavy fog you should drive with:

High beams reflect off fog and blind you. Use low beams or fog lights, slow down, and pull well off the road if you cannot see.

Q21. You turn left from a two-way street onto a street with two lanes going your way. Which lane should you turn into?

Finish a left turn in the left lane closest to the middle of the street, going your way. After the turn, you may signal and change lanes when it is safe.

Q22. In Alabama, unless you are on necessary official business, how closely may you follow an emergency vehicle that is on an emergency run?

Alabama law limits how closely ordinary vehicles may follow an emergency vehicle responding to a call, to keep the path clear for the responders.

Q23. In Alabama, a driver under 21 is over the limit with:

The under-21 threshold is far below the 0.08% adult limit, so even a small amount of alcohol can reach it.

Q24. A driver holds their left arm out the window, bent at the elbow and pointing down. What does that mean?

When a driver's left arm points down from the window, the driver is slowing down or stopping.

Q25. Which driver mistake is a common cause of crashes with bicyclists?

A common driver-caused bicycle crash happens when a driver turns left in front of a bicyclist coming the other way. Look for bicycles before you turn and give them the right-of-way.
