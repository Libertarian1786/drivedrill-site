Q1. In West Virginia, the move-over law requires you to change lanes, or slow down, when passing:

Move to a lane that is not next to the stopped vehicle when you safely can. When you cannot, slow down. People working at the roadside are struck every year by drivers who did neither.

Q2. The road is most slippery:

Oil and rubber build up on dry pavement and float to the surface when rain starts. Slow down and double your following distance.

Q3. In West Virginia, the minimum age to apply for a learner permit is:

Applicants under 18 also need parental consent, and the permit must be held for a set period before the road test.

Q4. In West Virginia, it is against the law to make a U-turn near a curve or the crest of a hill unless an approaching vehicle can see you from at least how far away?

West Virginia law bars turning around near a curve or hilltop where an approaching vehicle cannot see you from at least 500 feet in either direction.

Q5. When you park facing uphill next to a curb, you should turn your front wheels:

Uphill with a curb: wheels away from the curb, then let the car settle back so the tire rests against it. Downhill: wheels toward the curb.

Q6. When following a motorcycle you should:

Motorcycles are easy to lose behind your own windshield pillar, and even slight contact can spill a rider. Give a motorcycle a full lane and more space than you would a car.

Q7. The only thing that reduces blood alcohol concentration is:

The liver processes alcohol at a fixed rate. Nothing speeds it up, so the only plan that works is not driving.

Q8. In West Virginia, an applicant under 18 must log how much supervised driving before the road test?

A parent or guardian certifies the hours. Night driving is where new drivers crash most, which is why a share of the total has to be after dark.

Q9. An oncoming driver fails to dim their high beams. You should:

Watching the right edge line keeps you in your lane while your eyes recover. Retaliating with high beams blinds you both.

Q10. You arrive at an intersection where the traffic signal is completely dark because of a power failure. You should:

A dark signal is treated as an all-way stop. Stop, then take turns in the order vehicles arrived.
