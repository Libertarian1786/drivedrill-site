Q1. At a two-lane roundabout with no signs showing otherwise, you plan to take the first exit to the right. Which lane should you use?

To turn right at a multi-lane roundabout, enter in the right lane and exit from the right lane. Signs and lane arrows before the roundabout show which lane goes where.

Q2. A white bicycle symbol with two arrowheads above it is painted in a travel lane. What does this marking tell you?

This is a shared-lane marking, or sharrow. It shows that bicyclists may ride in the lane you are driving in and where they are likely to ride, so watch for them and give them room.

Q3. Which of these can be an early warning sign of carbon monoxide poisoning while you drive?

Sudden drowsiness, yawning, dizziness or nausea can be early signs of carbon monoxide poisoning. The gas has no smell, so these feelings may be your only warning.

Q4. Where should a child in a rear-facing car seat never ride?

An inflating front airbag can strike a rear-facing car seat and seriously injure or kill the child. A rear-facing seat belongs in the back seat.

Q5. When the lights at a railroad crossing start to flash, about how much warning do you usually have before the train arrives?

Crossing signals are set to give at least about 20 seconds of warning before the train reaches the crossing. When the lights start flashing, stop; there is not enough time to try to beat the train.

Q6. In West Virginia, according to the handbook, how should you let the driver ahead know you are passing in the daytime?

The West Virginia handbook says to move left when clear and sound your horn to alert the driver ahead that you are passing. At night you may blink your headlights instead.

Q7. In West Virginia, if there is ice or snow on the road, the manual says to stay at least how many seconds behind the vehicle ahead?

In icy or snowy conditions, West Virginia's manual says to stay at least six seconds behind the vehicle in front, more than the normal following distance.

Q8. On a long trip, how often should you stop to take a break?

On long trips, stop at least every two hours or every 100 miles to rest and stretch. Do not wait until you feel drowsy.

Q9. At a railroad crossing, the gate goes up, but the red lights are still flashing. What should you do?

Do not cross the tracks until the red lights stop flashing, even if the gate rises. Another train may be coming.

Q10. In West Virginia, how long must a Level 2 driver go without a conviction before graduating to a Level 3 license?

The West Virginia handbook says you must be conviction-free for 12 months before graduating to a Level 3 GDL.

Q11. The only thing that reduces blood alcohol concentration is:

The liver processes alcohol at a fixed rate. Nothing speeds it up, so the only plan that works is not driving.

Q12. In West Virginia, you may not park within how many feet of a mailbox that a carrier serves by vehicle for daily deliveries?

The West Virginia handbook says you may not park within 20 feet of any mailbox served by a carrier using a motor vehicle for daily deliveries.

Q13. Seat belts work best because they:

Belts keep you behind the wheel and away from the windshield, and they position you correctly for the airbag.

Q14. You are in the left lane of a highway and a line of traffic builds up behind you. You should:

The left lane is for passing. Once traffic stacks up behind you, moving right is safer, and the law often requires it.

Q15. At a railroad crossing, a small blue sign shows a phone number and a crossing ID number. When should you use it?

The blue Emergency Notification System (ENS) sign gives the railroad's emergency number and the crossing's ID. Call it if a vehicle is stalled on the tracks or the signals or gates are not working, so trains can be warned; call 911 too if anyone is in danger.

Q16. A police officer pulls you over at night. What should you do after you stop?

At night, turn on the inside light so the officer can see into your car. Stay inside with your hands in view unless you are told to get out.

Q17. After passing another vehicle, you should return to your lane when:

Seeing both headlights of the vehicle you passed in your mirror means you have enough room to move back over without cutting the driver off.

Q18. In West Virginia, what does the DMV do when you have 6 or more points on your record?

The West Virginia handbook says that when you have six points or more on your record, the DMV will send you a letter of caution.

Q19. In West Virginia, what is the jail sentence for driving while your license is revoked for DUI?

The West Virginia handbook says driving while revoked for DUI brings a mandatory minimum jail sentence of 30 days to six months, and the revocation is extended.

Q20. In West Virginia, completing an approved defensive driving class can remove how many points from your record, and how often may you use it?

West Virginia lets you have three points deducted from your record by completing an approved eight-hour defensive driving class, but you can only use this reduction once every 12 months.

Q21. Your vehicle has four-wheel drive. How should you drive on an icy road?

Four-wheel drive does not make ice safe, because the vehicle can still skid and slide, so drive slowly and carefully. It can give a false sense of security.

Q22. In West Virginia, a low-speed vehicle may be driven inside a municipality only on streets where the speed limit is no more than:

The West Virginia handbook says a low-speed vehicle may only be used on streets inside a municipality where the speed limit is not more than 25 mph.

Q23. In West Virginia, you may not park within how many feet of a fire hydrant?

The West Virginia handbook says you may not park within 15 feet of a fire hydrant.

Q24. In West Virginia, when is the ignition interlock program mandatory?

The West Virginia handbook says participation in the interlock program is mandatory if the driver has been revoked for two or more DUIs or related offenses within a ten-year period.

Q25. The recommended hand position on the steering wheel is:

Handbooks teach 9 and 3, 8 and 4, or lower still. Each gives good control and keeps your arms out of the airbag's path; 10 and 2 puts them where it deploys.
