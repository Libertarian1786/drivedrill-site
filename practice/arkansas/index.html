Q1. In Arkansas, after a crash or a serious traffic offense, can you be charged with DWI even if your BAC is under the usual adult limit?

The Arkansas guide says that after a crash or serious violation you can be charged with DWI with a BAC from 0.04% up to the adult limit.

Q2. In Arkansas, what is a mobile work zone?

A mobile work zone is short-term work that moves along the road, such as painting stripes, removing debris or patching potholes.

Q3. In Arkansas, a Class MD motorcycle license for ages 14 to 16 limits the rider to motorcycles of:

Arkansas's Class MD license is restricted to motorcycles of 250 cubic centimeters or less.

Q4. You have just driven through deep water, and your brakes feel weak. How can you dry them?

After driving through water, test your brakes. If they are wet, drive slowly in a low gear and press the brake pedal lightly until they work normally again.

Q5. In Arkansas, you have just started across railroad tracks when the gates begin to come down. What does the guide say to do?

The Arkansas guide says that if the gates come down as you begin to cross, keep going even if it means breaking the gate. Never stop on the tracks.

Q6. How should you wear the shoulder part of a lap and shoulder belt?

Wear the lap belt low across your hips and the shoulder belt across your chest. Never put the shoulder belt under your arm or behind your back; in a crash it cannot hold you there and can cause injuries.

Q7. How soon after heavy rain can a flash flood develop?

A flash flood can build within minutes or a few hours of heavy rain, often with little warning. Stay alert for water on roads, bridges and low areas during and after a downpour.

Q8. Black ice is most likely to form:

Bridges and shaded stretches lose heat fastest. If the road looks wet but spray has stopped coming off other cars, suspect ice.

Q9. Wide, short broken white lines separate your lane from the next lane near a freeway exit. What do they usually mean?

Large broken lane lines usually mark a lane that is ending or exiting. If you do not want to leave the freeway, move out of that lane early.

Q10. In Arkansas, a doubled fine for a moving traffic violation committed in a work zone can be as high as:

Work zone fines in Arkansas are doubled for moving violations such as speeding, reckless driving or distracted driving, and can reach as high as $5,000.

Q11. At some railroad crossings, white lines or a white X pattern are painted on the road on both sides of the tracks. What does that area mark?

These markings show the dynamic envelope, the space a train and its overhang take up as it passes. Never stop inside it; wait behind the stop line until there is room for your whole car past the tracks.

Q12. Why should you never run your car's engine inside a closed garage?

Exhaust contains carbon monoxide, a gas you cannot see or smell that can kill. Never start or run your engine in a garage with the door closed.

Q13. An Arkansas Learner's License (ages 14 to 16) stays restricted until when?

A Learner's License stays restricted until the driver turns 16 and the 6 months of restricted driving are complete.

Q14. In Arkansas, the minimum age to apply for a learner permit is:

Applicants under 18 also need a parent's or sponsor's signature.

Q15. Which hand signal tells other drivers that you are going to turn right?

To signal a right turn by hand, put your left arm out the window and bend it up at the elbow.

Q16. You bring a car with a small "donut" spare tire on it to your Arkansas skills test. What happens?

The Arkansas guide says no donut spares are allowed on a vehicle used for the skills test.

Q17. A white diamond is painted in a freeway lane. What does it mean?

A diamond symbol marks a high-occupancy vehicle (HOV), or carpool, lane. Signs tell you how many people must be in the vehicle and when the rule applies.

Q18. A motorcycle ahead of you has its turn signal blinking. What should you keep in mind?

Motorcycle turn signals usually do not cancel themselves, and riders sometimes forget to turn them off. Make sure the rider is really turning before you pull out.

Q19. In Arkansas, how can you skip the motorcycle skills exam?

The Arkansas guide says the motorcycle skills exam can be waived after successfully finishing a Motorcycle Safety Foundation Riders Course.

Q20. How much regular beer counts as one standard drink?

One standard drink is a 12-ounce beer, which has about as much alcohol as a shot of liquor or a glass of wine. A larger can or glass of beer counts as more than one drink.

Q21. You hit a parked car and cannot find its owner. What must you do?

If you damage an unattended vehicle, you must find the owner or leave a written note where it will be seen, with your name and how to reach you. Some states also require a police report.

Q22. Your car is stuck in snow. What is the best way to try to free it?

Rock the car slowly forward and back without spinning the tires. Spinning the wheels only digs the car in deeper.

Q23. What should you know about light rail trains running on city streets?

Light rail trains are very quiet, often quieter than buses and cars. Look both ways before you cross the tracks, and never turn in front of a train.

Q24. In Arkansas, trash is thrown from a moving car, and no one says who threw it. Who does the law presume threw it?

Arkansas law treats litter thrown from a vehicle as evidence that the driver threw it.

Q25. A highway has a posted minimum speed, but you cannot safely drive that fast. What should you do?

If you cannot or do not want to drive at or above the posted minimum speed, do not use that highway. Take another route.
