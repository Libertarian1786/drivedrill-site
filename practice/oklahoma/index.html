Q1. In Oklahoma, a learner permit must be held for at least how long before the road test?

The holding period exists so new drivers practise across seasons and conditions rather than cramming a licence in a weekend.

Q2. The road is most slippery:

Oil and rubber build up on dry pavement and float to the surface when rain starts. Slow down and double your following distance.

Q3. You must:

Driving without carrying the license is a separate violation in most states, even if you are licensed.

Q4. A left turn on red from a one-way street onto another one-way street is:

Most states permit this turn after a complete stop and yielding, but it is banned in some cities, so obey any posted sign.

Q5. In Oklahoma, points already on your driving record are reduced all the way to zero if you have:

Oklahoma's point system gradually forgives points for clean driving, and a full reset to zero happens after three consecutive years without a pointable conviction.

Q6. A funeral procession with its lights on is passing through an intersection. You should:

Do not cut into or interrupt a procession, even if you have a green light, unless directed by an officer.

Q7. You are involved in a crash. You are required to:

Stopping, identifying yourself and rendering aid are legal duties everywhere. Leaving the scene turns a minor crash into a criminal charge.

Q8. A strong crosswind is pushing your vehicle. You should:

Wind gusts are strongest in open country and beside large vehicles. Slow down, hold the wheel firmly, and expect a push when a truck passes.

Q9. According to the Oklahoma Driver Manual, most vehicles will become buoyant and float away in:

The manual warns that it takes surprisingly little water to float a car, which is why you should never try to drive across a flooded road.

Q10. In Oklahoma, the maximum posted speed limit on urban interstate highways is:

Urban interstates are held to a lower maximum than open rural highway, because of interchange spacing and traffic volume.
