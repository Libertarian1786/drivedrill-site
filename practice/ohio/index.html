Q1. Overdriving your headlights means:

High beams let you see about 350 feet ahead, and low beams much less. At highway speed you can outrun that, which is why night speeds should be lower.

Q2. You should check your mirrors:

Knowing what is behind you is what turns a sudden stop into a controlled one.

Q3. Ohio law prohibits parking within how far of a fire hydrant?

Ohio's distance is shorter than the 15 feet many states use, which is why it is worth learning for the Ohio test.

Q4. In the first 12 months with an Ohio probationary license, a driver under 18 may carry:

The passenger limit is one of the strongest protections for new drivers, because crash risk climbs sharply with teen passengers.

Q5. You come up behind slow-moving farm equipment displaying an orange triangle. You should:

The orange triangle marks a vehicle traveling under 25 mph. Closing speed is higher than it looks, so slow early and pass only with a long clear view.

Q6. During a level 3 snow emergency declared by a county sheriff in Ohio:

Level 1 means roads are hazardous, level 2 means only necessary travel, and level 3 closes the roads to everyone but emergency personnel.

Q7. Blood alcohol concentration depends on:

Two people drinking the same amount can end up at very different levels, so nobody can judge their own BAC reliably.

Q8. An Ohio driver under 18 must complete before the driving test:

The 50 hours come on top of the required driver education course, and a parent or guardian certifies them.

Q9. A wide white line across your lane at an intersection is:

Stop at the line, not past it. If your view is blocked once stopped, edge forward slowly only after stopping at the line.

Q10. Your brakes fail while driving. You should:

Pump to build pressure, downshift for engine braking, apply the parking brake gradually, and look for an open escape path or an uphill slope.
