Q1. After a crash with injury or significant damage you must usually:

Every state sets a damage threshold and a deadline for reporting. Exchange information at the scene and file the report on time.

Q2. Blood alcohol concentration depends on:

Two people drinking the same amount can end up at very different levels, so nobody can judge their own BAC reliably.

Q3. In Oregon, a learner permit must be held for at least how long before the road test?

The holding period exists so new drivers practice across seasons and conditions rather than cramming a license in a weekend.

Q4. The first ability alcohol affects in a driver is:

Judgment goes first, which is why an impaired driver often believes they are fine. Reaction time, vision and coordination follow.

Q5. You should never stop your vehicle:

Never start across tracks unless there is room for your whole vehicle on the far side. Trains overhang the rails by about three feet on each side.

Q6. A three-point turn should be used only:

The maneuver blocks the whole road for several seconds, so it needs a low-traffic street and a clear view both ways.

Q7. Oregon's cell phone law for drivers under 18 versus drivers 18 and older is:

Oregon sets a stricter rule for drivers under 18, banning cell phone and mobile device use outright, while older drivers may use the phone only with a hands-free accessory.

Q8. For drivers 21 and older, it is illegal to drive with a blood alcohol concentration at or above:

0.08% is the per-se limit for adult drivers in most of the country. You can still be arrested below it if your driving is impaired.

Q9. In Oregon, a newly licensed driver under 18 generally may not drive between:

Documented exceptions usually cover work, school activities, emergencies and driving with a licensed adult. Carry the paperwork if you rely on one.

Q10. Driving after using marijuana is:

Legal to possess does not mean legal to drive on. Impaired driving laws cover cannabis the same way they cover alcohol.

Q11. In Oregon, playing a vehicle radio or sound system so loud that it can be heard from how far away is illegal?

Oregon law sets a specific distance for excessively loud vehicle audio because it can drown out horns, sirens and screeching tires that warn you of danger.

Q12. In Oregon, a driver under 21 is over the limit with:

This is a true zero tolerance state: any measurable alcohol is enough for a suspension, and mouthwash or cough syrup can reach it.

Q13. Under Oregon's child passenger restraint law, a child must ride in an approved child safety seat until:

Oregon's child safety seat law uses an age-or-height test, and the child can only move to the adult seat belt alone once it fits them correctly on its own.

Q14. In Oregon, a teen's logged supervised driving hours only count if the supervising passenger is:

Oregon requires the person supervising a learner's practice driving to meet both an age and an experience threshold before those hours count toward the requirement.

Q15. In Oregon, after an emergency vehicle using its lights or siren passes you, you must:

Oregon requires drivers to keep well back from an emergency vehicle even after it has passed, giving responders room to work and travel safely.

Q16. Under Oregon's Open Container law, any open bottle or container of alcohol in the vehicle should be:

Oregon's Open Container law treats a bottle as open if its seal is broken even if it has been reclosed, so any open alcohol container belongs in the trunk, out of the passenger area.

Q17. A steady red arrow means:

A red arrow prohibits that movement entirely. Wait for a green or flashing yellow arrow, even if no traffic is coming.

Q18. A driver is following you too closely. The safest response is to:

Increase your own space cushion and give them a chance to go by. Brake-checking turns their mistake into your crash.

Q19. In many states, whenever your windshield wipers are on continuously you must also:

Wipers-on means lights-on in most states. It is about being seen by other drivers as much as your own vision.

Q20. In heavy fog you should drive with:

High beams reflect off fog and blind you. Use low beams or fog lights, slow down, and pull well off the road if you cannot see.

Q21. In Oregon, when parallel parking next to a curb, your vehicle should be parked:

Oregon sets a specific maximum distance from the curb for a proper parallel park, which is also checked on the drive test.

Q22. You must:

Driving without carrying the license is a separate violation in most states, even if you are licensed.

Q23. Speeding fines in a work zone are usually:

Most states increase work-zone fines and add penalties when workers are present, whether or not the crew is visible at that moment.

Q24. An unsecured load in the back of a pickup truck is:

You are responsible for anything that falls off your vehicle. Tie loads down and cover loose material.

Q25. You are in the left lane of a highway and a line of traffic builds up behind you. You should:

The left lane is for passing. Once traffic stacks up behind you, moving right is both safer and, in many states, required.
