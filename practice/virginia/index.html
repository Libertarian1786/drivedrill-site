Q1. In Virginia, you may not park within how many feet of a railroad crossing?

Virginia does not allow parking within 50 feet of a railroad crossing.

Q2. Standard drinks of beer, wine and distilled spirits:

A 12 oz beer, a 5 oz glass of wine and 1.5 oz of spirits each contain roughly the same alcohol, which is why counting drinks does not make you safe to drive.

Q3. In Virginia, low-speed vehicles may be driven on public roads with speed limits of:

In Virginia, low-speed vehicles may be operated on public roads with speed limits of 35 mph or less.

Q4. Which hand signal tells other drivers that you are going to turn right?

To signal a right turn by hand, put your left arm out the window and bend it up at the elbow.

Q5. Driving at night, you see red reflectors on the lane lines shining back at you in your headlights. What does this mean?

Road reflectors are made to shine red only toward drivers going the wrong way. If they shine red in your headlights, get out of the way and turn around as soon as it is safe.

Q6. Implied consent means that by driving in the state you have agreed to:

Refusing the test carries its own suspension, usually longer than the one for failing it, and the refusal can be used against you.

Q7. In Virginia, a law enforcement officer may charge you with reckless driving regardless of the posted speed limit if you are driving:

Virginia law allows an officer to charge reckless driving, regardless of the posted limit, when a driver goes 20 mph or more over the limit or exceeds 85 mph outright.

Q8. A large truck is backing up into a loading dock across your path. What should you do?

Truck drivers have a large blind spot behind the trailer and may not see you at all. Never cross behind a truck that is backing up; wait until it is done.

Q9. A three-point turn should be used only:

The maneuver blocks the whole road for several seconds, so it needs a low-traffic street and a clear view both ways.

Q10. A Virginia license restricted to daylight driving lets you drive during which hours?

A Virginia daylight-only license allows driving beginning a half-hour after sunrise and ending a half-hour before sunset.

Q11. In Virginia, you may not park within how many feet of an intersection?

Virginia does not allow parking within 20 feet of an intersection.

Q12. In Virginia, at highway speeds of 46 to 70 mph, the recommended following distance behind the vehicle ahead is:

Virginia's manual recommends 2 seconds under 35 mph, 3 seconds from 35-45 mph, and 4 seconds from 46-70 mph, noting the space cushion no longer works if you are speeding over 70.

Q13. At a railroad crossing, the gate goes up, but the red lights are still flashing. What should you do?

Do not cross the tracks until the red lights stop flashing, even if the gate rises. Another train may be coming.

Q14. On a long downgrade you should:

Continuous braking overheats the brakes and they fade. Let the engine hold the vehicle back and brake in short firm applications.

Q15. Two solid white lines side by side separate two lanes going the same way. What do they mean?

Double solid white lines are a barrier between lanes going the same way, such as beside a carpool lane. Do not cross them to change lanes; wait until they end or become broken.

Q16. In Virginia, collecting 18 demerit points within 12 months, or 24 within 24 months, leads to:

In Virginia, 18 demerit points within 12 months or 24 points within 24 months brings a 90-day suspension, and you must also complete a driver improvement clinic.

Q17. What should you know about wearing sunglasses or tinted lenses at night?

Avoid sunglasses and dark or tinted lenses at night, because they cut down the light you need to see clearly. This holds even if you think they help with glare.

Q18. On a road with no sidewalk, where should people walk?

Where there is no sidewalk, pedestrians should walk on the left side or shoulder, facing traffic, so they can see what is coming. Drivers should expect people walking there, especially at night.

Q19. How soon after heavy rain can a flash flood develop?

A flash flood can build within minutes or a few hours of heavy rain, often with little warning. Stay alert for water on roads, bridges and low areas during and after a downpour.

Q20. You should never park:

Hydrant clearance is set by state law, so learn your state's distance. Blocked hydrants delay firefighters and are towed.

Q21. In Virginia, you damage an unattended vehicle and leave a note for the owner. Within how long must you also report the crash to police?

In Virginia, after hitting an unattended vehicle and leaving a note, you must also report the crash to the police within 24 hours.

Q22. At a two-lane roundabout with no signs showing otherwise, you plan to take the first exit to the right. Which lane should you use?

To turn right at a multi-lane roundabout, enter in the right lane and exit from the right lane. Signs and lane arrows before the roundabout show which lane goes where.

Q23. In Virginia, a driver under 18 convicted of a demerit-point offense must finish a driver improvement clinic within how long?

In Virginia, a driver under 18 with a demerit-point conviction must complete a driver improvement clinic within 90 days, or DMV suspends the driving privilege.

Q24. A small sign under a railroad crossbuck reads 2 TRACKS. What does it tell you?

When more than one track crosses the road, a sign under the crossbuck shows the number of tracks.

Q25. In Virginia, an ambulance with lights and siren is coming toward you in the opposite lane of an undivided highway. What must you do?

In Virginia, on an undivided highway you must pull over to the edge of the road and stop until an emergency vehicle coming from the opposite direction passes.
