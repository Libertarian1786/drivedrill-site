Pass Guarantee

Pass your knowledge test or get your money back. The Pass Guarantee covers any paid DriveDrill purchase made on or after 4 October 2026, for everyone: any subscription charge, whatever the plan, or a one-time purchase (Full Access, Motorcycle Full Access or the CDL Pass); the free trial is free, so there is nothing to refund for it. To claim, email support@drivedrill.app with a photo of the failed result (the DMV's notice or score sheet; you may cover your licence number, date of birth and address on it), a screenshot of your mock exam history in DriveDrill (version 1.8 or later: Settings › Mock exam history; in CDL and Motorcycle it is Practice exam history) and the receipt email for each charge you claim (from Apple, or the payment email for an online purchase). One claim per person, not transferable; we answer within 5 business days. App Store purchases are refunded by Apple: when a claim meets the conditions we confirm it and ask you to request the refund at reportaproblem.apple.com (Apple usually answers within 48 hours), and when Apple asks us about it we tell Apple the claim qualifies under this guarantee; Apple makes the final decision on App Store refunds. Online purchases (paid by the parent link) are refunded by us to the card that paid; if a parent paid, either of you can claim. Not covered: road (driving) tests, tests outside the United States, purchases made anywhere other than the App Store or DriveDrill's own online payment link, and charges already refunded.
