Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Short version: DriveDrill does not collect your personal information. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics and no tracking. We could not identify you if we wanted to.

What the app stores, and where

Everything DriveDrill knows about you stays on your device:

  • The state and licence type you chose.
  • Which questions you have answered, whether you got them right, and which you bookmarked.
  • Your mock exam history.
  • How many free practice questions and mock exams you have used today and this week.
  • If you set them: your test date, and the time for practice reminders. Reminders are scheduled by your iPhone itself; nothing is sent to us or to any server.
  • Your learning-map progress: lessons passed, level and daily streak.

This lives in your iPhone's local storage. We cannot see it, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

iCloud sync (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Sync progress with iCloud in Settings, the same information is copied to your own private iCloud storage so it appears on your other devices. That data goes to Apple under your Apple Account and is governed by Apple's privacy policy. We have no access to it. You can turn the setting off at any time.

Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The App Store tells the app only whether a purchase is currently active. Apple provides us with anonymous, aggregated sales reports.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect names, email addresses or phone numbers.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites, and the app shows no App Tracking Transparency prompt because there is nothing to track.
  • We do not sell or share data, because we do not have any.

Children

DriveDrill is rated 4+ and is commonly used by teenagers preparing for a learner permit. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request. To remove everything the app stores, delete the app, or use Reset progress for this state in Settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
