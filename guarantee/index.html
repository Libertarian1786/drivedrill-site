The DriveDrill Pass Guarantee

Last updated: [PUBLISH DATE]

Pass your knowledge test or get your money back. If you study with DriveDrill and do not pass your state's knowledge test, we refund what you paid for DriveDrill in the 45 days before your test, if you meet the conditions below.

What it covers

Any paid DriveDrill purchase: a weekly or monthly subscription charge, or a one-time pass (Pass Pack, CDL Pass or Motorcycle Pass). Covers purchases made on or after [PUBLISH DATE]. The free trial is free, so there is nothing to refund for it.

What you get back

We refund the DriveDrill charges from the 45 days before your test. For a subscription, that is each weekly or monthly charge made in those 45 days; for a one-time pass, it is the pass.

The conditions

All three must be true:

  1. You took at least 3 full mock exams in DriveDrill, and the last one was within 30 days before your knowledge test. For a car or motorcycle test, the 3 exams must be for your state and type of test. For a CDL test, they must be practice exams in the CDL section, for the CDL test you took. (In CDL and Motorcycle, the app calls them practice exams.)
  2. You took your state's knowledge test within 45 days after each purchase or renewal charge you claim.
  3. You claim within 30 days of the failed result.

How to claim

Email support@drivedrill.app with:

  • a photo of the failed result (the DMV's notice or score sheet);
  • a screenshot of your mock exam history in DriveDrill (version 1.8 or later: Settings › Mock exam history; in CDL and Motorcycle it is Practice exam history);
  • your Apple receipt email for each charge you claim.

You may cover your licence number, date of birth and address on the result before you send it.

One claim per person, and it is not transferable. We answer within 5 business days.

How the refund works

App Store purchases are refunded by Apple, so there are two steps:

  1. We will ask you to request the refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple usually answers within 48 hours.
  2. If Apple declines, DriveDrill refunds you: we will pay you back directly by PayPal, so we will ask you for your PayPal email.

Not covered

  • Road (driving) tests.
  • Tests outside the United States.
  • Purchases made outside the App Store.
  • Charges Apple has already refunded.

Changes to these terms

DriveDrill may change these terms for future purchases. The terms in force on your purchase date are the ones that apply.
