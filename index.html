Questions

Good to know.

Does it cover my state?

Yes. DriveDrill covers all 50 states and DC, with full mock exams in your state's real format, whether your test is at the DMV, the DPS or another state office. Find your state.

Can I try it first?

Yes: 3-Day Free Trial · Cancel Anytime. Cancel in your iPhone Settings before the 3 days end and you pay nothing.

How does the Pass Guarantee work?

Pass your knowledge test or get your money back. Conditions apply: read the Pass Guarantee.

Can a parent or guardian pay?

Yes. Your teen taps “Have a parent or guardian pay” in the app and you get a link to pay online, or use Apple's Ask to Buy. You can also buy on the web, then open the link on the iPhone with DriveDrill to unlock it. How parents can pay.

Is it in Spanish?

Yes. You can study in English or Spanish. Ver en español.

Is there a motorcycle test? Is it only for teens?

The motorcycle permit test is in the same app, with its own lessons and practice exams. And it isn't only for teens: anyone getting a car or motorcycle permit can use it, at any age. Motorcycle prep.

More questions
How many practice questions are there?

6,250+ practice questions in all, 5,600+ of them for the car permit, plus the motorcycle test.

How long is the readiness check?

6 questions, about 3 minutes. It shows where you stand and builds your plan from your answers. It is practice, not your state's knowledge test.

Is DriveDrill run by the DMV?

No. DriveDrill is an independent study app and is not affiliated with any state agency.
