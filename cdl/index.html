The eight CDL knowledge tests

Each test has its own practice exam, scored at 80%: the federal passing score for every CDL knowledge test (49 CFR 383.135). The number of questions on the real test varies by state.

  • General KnowledgeThe test everyone takes.
  • Air BrakesIf your vehicle has air brakes.
  • Combination VehiclesFor combination vehicles, like a tractor-trailer.
  • Hazardous MaterialsThe H endorsement, to haul hazardous materials.
  • TankerThe N endorsement, to haul liquids or gases in tanks.
  • Doubles/TriplesThe T endorsement, to pull double or triple trailers.
  • PassengerThe P endorsement, to drive a bus.
  • School BusThe S endorsement, to drive a school bus.
