The eight CDL knowledge tests

  • General Knowledge: the test everyone takes.
  • Air Brakes: if your vehicle has air brakes.
  • Combination Vehicles: for combination vehicles, like a tractor-trailer.
  • Hazardous Materials: the H endorsement, to haul hazardous materials.
  • Tanker: the N endorsement, to haul liquids or gases in tanks.
  • Doubles/Triples: the T endorsement, to pull double or triple trailers.
  • Passenger: the P endorsement, to drive a bus.
  • School Bus: the S endorsement, to drive a school bus.

Each test has its own practice exam, scored at 80%: the federal passing score for every CDL knowledge test (49 CFR 383.135). The number of questions on the real test varies by state.
